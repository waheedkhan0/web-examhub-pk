## KPPSC Assistant Statistician (BPS-16) - Agriculture Department

The **Agriculture Department of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** relies on accurate biometric, econometric, and agricultural census data to formulate provincial food security policies, forecast wheat and maize yields, determine procurement quotas, and analyze field experiment data across agricultural stations.

The post of **Assistant Statistician (BPS-16)** (**Advt. No. 02/2026**) recruits professionals in Statistics, Mathematics, or Economics.

---

### KPPSC 45-MCQs Examination Syllabus

The screening paper consists of 45 objective MCQs:

#### 1. Probability & Distributions
- Properties of Normal Distribution: Bell-shaped symmetry, mean = median = mode, area properties ($\mu \pm \sigma \approx 68.27\%$, $\mu \pm 2\sigma \approx 95.45\%$, $\mu \pm 3\sigma \approx 99.73\%$), standard normal variate $Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$.
- Binomial and Poisson distributions: Mean and variance relationships (Binomial: $\text{mean} = np, \text{var} = npq$; Poisson: $\text{mean} = \text{variance} = \lambda$).
- Sampling distributions: Degrees of freedom for Student's $t$, Chi-Square, and Snedecor's $F$.

#### 2. Statistical Inference & Testing
- Null hypothesis ($H_0$) vs. Alternative hypothesis ($H_1$).
- Level of significance ($\alpha$), p-value interpretations, critical regions, one-tailed vs. two-tailed tests.
- Comparing two population means (independent samples $t$-test vs paired $t$-test).
- Contingency table analysis and Chi-Square test for independence: $\chi^2 = \sum \frac{(O - E)^2}{E}$ with $(r-1)(c-1)$ degrees of freedom.

#### 3. Agricultural Experimental Design
- Three fundamental principles: **Randomization** (eliminates bias), **Replication** (estimates experimental error), **Local Control** (reduces experimental error).
- Analysis of Variance (ANOVA): Partitioning total sum of squares into Treatments and Error.
- Choosing the right design: Completely Randomized Design (CRD) for homogeneous lab conditions vs. Randomized Complete Block Design (RCBD) for field plots with one-directional fertility gradients.

### High-Yield Exam Preparation Advice

- **Key Constants & Properties:** Memorize coefficients of variation, limits of correlation coefficient ($-1 \le r \le +1$), and moments about the mean.
- **Time Management:** Calculations in KPPSC must be solved mentally or with scratch paper rapidly without scientific calculators.
