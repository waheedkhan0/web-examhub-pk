## MDCAT: How the 200-MCQ Paper Is Won

MDCAT is the gateway to every MBBS and BDS seat in Pakistan, public or private. Because merit is built from a single standardised score, the difference between a government college and a private one is often a handful of marks — and those marks are usually lost to negative marking and to question framings that test a definition rather than understanding.

### Paper Structure and Mark Distribution

| Section | MCQs | Marks | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biology | 60 | 60 | 30% |
| Chemistry | 50 | 50 | 25% |
| Physics | 50 | 50 | 25% |
| English | 20 | 20 | 10% |
| Logical Reasoning | 20 | 20 | 10% |

Two sections decide the outcome for most candidates: Biology is the largest block and the most scoring, while Physics punishes slow arithmetic. English and Logical Reasoning are the cheapest marks available — vocabulary, grammar and quick pattern recognition can be banked in a few days of focused work.

### Negative Marking Changes How You Attempt the Paper

Each wrong answer costs 0.25 marks. Answering a question you cannot narrow down is therefore expected-value negative, while eliminating two of four options and guessing makes the guess worthwhile. Practise with a stopwatch and commit to a per-question budget: 200 questions in 180 minutes allows roughly 54 seconds each, and any question that needs a full derivation must be flagged and returned to.

### What the Syllabus Actually Tests

- **Biology:** cell structure and division, genetics and inheritance, human physiology (digestion, circulation, respiration, excretion, coordination), plant physiology, reproduction, ecology, evolution, and biological classification.
- **Chemistry:** atomic structure and periodicity, chemical bonding, mole concept and stoichiometry, solutions, equilibrium and thermodynamics, electrochemistry, organic nomenclature and reactions, and biomolecules.
- **Physics:** units and measurement, mechanics, waves and sound, optics, electrostatics and current electricity, magnetism and electromagnetic induction, thermodynamics, and modern physics with basic electronics.
- **English:** vocabulary in context, synonyms and antonyms, grammar, prepositions, sentence correction, analogies, and short comprehension passages.
- **Logical Reasoning:** number and letter series, coding-decoding, analogies, syllogisms, and data-sufficiency style questions.

### A Preparation Sequence That Works

1. **Syllabus mapping first.** Take the PMC syllabus line by line and mark what you cannot define from memory; that list is your actual syllabus.
2. **Topic-wise MCQs before full mocks.** Work one chapter at a time and read every explanation, including the ones for options you did not choose.
3. **Formula and fact sheets.** Physics numericals and Biology recall items (deficiency diseases, chromosome numbers, hormone actions, enzyme substrates) reward spaced repetition far more than re-reading notes.
4. **Full-length timed mocks in the last month.** Practise the real 180-minute load at the same time of day as your exam slot.
5. **Error log.** Record every wrong answer with the reason: concept gap, misread, or calculation slip. The reason matters more than the mark.

### Exam-Day Checklist

- Attempt every question you can eliminate down to two options; leave pure guesses blank.
- Do not spend the first hour perfecting the hardest Physics numericals — bank the 60 Biology marks early.
- Reserve the final ten minutes for the review pass and to fill the answer sheet completely.

*Always confirm the current pattern, fee schedule, and validity rule against the latest PMDC notification — the council has revised the scheme in recent years.*
