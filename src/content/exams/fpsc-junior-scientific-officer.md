## FPSC Junior Scientific Officer (BS-16) - AFIP

The **Armed Forces Institute of Pathology (AFIP)** in Rawalpindi, functioning under the Ministry of Defence, is Pakistan's premier tertiary-care diagnostic and medical research facility. The post of **Junior Scientific Officer (BS-16)** (**Case No. 117/2026**) recruits specialized scientific personnel across molecular diagnostics, clinical genomics, and biochemical investigation.

---

### Official Syllabus Specification

The FPSC examination for Case No. 117/2026 consists of a single 100-mark MCQ objective screening test:

#### Part-I: English (20 Marks)
- **Grammar Usage:** Tenses, prepositions, subject-verb agreement, and articles.
- **Sentence Structuring:** Identifying grammatical flaws, modifying phrases, parallel structures, and academic sentence completion.

#### Part-II: General Science / Specialized Bioscience Test (80 Marks)
Candidates are tested on graduate (BS 4-Year / M.Sc.) curriculum:
1. **Biochemistry:** Amino acids, protein structure hierarchies, enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten kinetics), glycolysis, Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation, lipid metabolism.
2. **Human Physiology:** Homeostasis, blood cell lineages, renal clearance mechanisms, respiratory gas exchange, neuromuscular transmission, and cardiac cycles.
3. **Immunology:** Humoral vs. cell-mediated immunity, MHC class I and II complexes, antibody structures (IgG, IgM, IgA, IgE, IgD), immunological techniques (ELISA, Western Blot, RIA, Immunohistochemistry).
4. **Mycology:** Fungal taxonomy, medically significant fungi (*Candida*, *Aspergillus*, *Cryptococcus*), mycotoxins, diagnostic fungal culture media (SDA), and antifungal modes of action.
5. **Biological Databases:** Primary vs. secondary databases, NCBI Entrez, GenBank accession formats, UniProtKB, Protein Data Bank (PDB), EMBL-EBI.
6. **Evolutionary Biology & Phylogenetics:** Sequence alignment (global vs. local, Needleman-Wunsch, Smith-Waterman), scoring matrices (PAM, BLOSUM62), neighbor-joining and maximum likelihood phylogenetic tree reconstruction.
7. **Genomics & Transcriptomics:** Sanger sequencing principles, High-Throughput / Next-Generation Sequencing (Illumina, Nanopore), RNA isolation, cDNA synthesis, real-time quantitative PCR (RT-qPCR).
8. **Structural Bioinformatics & Proteomics:** Secondary structure prediction (Chou-Fasman, GOR), tertiary structure homology modeling, Ramachandran plot analysis, mass spectrometry (MALDI-TOF, LC-MS).
9. **Molecular Docking & Drug Discovery:** Lead compound identification, structure-based vs. ligand-based drug design, binding energy calculations, hydrogen bonding and electrostatic interactions.
10. **DNA & RNA Structure:** Double helix dimensions (Watson-Crick model), major and minor grooves, A, B, and Z DNA conformations, mRNA, tRNA hairpin loops, rRNA assemblies.
11. **Chromosomes & Chromatin:** Nucleosome core octamer (H2A, H2B, H3, H4), linker histone H1, euchromatin vs. heterochromatin, telomeres, and centromeric repeats.
12. **Denaturation & Renaturation:** DNA melting temperature ($T_m$), factors influencing $T_m$ (GC content, salt concentration), hyperchromic shift, and $C_0t$ kinetic curves.

---

### High-Yield Preparation Topics

- **PCR and Primer Design:** Melting temperatures, primer dimer formation, annealing temperatures ($T_a = T_m - 5^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Enzyme Inhibitors:** Distinguishing competitive, non-competitive, and uncompetitive inhibition using Lineweaver-Burk plots.
- **Biochemical Assays:** Standard clinical laboratory diagnostic principles utilized in tertiary diagnostic centers like AFIP.
