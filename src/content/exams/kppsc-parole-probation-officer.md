## KPPSC Parole / Probation Officer (BPS-16)

The **Directorate of Reclamation and Probation**, functioning under the Home and Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, administers the non-custodial rehabilitation of eligible offenders.

**Probation and Parole Officers (BPS-16)** play an indispensable judicial and social welfare role: they prepare Pre-Sentence Investigation (PSI) reports for trial courts, supervise convicts released on probation under the **Probation of Offenders Ordinance, 1960**, monitor prisoners released on parole under the **Good Conduct Prisoners' Probational Release Act, 1926**, and reintegrate juvenile offenders into society under the **Juvenile Justice System Act, 2018**.

---

### KPPSC 45-MCQs Examination Blueprint

The screening test consists of 45 objective MCQs focused on:

#### 1. Core Social Work Methods (approx. 15 Questions)
- **Social Casework:** The six-step process (Intake, Study, Diagnosis, Treatment, Evaluation, Termination). Principles of acceptance, individualization, non-judgmental attitude, and client confidentiality.
- **Social Group Work:** Group dynamics, therapeutic interventions, building pro-social peer networks.
- **Social Welfare Administration:** Management of shelter homes, rehabilitation centers, and post-release tracking.

#### 2. Criminological & Sociological Theories (approx. 15 Questions)
- **Emile Durkheim's Anomie Theory:** Normlessness and its influence on deviant behavior.
- **Robert K. Merton's Strain Theory:** Adaptations to institutionalized means and cultural goals (Conformity, Innovation, Ritualism, Retreatism, Rebellion).
- **Edwin Sutherland's Differential Association Theory:** Criminal behavior learned through interactive social communication.
- **Restorative Justice:** Repairing harm caused by criminal behavior through victim-offender mediation (VOM) and community restitution.

#### 3. Statutory Probation & Juvenile Laws in Pakistan (approx. 15 Questions)
- **Probation of Offenders Ordinance, 1960:**
  - Offenses eligible for probation vs non-eligible offenses (capital crimes, life imprisonment, offenses against the state).
  - Power of courts to make probation orders without proceeding to conviction (Section 4) and on conviction (Section 5).
  - Conditions of probation bond and consequences of breach.
- **Juvenile Justice System Act (JJSA), 2018:**
  - Definition of child (under 18 years).
  - Diversion mechanisms (Section 9) with consent of the victim or juvenile.
  - Prohibition on handcuffing, solitary confinement, or awarding capital punishment to juvenile offenders.
