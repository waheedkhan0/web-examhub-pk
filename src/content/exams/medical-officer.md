## Medical Officer (BS-17) Recruitment Guide

**Medical Officers (MO)** and **Woman Medical Officers (WMO)** in Basic Pay Scale 17 form the frontline backbone of Pakistan's tertiary teaching hospitals, District Headquarter (DHQ) hospitals, Tehsil Headquarter (THQ) hospitals, and Basic Health Units (BHUs).

At the Federal level, the **Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)** recruits doctors for prestigious federal institutions such as the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), Federal Government Polyclinic Hospital Islamabad, and National Institute of Health (NIH). At the provincial level, **KPPSC** conducts major recruitment drives for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Health Department.

---

### Core Cadres Advertised

1. **Medical Officer / Woman Medical Officer (BS-17):**
   - Clinical ward rotations, outpatient departments (OPD), emergency room (ER) trauma care, primary care delivery.
2. **Casualty Medical Officer (CMO - BS-17):**
   - 24/7 emergency triage, trauma resuscitation, medico-legal examinations.
3. **Demonstrator / Lecturer in Medical Colleges (BS-17):**
   - Pre-clinical teaching in Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, and Community Medicine.

### Examination Pattern & Marks Breakdown

The written screening test comprises **100 Multiple Choice Questions** (100 minutes duration) based strictly on undergraduate MBBS curriculum:

#### Clinical Subjects (70-75 Marks)
- **General Medicine & Therapeutics (25 Marks):**
  - Cardiovascular disorders (hypertension, ischemic heart disease, heart failure).
  - Respiratory system (pneumonia, tuberculosis, asthma, COPD).
  - Gastroenterology & Hepatology (cirrhosis, viral hepatitis, peptic ulcer disease).
  - Endocrinology (diabetes mellitus, thyroid disease).
  - Neurology (stroke, meningitis, epilepsy).
  - Infectious diseases (malaria, typhoid, dengue, sepsis).
- **General Surgery & Allied (20 Marks):**
  - Acute abdomen (appendicitis, peritonitis, intestinal obstruction, cholecystitis).
  - Trauma care, Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS) protocol, burn management.
  - Wound healing, surgical infections, pre-operative and post-operative management.
  - Shock, fluid resuscitation, and blood transfusion.
- **Obstetrics & Gynecology (15 Marks):**
  - Antenatal care, high-risk pregnancy, pre-eclampsia, eclampsia.
  - Normal and abnormal labor management, post-partum hemorrhage (PPH).
  - Contraception and family planning.
  - Gynecological emergencies and common infections.
- **Pediatrics (10 Marks):**
  - Neonatal resuscitation and APGAR scoring.
  - Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) schedule of Pakistan.
  - Acute respiratory infections, diarrhea, dehydration protocols (WHO guidelines).
  - Malnutrition (Kwashiorkor, Marasmus) and developmental milestones.

#### Pre-Clinical & Para-Clinical Subjects (25-30 Marks)
- **Pharmacology:** Mechanism of action, indications, adverse drug reactions, drug interactions, contraindications of essential drugs.
- **Pathology & Microbiology:** Basic disease mechanisms, blood disorders, bacteriology, virology, immunology.
- **Public Health & Preventive Medicine:** Epidemic control, biostatistics, primary healthcare delivery, maternal & child health (MCH).
- **Forensic Medicine & Toxicology:** Medico-legal certificates, poisoning signs, autopsy principles.
