## FPSC Senior Elementary Teacher (SET) (BPS-16)

The Federal Public Service Commission conducts recruitment for **Senior Elementary Teachers (SET) (BPS-16)** under the Directorate of Federal Government Educational Institutions (Cantonments/Garrisons) and Federal Directorate of Education (FDE), Islamabad (**Advt. No. 02/2026**).

Seats are advertised under distinct case numbers for:
1. **SET (Male) (BPS-16)**
2. **SET (Female) (BPS-16)**
3. **SET (Drawing / Fine Arts - Female) (BPS-16)**
4. **SET (Computer Science) (BPS-16)**

---

### Tripartite Test Structure

FPSC tests for teaching cadres are structured into three distinct segments:

#### Part-I: English (20 Marks)
- Standard English grammar at the graduate level, focusing on correct prepositions, sentence formation, vocabulary, and paragraph comprehension logic.

#### Part-II: Academic Subject Matter (50 Marks)
- Comprehensive test of the candidate's core degree syllabus (Physics/Math, Chemistry/Biology, English, Urdu, or Computer Science) at the secondary and higher secondary school level.

#### Part-III: Professional Teacher Training (30 Marks)
This section is decisive for merit ranking:
1. **Teaching Techniques:** Differentiated instruction, project-based learning, reciprocal teaching, microteaching principles.
2. **Classroom Discipline & Management:** Assertive discipline model, token economies, managing large classes, time on task.
3. **Testing & Measurement:** Types of test items (objective vs subjective), item difficulty index, discrimination index, standard error of measurement, Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (Remembering to Creating).
4. **Child Psychology:** Cognitive developmental milestones, adolescent peer dynamics, guidance and counseling in schools.

---

### Key FPSC Exam Tips for SET Candidates

- **Do Not Skip Pedagogy:** Candidates scoring 40/50 in the subject test often miss the merit cutoff because they scored under 15 in Pedagogy.
- **Bloom's Taxonomy:** Almost every FPSC paper includes 2-3 questions asking candidates to classify an objective into the Cognitive, Affective, or Psychomotor domain.
- **Practice Online Mock Tests:** Take ExamHub's timed Pedagogy and Educational Psychology tests to simulate real exam pacing.
