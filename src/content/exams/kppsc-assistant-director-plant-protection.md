## KPPSC Assistant Director (Plant Protection) (BS-17)

The **Directorate General of Agriculture Extension and Adaptive Research, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** is tasked with safeguarding agricultural productivity against destructive insect pests, invasive swarms, fungal epidemics, and post-harvest diseases.

The appointment of **Assistant Director (Plant Protection) (BS-17)** (**Advt. No. 02/2026**) oversees plant protection field units, pesticide quality monitoring, spray campaigns, and farmer advisory services across KP's districts.

---

### KPPSC 45-MCQs Examination Blueprint

The KPPSC screening paper contains 45 objective MCQs focused exclusively on prescribed qualifications:

#### 1. Applied Agricultural Entomology (approx. 20-25 Questions)
- **Insect Anatomy & Physiology:** Types of insect antennae, legs, wing venation, and mouthparts (chewing, piercing-sucking, siphoning, sponging).
- **Economic Pests of KP Crops:** 
  - Sugarcane pyrilla, stem borer, and whitefly in Charsadda and Mardan basins.
  - Fruit fly (*Bactrocera dorsalis* and *Bactrocera zonata*) in deciduous orchard zones (Swat, Malakand, Peshawar).
  - Codling moth (*Cydia pomonella*) in apple production areas.
- **Locust Control:** Desert locust (*Schistocerca gregaria*) biology, solitary vs gregarious phase transformation, and survey and control operations.
- **Pesticide Toxicology:** LD50/LC50 determination, WHO pesticide hazard classifications, organophosphates, synthetic pyrethroids, neonicotinoids, and maximum residue limits (MRLs).

#### 2. Plant Pathology & Disease Diagnostics (approx. 15-20 Questions)
- **Rusts and Smuts:** Wheat yellow/stripe rust (*Puccinia striiformis*) epidemiology in cooler KP districts.
- **Blights & Wilts:** Potato late blight (*Phytophthora infestans*) and tomato bacterial wilt (*Ralstonia solanacearum*).
- **Fungicides & Bactericides:** Systemic vs contact fungicides, copper oxychloride, strobilurins, and triazole resistance.
- **Quarantine Measures:** Seed inspection procedures, international phytosanitary certificates, and preventing entry of destructive exotic pathogens.

---

### Recommended Study Approach

1. **Review High-Yield Entomology Handbooks:** Focus on orders of economic importance (*Lepidoptera, Hemiptera, Diptera, Coleoptera*).
2. **Memorize Disease Causative Agents:** Fungal taxonomic names, alternate hosts, and environmental triggers are frequently tested.
3. **Attempt ExamHub 45-Minute Timed Mockups:** Replicate the high-speed pressure of the KPPSC examination hall.
