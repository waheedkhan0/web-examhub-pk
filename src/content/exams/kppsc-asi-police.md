## KP Police Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) (BPS-11)

The **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police** is renowned for its frontline counter-terrorism, law enforcement, and crime investigation capabilities. The post of **Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) (BPS-11)** recruited through **KPPSC (Advt. No. 07/2026)** is the premier entry point into the executive police leadership.

ASIs lead police post checkpoints, register and investigate cases under the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), command patrolling squads, and maintain public order.

---

### Step-by-Step Selection Process

1. **Physical Endurance & Measurement Test:**
   - **Height:** Male 5'-7", Female 5'-1".
   - **Chest (Male):** 33" unexpanded to 34.5" expanded.
   - **Running Test:** 1 Mile (1.6 km) in 8 minutes for males; 10 minutes for females.
2. **Written Examination (250 Marks Total):**
   - **Urdu (75 Marks):** Written descriptive & grammar paper.
   - **English (75 Marks):** Written descriptive essay & grammar paper.
   - **General Knowledge (50 Marks):** Objective MCQ / short answers paper.
   - **Computer Literacy (50 Marks):** Objective & practical concepts paper.
3. **Psychological Evaluation & Interview (50 Marks):**
   - Personality assessment and Viva Voce conducted by KPPSC panel.

---

### Crucial Syllabus Details for the Written Test

#### Urdu Section (75 Marks)
- Essay writing on practical topics like "Role of Police in Modern Society", "Traffic Sense", "Combating Narcotics", or "National Unity".
- Mastering grammatical rules: correct singular-plural forms (*Wahid/Juma*), gender concordance (*Tazkeer-o-Tanees*), and sentence correction.

#### English Section (75 Marks)
- Direct instruction on writing clean, active English paragraphs without spelling errors.
- Preposition drills: prepositions followed by specific verbs (*accused of*, *congratulate on*, *abide by*).

#### General Knowledge (50 Marks)
- KP geography: mountain passes (*Khyber Pass, Gomal Pass, Tochi Pass, Shandur Pass*), major barrages, and tribal district identities.
- Current national cabinet, provincial governors, Chief Ministers, and foreign heads of state.

#### Computer Literacy (50 Marks)
- Standard MS Office shortcuts (`Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+V`, `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+B`, `Ctrl+P`, `F5` in PowerPoint, `F7` for Spellcheck).
- Basic computer terminology (binary, byte, gigabyte, terabyte, RAM vs ROM).
