## FPSC Electrical Engineer (BS-16) - AFIP

The **Armed Forces Institute of Pathology (AFIP)** houses high-precision clinical pathology, radiology, electron microscopy, and biomedical equipment. The **Electrical Engineer (BS-16)** (**Case No. 118/2026**) ensures uninterrupted operation, calibration, troubleshooting, and diagnostic testing of critical electro-medical systems.

---

### Official Syllabus Breakdown

The written test conducted by FPSC is a 100-mark single-paper screening examination:

#### Part-I: English (20 Marks)
- **Grammar Usage:** Correct preposition placement, verb tenses, subject-verb agreement, modifiers.
- **Sentence Structuring:** Detecting structural errors, parallel construction, idiomatic expressions.

#### Part-II: Professional Engineering Test (80 Marks)
1. **DC Machines:** Principles of DC generator and motor operation, back EMF, torque characteristics of series, shunt, and compound machines, and losses/efficiency.
2. **Power and Digital Electronics:** Switching characteristics of SCR, TRIAC, MOSFET, IGBT; pulse width modulation (PWM); single-phase and three-phase uncontrolled and controlled rectifiers.
3. **Electric Circuits:** Node voltage and mesh current methods; Superposition, Thevenin’s, Norton’s, and Maximum Power Transfer theorems; AC steady-state analysis, impedance, resonance, and power factor correction.
4. **Semiconductor Devices:** P-N junction physics, reverse recovery time, Zener diode regulation, BJT biasing configurations (CE, CB, CC), and FET characteristics.
5. **Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps):** Ideal vs practical op-amp parameters, inverting/non-inverting amplifiers, summing amplifiers, integrators, differentiators, instrumentation amplifiers, and common-mode rejection ratio (CMRR).
6. **Oscillators & Circuit Theory:** Barkhausen criterion for oscillations, RC phase shift oscillators, LC tuned oscillators (Colpitts, Hartley), and crystal-controlled stable oscillators.
7. **Logic Design & Switching Theory:** Boolean algebra simplification, Karnaugh Maps (K-Maps), multiplexers, decoders, encoders, flip-flops (SR, JK, D, T), counters, and shift registers.
8. **Repairs of Multilayer Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs):** Soldering/desoldering safety standards, ESD (Electrostatic Discharge) prevention protocols, trace repair, multilayer delamination identification, and surface-mount device (SMD) rework.
9. **Specialized Diagnostic Test Equipment:** Digital Storage Oscilloscopes (DSO), spectrum analyzers, signal generators, logic analyzers, biomedical safety analyzers, and leakage current testers.
10. **RF Devices:** High-frequency skin effect, impedance matching networks, RF filtering, electromagnetic interference (EMI) suppression, and shielding in clinical hospital equipment.

---

### Key FPSC Exam Tips for Engineering Candidates

- **Numerical Calculations:** Practice standard circuit theorems without calculators (e.g. calculating Thevenin resistance $R_{th}$ or resonant frequency $f_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$).
- **Electro-Medical Safety:** Pay close attention to electrical grounding, leakage current classifications (Type B, BF, CF applied parts), and hospital isolation transformers.
- **Digital Logic:** Be proficient with Karnaugh map minimization and flip-flop excitation tables.
