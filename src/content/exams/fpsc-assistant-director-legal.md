## FPSC Assistant Director (Legal / Enforcement) (BS-17) - Pak-EPA

The **Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency (Pak-EPA)**, an autonomous body under the Ministry of Climate Change & Environmental Coordination, enforces federal environmental standards, regulates pollution controls, monitors industrial effluent, and prosecutes violators before Environmental Tribunals and appellate courts.

The post of **Assistant Director (Legal / Enforcement) (BS-17)** (**Case No. 121/2026**) represents an executive legal and enforcement appointment requiring an LLB degree and 2 years of proven litigation or prosecution experience.

---

### Test Scheme & Marks Distribution

The screening examination totals 100 marks:

#### Part-I: English (20 Marks)
- **Grammar Usage & Sentence Structuring:** Correct syntax, sentence logic, prepositions, idiomatic expressions, and legal English composition.

#### Part-II: Professional Legal Test (80 Marks)
The professional syllabus focuses heavily on environmental jurisprudence and federal procedural law:
1. **Pakistan Environmental Protection Act (PEPA), 1997:**
   - Powers and functions of the Federal Agency (Pak-EPA).
   - Prohibition of certain discharges or emissions (Section 11).
   - Initial Environmental Examination (IEE) and Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) mandates (Section 12).
   - Environmental Protection Orders (EPO) (Section 16).
   - Penalties and offenses by corporate bodies (Sections 17-19).
2. **Environmental Tribunals & Magistrates:**
   - Establishment, jurisdiction, and procedures of Environmental Tribunals (Section 20-22).
   - Appeals to High Court (Section 23).
   - Powers of Environmental Magistrates to take cognizance and issue summary trials.
3. **National Environmental Quality Standards (NEQS):**
   - Permissible emission levels for ambient air, municipal/industrial effluent, motor vehicle exhausts, and noise levels.
4. **Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), 1898:**
   - Cognizable vs non-cognizable offenses, powers of arrest, search warrants, seizure memos, preparation of charge sheets/complaints, and recording confessions.
5. **Code of Civil Procedure (CPC), 1908:**
   - Territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction, temporary injunctions (Order 39), suits against the Government, and execution of decrees.
6. **Qanun-e-Shahadat Order (QSO), 1984:**
   - Evidentiary value of chemical lab reports, chain of custody of collected industrial samples, primary vs secondary evidence, burden of proof, and expert opinions (Article 59).
7. **Constitution of Pakistan, 1973:**
   - Article 9 (Right to Life incorporating Right to Clean Environment — *Shehla Zia case* doctrine).
   - Article 199 (Writ Jurisdiction of High Courts in environmental public interest litigation).

---

### Crucial Case Laws and Judicial Precedents

To distinguish yourself in both the screening test and the subsequent interview:
- **Shehla Zia v. WAPDA (PLD 1994 SC 693):** Landmark verdict establishing that the Right to Life under Article 9 includes the right to a healthy, clean, and unpolluted environment.
- **Asghar Leghari v. Federation of Pakistan (2015):** Groundbreaking climate change justice judgment establishing the Climate Change Commission.
