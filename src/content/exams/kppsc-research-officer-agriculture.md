## KPPSC Research Officer (BS-17) - Agriculture Department

The **Directorate General of Agricultural Research, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** conducts agricultural experimentation, seed certification, crop breeding, and soil nutrient profiling across agricultural research institutes (Tarnab, D.I. Khan, Mingora, Abbottabad).

Recruitment for **Research Officer (BS-17)** is conducted by the **KPPSC** under **Advertisement No. 02/2026** for multiple specialized posts:
1. **Research Officer (Soil Science) (BS-17)**
2. **Research Officer (Biotechnology) (BS-17)**
3. **Research Officer (Pulses & Legumes Crops) (BS-17)**

---

### KPPSC 45-MCQ Screening Format

Unlike FPSC's 100-mark format, KPPSC general departmental recruitments utilize a concise, fast-paced **45 MCQs in 45 minutes** screening test. All 45 questions are drawn from the candidate's prescribed academic discipline:

#### 1. Soil Science Specialty
- Soil physical properties (texture, structure, bulk density, porosity).
- Soil organic matter (SOM) and C:N ratio dynamics.
- Chemistry of macronutrients ($N, P, K$) and secondary nutrients ($Ca, Mg, S$).
- Saline, sodic, and saline-sodic soils classification (ECe, ESP, SAR) and gypsum requirement calculations.

#### 2. Biotechnology & Crop Breeding Specialty
- Plant micropropagation protocols (callus induction, organogenesis, somatic embryogenesis).
- Marker-Assisted Selection (MAS) and quantitative trait loci (QTL) mapping.
- CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing in crop plants for drought and heat tolerance.
- Heterosis, hybrid vigor, and male sterility mechanisms in legume and cereal crops.

#### 3. Agronomy & Pulses Specialization
- Symbiotic nitrogen fixation: root nodule organogenesis by *Rhizobium* species.
- Integrated weed management (IWM) in leguminous and oilseed crops.
- Dryland farming techniques for Southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa agro-ecological zones.
