## KPPSC Assistant Director (Legal) (BPS-17) - Transport Department

The **Directorate of Transport and Mass Transit, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** oversees vehicular regulation, vehicle emission testing, regional route permits, fare structures, and public transit systems (including the Peshawar Bus Rapid Transit - Zu Peshawar).

The **Assistant Director (Legal) (BPS-17)** defends the Directorate and Regional Transport Authorities (RTAs) before Civil Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court in route-permit disputes, public interest litigation, and service matters.

---

### KPPSC 45-MCQs Examination Blueprint

The screening test comprises 45 objective MCQs based on:

#### 1. Transport Regulations & Motor Vehicle Laws (approx. 20 Questions)
- **Provincial Motor Vehicles Ordinance, 1965:**
  - Licensing provisions: Disqualification for holding driving license, medical fitness standards.
  - Registration requirements: Temporary registration, transfer of ownership, suspension and cancellation of registration.
  - Regional Transport Authorities (RTAs): Constitution, powers to grant, refuse, cancel, or suspend stage carriage, contract carriage, and goods carriage permits.
  - Mandatory Third-Party Insurance requirements and claims tribunals.
- **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Urban Mass Transit Framework:** Regulatory functions, fare determination mechanisms, and operator concession agreements.

#### 2. Procedural & Administrative Law (approx. 25 Questions)
- **Administrative Law Doctrines:**
  - *Audi Alteram Partem* (No one shall be condemned unheard) as an indispensable requirement in cancellation of public route permits.
  - Doctrine of Legitimate Expectation and Proportionality in regulatory enforcement.
- **Constitutional Writs (Article 199):**
  - Writs of Mandamus, Prohibition, and Certiorari against public statutory regulators.
- **Civil Procedure (CPC):** Notice under Section 80 CPC before instituting suits against government departments, and bar on injunctions interfering with public infrastructure under Order 39.
