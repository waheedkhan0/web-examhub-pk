## Civil Engineer / Assistant Executive Engineer (BS-17) Guide

Civil engineering candidates in Pakistan compete for prestigious gazetted public sector posts including **Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE - BS-17)**, **Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO - BS-17)**, and **Assistant Director Engineering (BS-17)**.

Major employing federal departments include **Pakistan Public Works Department (Pak PWD)**, **Military Engineer Services (MES)**, **Ministry of Housing & Works**, and **Water & Power Development Authority (WAPDA)**. At the provincial level in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, **KPPSC** recruits engineers for the **Communication & Works Department (C&W)**, **Irrigation Department**, and **Local Government & Rural Development Department (LG&RDD)**.

---

### Key Eligibility Standards

- **Academic Qualifications:** B.Sc / B.E / B.S degree in Civil Engineering from a university accredited under PEC Level-II (Washington Accord).
- **PEC Registration:** Mandatory valid Green Card registration as Registered Engineer (RE) or Professional Engineer (PE) issued by the **Pakistan Engineering Council**.
- **Age Bracket:** 21 to 30 years (+ standard government age relaxation).

### Detailed Test Scheme & Syllabus (100 MCQs)

FPSC and KPPSC tests are designed to evaluate both theoretical grasp and field execution knowledge:

#### Part-I: English (20 Marks - FPSC Standard)
- Professional vocabulary, technical sentence correction, prepositions, and structural grammar.

#### Part-II: Professional Civil Engineering Domain (80 Marks)

1. **Structural Analysis & Mechanics of Materials (15 Marks):**
   - Stress-strain relationships, Mohr's circle, bending moment and shear force diagrams (BMD/SFD).
   - Deflection of beams (moment-area method, virtual work method).
   - Truss analysis, indeterminate structures, column buckling (Euler's formula).

2. **Concrete Technology & Reinforced Concrete Design (15 Marks):**
   - Cement types, hydration, water-cement ratio, workability tests (slump test, compaction factor).
   - ACI / British standard code provisions for beams, slabs, and columns.
   - Singly and doubly reinforced sections, shear reinforcement, development length.
   - Pre-stressed concrete principles, losses in pre-stress.

3. **Geotechnical Engineering & Soil Mechanics (15 Marks):**
   - Phase relationships, index properties, soil classification (USCS and AASHTO).
   - Permeability, Darcy's law, seepage analysis, flow nets.
   - Consolidation theory, Terzaghi's 1-D consolidation equation, settlement calculation.
   - Shear strength of soils (direct shear test, triaxial test, Mohr-Coulomb failure criteria).
   - Bearing capacity of shallow and deep foundations (Terzaghi, Meyerhof formulas).

4. **Fluid Mechanics & Water Resources / Irrigation (15 Marks):**
   - Hydrostatic pressure, buoyancy, continuity equation, Bernoulli's equation.
   - Flow through pipes, Darcy-Weisbach equation, Moody's chart, water hammer.
   - Open channel flow, Manning's and Chezy's equations, hydraulic jump.
   - Design of unlined and lined irrigation canals (Lacey's and Kennedy's silt theories).
   - Dams, spillways, barrages, cross-drainage works.

5. **Transportation & Highway Engineering (10 Marks):**
   - Geometric design of highways (stopping sight distance, passing sight distance, superelevation).
   - Pavement design: flexible pavements (CBR method) and rigid pavements (Westergaard method).
   - Traffic engineering: traffic volume studies, capacity, level of service (LOS).

6. **Surveying & Construction Management (10 Marks):**
   - Levelling, curvature and refraction corrections, contouring, total station, GPS/GIS basics.
   - Project scheduling: CPM, PERT, critical path calculation, float times.
   - Quantity takeoff, rate analysis, bill of quantities (BOQ), PEC standard bidding documents.
