## Charge Nurse (BS-16/17) Recruitment Guide

**Charge Nurses** (also designated as Staff Nurses or Nursing Officers) oversee patient care coordination, medication administration, ward management, critical care nursing, and infection control across government teaching and civil hospitals.

Both the **Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)** (recruiting for federal hospitals in Islamabad) and **KPPSC** (recruiting for provincial DHQs, THQs, and medical teaching institutions) conduct highly competitive written screening tests to select qualified nursing professionals.

---

### Key Eligibility Criteria

- **Academic Qualifications:**
  1. Generic B.Sc Nursing (4-Years degree from an HEC/PNC recognized nursing college) OR
  2. 3-Year Diploma in General Nursing + 1-Year Diploma in Midwifery (for Female candidates) or 1-Year specialized nursing diploma (for Male candidates).
- **Professional Registration:** Active, valid registration card issued by the **Pakistan Nursing and Midwifery Council (PNMC)**.
- **Age Limits:** Typically 21 to 30 years (with standard 5-year general age relaxation granted by the government).

---

### Examination Syllabus Breakdown (100 MCQs)

The written test covers the core curriculum accredited by the Pakistan Nursing Council:

#### 1. Fundamentals of Nursing & Foundations (25 Marks)
- Nursing process (Assessment, Diagnosis, Planning, Implementation, Evaluation).
- Vital signs monitoring (temperature, pulse, respiration, blood pressure, oxygen saturation).
- Aseptic techniques, hand hygiene, personal protective equipment (PPE), standard precautions.
- Patient safety, pressure injury prevention (Braden scale), fluid balance charts.
- Route-specific drug administration protocols (5 Rights of medication safety).

#### 2. Medical-Surgical Nursing (25 Marks)
- Care of patients with myocardial infarction, congestive heart failure, stroke.
- Respiratory nursing care: tracheostomy care, oxygen therapy, chest tube drainage.
- Post-operative wound care, surgical drain maintenance, pain management.
- Diabetes mellitus management, insulin administration techniques, hypoglycemia crisis.
- Infection control protocols, central line-associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI) prevention.

#### 3. Maternal, Newborn & Child Health (MNCH) (20 Marks)
- Stages of labor, physiological changes in pregnancy, antenatal risk assessment.
- Postpartum care, lochia assessment, care of newborns, APGAR scoring.
- Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC), exclusive breastfeeding advantages, immunization schedule.
- Pediatric assessment, dehydration management, oral rehydration therapy (ORT).

#### 4. Pharmacology & Dosage Calculation (15 Marks)
- Drug dosage calculation formulas (IV drip rates, drops per minute, pediatric dosage rules).
- Emergency medications (Adrenaline, Atropine, Amiodarone, Lasix, Insulin).
- Antibiotic stewardship, side effects, anaphylaxis identification and prompt intervention.

#### 5. Community Health Nursing & Professional Ethics (15 Marks)
- Primary healthcare components, epidemiological concepts, disease surveillance.
- Code of ethics for nurses, patient confidentiality, informed consent, documentation standards.
- Basic English grammar and general knowledge components (where applicable for FPSC).

### Preparation Guidance & Best Practices

1. **Practice Drug Calculations:** Practice converting units (mg to mcg, ml/hr to gtts/min) without calculator errors.
2. **Review High-Yield Flashcards:** Memorize normal laboratory values (serum potassium, sodium, arterial blood gas norms).
3. **Use ExamHub:** Attempt our medical, biological sciences, and health questions with detailed rationale.
