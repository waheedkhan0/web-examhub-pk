## FPSC FIA Superintendent (BS-16)

The **Federal Investigation Agency (FIA)** recruits its core administrative leadership—**Superintendent (BS-16)**—through the **Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)**. 

Superintendents serve as key administrative custodians across FIA Zonal Directorates, Headquarters circles, and Specialized Investigation Wings (Anti-Corruption, Cyber Crime, Border Management, and Counter-Terrorism).

---

### Official Syllabus & Marks Breakdown

The written screening test comprises 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) for 100 marks:

#### Part-I: English (20 Marks)
- **Grammar Usage:** Identifying faults in subject-verb agreement, pronoun references, conditional clauses, and prepositions.
- **Sentence Structuring:** Sentence logic, passive vs active construction, and official drafting vocabulary.

#### Part-II: Professional Test (80 Marks)
This section is heavily weighted towards administrative governance, federal regulations, and departmental statutes:

1. **Human Resource Management (HRM):**
   - Human resource planning, recruitment strategies, selection tests, orientation.
   - Job evaluation, key performance indicators (KPIs), performance appraisal methods (360-degree, MBO).
   - Employee discipline, motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg), and grievance handling.

2. **Civil Service Rules & Regulations:**
   - **Civil Servants Act, 1973:** Terms and conditions of service, seniority determination, probation, discharge, disciplinary procedures.
   - **Rules of Business, 1973:** Hierarchy of federal ministries, roles of Ministers, Federal Secretaries, Additional Secretaries, and Joint Secretaries; submission of summaries to the Prime Minister and Federal Cabinet.

3. **Financial Management & Budgeting:**
   - **Powers of Principal Accounting Officers (PAO) Regulations, 2021:** Duties of the Secretary as PAO, internal financial control, delegation of financial powers, sanctions, re-appropriation of funds.
   - **General Financial Rules (GFR):** Treasury rules, maintenance of service books, contingent expenditure, and audit objections.

4. **Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) Rules, 2004:**
   - Scope of PPRA rules, procurement planning, competitive bidding methods (Single Stage One Envelope, Single Stage Two Envelopes).
   - Bid security, bid evaluation criteria, disqualification of contractors, redressal of grievances, and procurement transparency.

5. **FIA Departmental Acts & Rules:**
   - **FIA Act, 1974 (Act VIII of 1975):** Composition of the agency, superintendence by Federal Government, powers of members of the agency (search, arrest, seizure), schedule offenses.
   - **FIA Rules, 1975:** Investigation circles, procedure of preliminary inquiries, registration of FIRs, case diaries.

6. **Basic Arithmetic & IT Literacy:**
   - Arithmetic averages, percentage calculations, profit and loss, simple interest, and basic data handling.
   - Practical knowledge of MS Word (official drafting), MS Excel (spreadsheets and budgets), and secure official email communication.

---

### High-Yield Preparation Strategy

- **Master the PPRA Rules & PAO Regulations:** The FPSC frequently tests specific rule numbers (e.g., Rule 36 of PPRA for bidding methods and time limits for grievance redressal under Rule 48).
- **Civil Servants Act, 1973:** Understand the grounds for removal, dismissal, compulsory retirement, and reductions in rank under the Civil Servants (Efficiency & Discipline) Rules.
- **Practice Mock Tests on ExamHub:** Utilize our interactive practice tests to cement your knowledge before exam day.
