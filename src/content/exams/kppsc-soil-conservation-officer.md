## KPPSC Soil Conservation Assistant/Officer (BS-17)

The **Directorate General of Soil and Water Conservation, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** undertakes crucial interventions across mountainous, piedmont, and rainfed (Barani) regions to prevent topsoil loss, stabilize active gullies, build water storage check dams, and conserve fertile agricultural land.

The appointment of **Soil Conservation Assistant / Officer (BS-17)** (**Advt. No. 02/2026**) seeks candidates trained in Soil Sciences or Agricultural Engineering.

---

### KPPSC 45-MCQs Test Outline

The screening paper consists of 45 objective Multiple Choice Questions:

#### 1. Soil Science Fundamentals (approx. 20-25 Questions)
- **Soil Physics:** Soil texture triangle, particle size distribution (Stokes' Law for hydrometer analysis), soil bulk density vs particle density, total porosity, water retention curves (field capacity, permanent wilting point).
- **Soil Chemistry:** Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC), base saturation percentage, pH effects on nutrient availability, reclamation of alkaline and acidic soils.
- **Organic Matter Dynamics:** Humus formation, role of soil microorganisms, soil aggregate stability against water dispersion.

#### 2. Soil Conservation & Agricultural Engineering (approx. 20-25 Questions)
- **Forms of Water Erosion:** Splash impact detachment, sheet wash, rill development, and gully progression (stages of gully erosion: initiation, development, healing).
- **USLE Equation:** Understanding the factors: Rainfall erosivity ($R$), soil erodibility ($K$), slope length and steepness ($LS$), cover management ($C$), and support practices ($P$).
- **Structures for Gully Control:** Design principles of loose stone check dams, wire-woven gabion spurs, masonry spillways, and chute spillways.
- **Terracing Techniques:** Level bench terraces, inward sloping terraces, broad-based diversion terraces, and contour trenching for arid reforestation in Southern KP (Karak, Kohat, Lakki Marwat).
- **Rainwater Harvesting:** Farm ponds, micro-catchment water harvesting, retaining runoff in Barani tract agriculture.

---

### Exam Strategy & High-Scoring Tips

- **Formula Inquiries:** USLE factors and the Rational runoff equation ($Q = 0.278 \cdot C \cdot I \cdot A$) are frequently queried in both conceptual and calculation form.
- **KP Specific Soil Challenges:** Familiarize yourself with saline-sodic soils in Southern KP and water erosion hazards in Hazara and Malakand divisions.
