## Ministry of Interior (MoI) Recruitment Overview

The **Ministry of Interior (MoI)**, Islamabad, oversees Pakistan's internal security, law enforcement, border control, civil registration, and counter-terrorism infrastructure.

Recruitment for gazetted positions (BS-16 and above) is conducted by the **Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)**, while non-gazetted and operational positions (BS-07 to BS-15) are advertised directly or conducted through testing agencies such as NTS, PTS, or departmental recruitment committees.

---

### Key Attached Departments & Regular Posts

1. **Federal Investigation Agency (FIA):**
   - Assistant Director (BS-17), Inspector Investigation (BS-16), Sub-Inspector (BS-14), Assistant Sub-Inspector (BS-11), Constable (BS-07).
   - Core jurisdictions: Cybercrime, immigration, anti-corruption, financial crimes.

2. **Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT) Police:**
   - Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP - via CSS), Deputy Superintendent (DSP), Inspector (BS-16), Sub-Inspector (BS-14), ASI (BS-11), Executive Constables.
   - Requires rigorous physical endurance tests (1.6 km run in 7 minutes, pushups, situps) before written examination.

3. **National Counter Terrorism Authority (NACTA):**
   - Assistant Director Intelligence (BS-17), Research Officer (BS-17), Monitoring Officers (BS-16).
   - Exam covers strategic analysis, National Action Plan (NAP), terrorism threat matrix, international treaties.

4. **Directorate General of Civil Defence:**
   - Civil Defence Officers (BS-16), Instructors (BS-14), Station Officers (BS-11).
   - Focuses on disaster management, fire fighting protocols, industrial safety, and public hazard response.

5. **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA):**
   - Deputy Assistant Directors, Junior Executive, Network Administrators.

---

### Standard Screening Examination Pattern

For BS-16 and BS-17 posts conducted by FPSC:
- **English Composition & Grammar (20%):** Sentence completion, syntax, prepositions, idiomatic phrases, vocabulary.
- **Pakistan Affairs & Constitution (20%):** 1973 Constitution fundamental rights, administrative structure, post-1947 developments.
- **Current Affairs & International Relations (20%):** Regional dynamics, internal security challenges, economic issues.
- **Islamic Studies / Ethics (10%):** Sirah, Islamic jurisprudence basics, Islamic history.
- **Professional & Functional Knowledge (30%):** Specific departmental rules, criminal procedure, official secret act, service laws.
