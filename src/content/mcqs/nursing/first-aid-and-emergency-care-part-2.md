    question: You are being observed while performing CPR. For effective chest compressions, how much chest should be pressed down?
    options:
      - 1 inch
      - 2inches
      - 3 inches
      - 4 inches
    answer: 1
    explanation: During adult CPR, effective chest compressions should depress the chest by at least 2 inches (5 cm) but not more than 2.4 inches (6 cm). This depth helps maintain adequate blood flow to vital organs. - A. 1 inch — Too shallow for effective circulation. - C & D. 3 or 4 inches — Too deep and may cause injury.
