      - Stand upright
    answer: 1

    question: What is the correct sequence for the Primary Survey?
    options:
      - Damage, Response, Airway, Breathing
      - Danger, Response, Airway, Breathing
      - Danger, Reply, Artery, Breathing
      - Danger, Response, Advice, Back
    answer: 1
    explanation: "The correct sequence for the Primary Survey is: Danger, Response, Airway, Breathing, Circulation (DRABC) This is the correct order to assess and treat a patient in a medical emergency : Danger : Check for any hazards or dangers to yourself or the patient. Response : Check the patient's response level (are they alert, unconscious, etc.). Airway : Check the patient's airway to make sure it is clear and secure. Breathing : Check the patient's breathing to make sure they are breathing adequately. Circulation : Check the patient's circulation to make sure they have a pulse and are bleeding."

      - Call 999 and push in the centre of their chest at a regular rate
      - Call 999 and wait for the paramedics to arrive
      - None
    answer: 1

Then, start CPR by pushing down on the center of their chest at a regular rate, about 2 inches deep and 100-120 compressions per minute. Continue this until emergency medical personnel arrive.

T - Time : Time is of the essence . Call for emergency medical services immediately if you observe any of these signs."
