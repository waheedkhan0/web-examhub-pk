    question: The antibody which can cross placental barrier is
    options:
      - lgA
      - lgE
      - lgM
      - lgG
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Antibodies are like tiny soldiers in your body that fight off infections. Different types of antibodies have different jobs and abilities. * IgG (Immunoglobulin G): This is the only type of antibody that can cross the placenta . The placenta is a temporary organ that connects a developing baby to the mother's uterus. Because IgG can cross the placenta, it provides passive immunity to the fetus (baby) before birth, protecting the baby from infections. * IgA (Immunoglobulin A): Found mainly in body secretions like tears, saliva, and breast milk. It helps protect surfaces like your airways and digestive system from infections. IgA does *not* cross the placenta. * IgE (Immunoglobulin E): Involved in allergic reactions and fighting off parasitic infections. IgE does *not* cross the placenta. * IgM (Immunoglobulin M): The first antibody produced during an infection. It's a big molecule and stays mostly in the bloodstream. IgM does *not* cross the placenta. So, IgG is special because it can travel from the mother to the baby across the placenta, providing crucial protection during pregnancy."

    question: "Match List-I with List-II and select the correct answer: List-I (Radioisotope) List-II (Diagnostic use) a. Arsenic-74 1. Activity of thyroid gland b. Cobalt-60 2. Blood disorder c. Iodine-131 3. Tumour d. Sodium-24 4. Cancer"

      - a-4, b-3, c-1, d-2
      - a-3, b-4, c-1, d-2
      - a-4, b-3, c-2, d-1
    answer: 2
    explanation: "* Arsenic-74: This radioisotope is primarily used to locate tumors within the body. * Cobalt-60: Cobalt-60 is a strong radioactive source used in radiation therapy to treat cancer . It works by destroying cancerous cells. * Iodine-131: This radioisotope is readily absorbed by the thyroid gland. Therefore, it's used to measure the activity of the thyroid gland and diagnose thyroid disorders. * Sodium-24: This radioisotope is used to study blood disorders , particularly issues related to blood circulation. Therefore, the correct matching is: a - 3 (Arsenic-74 - Tumour) b - 4 (Cobalt-60 - Cancer) c - 1 (Iodine-131 - Activity of thyroid gland) d - 2 (Sodium-24 - Blood disorder) So, the correct answer is Option C: a-3, b-4, c-1, d-2"
