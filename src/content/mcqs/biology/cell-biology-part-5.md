    question: Which biomolecule serves as the primary energy currency of the cell?
    options:
      - Glucose
      - ATP
      - NADH
      - FADH2
    answer: 1
    explanation: ATP (adenosine triphosphate) stores and transfers energy for cellular processes through hydrolysis of its high-energy phosphate bonds.

    options:
      - Cell wall composition
      - Mode of nutrition
      - Reproductive structures
      - All of the above
    answer: 3
    explanation: Fungi have chitin in cell walls, absorptive heterotrophic nutrition (secrete enzymes externally), and unique reproductive structures distinguishing them from plants and animals.
