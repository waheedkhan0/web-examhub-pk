    question: What is the initial drug of choice for the immediate management of acute eclamptic seizures in a pregnant patient?
    options:
      - Diazepam IV bolus

    answer: 1

    question: In the initial emergency department management of ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI), which antiplatelet loading dose is given immediately?
    options:
      - Aspirin 75 mg
      - Aspirin 300 mg chewable + Clopidogrel 300-600 mg
      - Heparin 10,000 units subcutaneous only
      - Warfarin 10 mg
    answer: 1
    explanation: Immediate dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) for acute STEMI consists of chewable non-enteric coated Aspirin (162-325 mg, typically 300 mg) combined with a P2Y12 inhibitor such as Clopidogrel (300 to 600 mg loading dose) unless contraindicated.
    keyConcept: Acute Coronary Syndrome Loading Protocols

  - id: 3
    question: "A 28-year-old patient presents with fever, neck stiffness, and headache. Lumbar puncture reveals cloudy CSF, elevated opening pressure, neutrophilic pleocytosis, marked protein elevation (>100 mg/dL), and markedly low CSF glucose (<40% of serum glucose). What is the diagnosis?"
    options:
      - Viral (Aseptic) Meningitis
      - Acute Bacterial Meningitis
      - Tuberculous Meningitis
      - Guillain-Barré Syndrome
    answer: 1
    explanation: Acute bacterial meningitis produces turbid CSF with predominantly polymorphonuclear neutrophils (PMNs), high protein, and low glucose (hypoglycorrhachia) consumed by multiplying bacteria. Viral meningitis shows lymphocytic predominance with normal glucose.
    keyConcept: CSF Findings in Meningitis Differential Diagnosis
    testedIn:
      - PPSC Medical Officer 2023
      - KPPSC MO 2024

  - id: 5
    question: What is the fluid of choice and primary resuscitation strategy in the initial management of Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)?
    options:
      - 5% Dextrose water at 100 mL/hr
      - 0.9% Normal Saline (isotonic NaCl) 1000-1500 mL in the first hour
      - Sodium bicarbonate 500 mL bolus
      - Direct rapid intravenous insulin bolus without hydration
    answer: 1
    explanation: "The initial priority in DKA is intravascular volume expansion with isotonic saline (0.9% NaCl) at 10-20 mL/kg/hr (approx 1-1.5 L in the first hour). Insulin infusion (0.1 units/kg/hr) is initiated after confirming adequate fluid hydration and serum potassium > 3.3 mEq/L."
    keyConcept: DKA Fluid Resuscitation Guidelines

    question: What is the standard intensive phase pharmacological regimen for new pulmonary tuberculosis under the National TB Control Program of Pakistan?

    question: In pediatric dehydration according to WHO IMNCI guidelines, which clinical sign indicates 'Severe Dehydration'?
    options:
      - Child is restless and irritable
      - Lethargic or unconscious child with skin pinch going back very slowly (> 2 seconds) and sunken eyes
      - Drinking eagerly and thirsty
      - Mild dry mouth only
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Under WHO IMNCI criteria, Severe Dehydration is diagnosed if two or more of the following signs are present: 1. Lethargy or unconsciousness, 2. Sunken eyes, 3. Inability to drink or drinking poorly, 4. Skin pinch going back very slowly (more than 2 seconds)."

    question: Which laboratory parameter is the most sensitive and specific biomarker for diagnosing acute myocardial infarction within 3 to 6 hours of chest pain onset?
    options:
      - AST (SGOT)
      - LDH (Lactate Dehydrogenase)
      - Cardiac Troponin I or T (cTnI / cTnT)
      - Total Creatine Kinase (CK)
    answer: 2

    keyConcept: Cardiac Biomarkers in Myocardial Infarction

    question: What is the first-line pharmacotherapy for an adult patient presenting with acute anaphylactic shock?
    options:
      - IV Hydrocortisone 200 mg
      - Intramuscular Epinephrine (Adrenaline) 1:1,000 (0.5 mg) in the mid-anterolateral thigh
      - Oral Chlorpheniramine

    answer: 1

Antihistamines and corticosteroids are secondary adjuncts and do not reverse airway obstruction or cardiovascular collapse promptly."
    keyConcept: First-line Treatment for Anaphylaxis

    question: Which antibiotic class is strictly contraindicated in pregnant women and growing children due to teeth discoloration and bone growth impairment?
    options:
      - Beta-lactams (Penicillins)
      - Tetracyclines (e.g. Doxycycline)
      - Macrolides (Azithromycin)
      - Cephalosporins
    answer: 1
    explanation: Tetracyclines chelate calcium and deposit in developing bones and deciduous/permanent teeth, causing permanent brownish-yellow enamel discoloration and hypoplasia; they are classified as pregnancy category D.
    keyConcept: "Teratogenic & Contraindicated Drugs in Pregnancy"

  - id: 12
    question: In obstetrics, placenta previa is characteristically defined by which clinical presentation?
    options:
      - Painful vaginal bleeding with rigid, woody abdomen in the third trimester
      - Painless, bright-red vaginal bleeding in the second or third trimester without uterine tenderness
      - Continuous watery amniotic fluid discharge with fever
      - Severe lower abdominal cramping without visible bleeding
    answer: 1
    explanation: Placenta previa classically presents as painless, recurrent, bright red vaginal bleeding in late second or third trimester without uterine tetany. Abruptio placentae presents with painful, dark vaginal bleeding and a tense, tender, wooden uterus.
    keyConcept: Placenta Previa vs Abruptio Placentae

  - id: 13
    question: "In the diagnosis of acute pancreatitis, serum lipase is preferred over serum amylase because:"
    options:
      - Lipase is cheaper to test
      - Lipase remains elevated longer (7-14 days) and has greater sensitivity and specificity
      - Amylase never rises in acute pancreatitis
      - Lipase is produced only in the liver
    answer: 1
    explanation: Serum lipase has higher sensitivity (85-100%) and specificity for pancreatic injury. Amylase normalizes within 48-72 hours, whereas lipase stays elevated for 8 to 14 days, providing wider diagnostic utility.
    keyConcept: Serum Enzymes in Acute Pancreatitis
