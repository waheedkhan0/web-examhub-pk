  - id: 1
    question: "Immunity from maternal antibodies crossing placenta is:"
    options:
      - Active natural
      - Active artificial
      - Passive natural
      - Passive artificial
    answer: 2
    explanation: Passive natural immunity provides temporary protection to fetus/newborn; antibodies degrade over months and no memory cells are produced.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
  - id: 2
    question: "C4 plants minimize photorespiration by:"
    options:
      - Keeping stomata closed during day
      - Spatial separation of initial CO₂ fixation and Calvin cycle
      - Using PEP carboxylase with high affinity for CO₂
      - Both B and C
    answer: 3
    explanation: C4 plants fix CO₂ into oxaloacetate (via PEP carboxylase) in mesophyll cells; C4 acids transported to bundle sheath cells where CO₂ released for Calvin cycle.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
  - id: 3
    question: "Platelets are derived from bone marrow cells called:"
    options:
      - Erythroblasts
      - Myeloblasts
      - Megakaryocytes
      - Lymphoblasts
    answer: 2
    explanation: Megakaryocytes fragment to release 2000-3000 platelets each into bloodstream; platelets lack nuclei and survive 7-10 days.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
  - id: 4
    question: "Sister chromatids separate during:"
    options:
      - Anaphase of mitosis
      - Anaphase I of meiosis
      - Anaphase II of meiosis
      - Both A and C
    answer: 3
    explanation: Sister chromatids separate during anaphase of mitosis and anaphase II of meiosis; homologous chromosomes separate during anaphase I of meiosis.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
  - id: 5
    question: "Retroviruses differ from other viruses by:"
    options:
      - Having DNA genome
      - Having RNA genome
      - Using reverse transcriptase
      - Having protein coat
    answer: 2
    explanation: Retroviruses (e.g., HIV) carry reverse transcriptase enzyme that converts their RNA genome into DNA after entering host cell; DNA integrates into host genome.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
  - id: 6
    question: "Estrogen is produced primarily by:"
    options:
      - Anterior pituitary
      - Ovarian follicles
      - Corpus luteum
      - Adrenal cortex
    answer: 1
    explanation: Granulosa cells of developing ovarian follicles secrete estrogen during follicular phase; corpus luteum secretes both estrogen and progesterone after ovulation.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
  - id: 7
    question: "Osmosis specifically refers to movement of:"
    options:
      - Solutes
      - Water
      - Ions
      - Glucose
    answer: 1
    explanation: Osmosis is passive diffusion of water across selectively permeable membrane from region of higher water potential to lower water potential.
    keyConcept: Cell Structure & Function
