  - id: 6
    question: "The process of conversion of nitrates to nitrogen gas is called:"
    options:
      - Nitrogen fixation
      - Nitrification
      - Denitrification
      - Ammonification
    answer: 2
    explanation: Denitrification is carried out by denitrifying bacteria (e.g., Pseudomonas) under anaerobic conditions, converting nitrates (NO₃⁻) back to atmospheric nitrogen (N₂).
