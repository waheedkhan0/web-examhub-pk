    question: "The external source of recuritment are 1. Lay-off 2. Casual callers 3. Recommendations 4. Campus recuritment Select the correct answer:"
    options:
      - Both 1 and 2
      - 1, 2 and 3
      - Both 1 and 4
      - 2, 3 and 4
    answer: 3
    explanation: "This question asks about external sources of recruitment . This means where a company finds potential employees from outside the company. Let's look at each option: 1. Lay-off: This is when a company *fires* or *ends employment* to an employee. This happens *within* a company, not from an outside source. So, lay-off is not an external source . 2. Casual callers: These are people who walk into a company or send in resumes on their own , hoping for a job. They are external candidates , not existing employees. 3. Recommendations: This refers to getting potential employees referred by current employees or other external contacts . This is definitely sourcing from outside the company . 4. Campus recruitment: This involves companies visiting college and university campuses to find and hire new graduates. This is a very common method of external recruitment . Therefore: * Lay-off (1) is *not* an external source. * Casual callers (2) *are* an external source. * Recommendations (3) *are* an external source. * Campus recruitment (4) *are* an external source. So, the correct answer is Option D: 2, 3 and 4 ."

Job Summary : This provides a concise overview of the job, highlighting key aspects such as the job's purpose and essential functions. Job Specification : This component specifies the qualifications, skills, and attributes required for a person to perform the job successfully. \"Role Analysis\" is not commonly included as a standard component of Job Analysis.     question: "Statement (I): T-group and sensitivity training are not the same. Statement (II): T-group participants belong to diverse groups and may not be familiar with each other. Statement (III): T-group training sessions are well structured."
    options:
      - Statements (I) and (II) are correct
      - Statements (II) and (III) are correct
      - All three statements are correct
      - Statement (II) is correct
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Statement (I): T-group and sensitivity training are not the same. This is TRUE . While related, they're not identical. Sensitivity training is a broader term. T-groups are a specific method within sensitivity training. Statement (II): T-group participants belong to diverse groups and may not be familiar with each other. This is TRUE . T-groups often bring together people from different backgrounds and levels within an organization (or even from different organizations) who don't know each other well. This helps foster open communication and learning from diverse perspectives. Statement (III): T-group training sessions are well structured. This is FALSE . T-groups are intentionally unstructured . The lack of a rigid agenda encourages participants to take the lead and reveal their behaviors and communication styles naturally. The facilitator guides, but doesn't dictate the content. The unstructured nature is key to revealing interpersonal dynamics. So, Statement (I) and Statement (II) are correct. Therefore, the correct answer is A: Statements (I) and (II) are correct"
