    question: A Profitability Index (PI) of 0.9 means?
    options:
      - The project's NPV is positive, and the project should be accepted
      - The project's NPV is zero, and the project should be accepted
      - The project's NPV is negative, and the project should be rejected
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: "A Profitability Index (PI) of 0.9 means the project's NPV is negative , and the project should be rejected . The Profitability Index (PI) compares the present value of future cash inflows with the initial investment. PI > 1: NPV is positive → Accept the project. PI = 1: NPV is zero → Indifferent between accepting and rejecting. PI < 1: NPV is negative → Reject the project."

    question: If a bond is selling at a higher market price than its par value, then?
    options:
      - P is equal to par value and YTM is equal to coupon rate
      - P is lower than par value and YTM is higher than coupon rate
      - P is higher than par value and YTM is lower than coupon rate
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: When a bond sells above its par value, its YTM is lower than its coupon rate . Par value is the face value of the bond. If market price > par value , the bond is trading at a premium. For a premium bond, the yield to maturity (YTM) < coupon rate. Therefore, P > Par Value and YTM < Coupon Rate .
