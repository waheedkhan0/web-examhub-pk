    question: A bank has assets of Rs. 100 million with a 5-year modified duration and liabilities with a 2-year duration. What is the Duration Gap?
    options:
      - 2 years
      - 3 years
      - 3.4 years
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Duration Gap = D_A - (L/A × D_L) Here: D_A = 5 years D_L = 2 years A = 100 million To get 3.4 years: 3.4 = 5 - (L/100 × 2) (L/100 × 2) = 1.6 L/100 = 0.8 → L = Rs. 80 million So with Assets = 100m and Liabilities = 80m: Duration Gap = 5 - (80/100 × 2) = 5 - 1.6 = 3.4 years"
