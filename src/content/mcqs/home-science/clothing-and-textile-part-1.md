    question: This twill weave material is used for expensive suits and outer wear
    options:
      - gabardine
      - linen
      - velour
      - pelIon
    answer: 0
    explanation: "The correct answer is A: gabardine. Let's break down why: Gabardine: This is a tightly woven twill fabric. Its distinct diagonal rib pattern makes it durable and water-repellent. These qualities make it perfect for high-quality suits and outerwear like coats and trench coats. Linen: This is a fabric made from flax fibers. It's known for being lightweight and breathable, more suitable for summer clothing than suits or outerwear requiring structure and water resistance. Velour: This is a plush, knitted fabric similar to velvet. It's soft and comfortable but not typically used for structured suits or heavy-duty outerwear . Pellon: Pellon is a type of interfacing or stabilizer. It's used inside garments to provide structure and support , but it's not the actual outer fabric of a suit or coat. Therefore, gabardine is the best choice for expensive suits and outerwear because of its twill weave, durability, and water resistance."

    question: Assertion (A) The most suitable method of printion with disperse dyes is transfer printing. Reason (R) Disperse dyes sublime when hearted to high temperature.
    options:
      - Both A and R are incorrect
      - Both A and R are correct
      - A is correct, but R is incorrect
      - A is incorrect, but R is correct
    answer: 1
    explanation: "The correct answer is Option B: Both A and R are correct Let's break down why: Assertion (A): The most suitable method of printing with disperse dyes is transfer printing. This is TRUE . Disperse dyes are a type of dye that are commonly used on synthetic fabrics like polyester. Transfer printing is a great method for disperse dyes because it uses heat to transfer the dye onto the fabric. Reason (R): Disperse dyes sublime when heated to high temperature. This is also TRUE . Sublimation means that a substance goes directly from a solid to a gas, skipping the liquid phase. Disperse dyes do this when heated, which is what makes transfer printing work so well. Why it works together: Because disperse dyes sublime when heated, we can use that property in transfer printing. The dye is printed onto a special paper and then heat is applied. The dye turns into a gas and transfers onto the fabric, creating the print. Therefore, both the assertion and the reason are correct, and the reason explains why the assertion is true."
