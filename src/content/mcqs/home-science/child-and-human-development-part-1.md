    question: Which is the specific sequence of human development?
    options:
      - Cephalocaudal Sequence
      - Proximodistal Sequence
      - Both A and B
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: "The correct answer is C: Both A and B . Let's break down why: * Human development follows predictable patterns. Two important patterns are Cephalocaudal and Proximodistal. * Cephalocaudal Sequence : \"Cephalo\" refers to head, and \"caudal\" refers to tail. This means development progresses from the head downwards. Think about it: babies gain control of their head and neck muscles *before* they can control their legs and feet. * Proximodistal Sequence : \"Proximo\" means near, and \"distal\" means far. This means development progresses from the center of the body outwards. For example, a baby learns to control their arms before they can control their hands and fingers. They can move their whole arm before they can grasp something with their fingers. * Since both of these sequences are correct, the answer is Both A and B . * Therefore , human development follows both the Cephalocaudal (head-to-toe) and Proximodistal (center-out) sequences."

    question: Write in correct sequence. 1. Adulthood 2. Infancy 3. Adolescence 4. Old age
    options:
      - 3, 2, 1, 4
      - 1, 3, 2, 4
      - 2, 3, 1, 4
      - 4, 1. 2, 3
    answer: 2
    explanation: The stages of human development follow a chronological order. Infancy comes first, as it is the initial stage of life characterized by rapid growth and development. Next is Adolescence , which marks the transition from childhood to adulthood, involving significant physical, psychological, and social changes. After adolescence comes Adulthood , where individuals reach full physical and intellectual maturity and take on adult roles and responsibilities. Lastly, Old age follows, representing the final stage of the human life cycle, characterized by declining physical abilities and increased susceptibility to age-related health issues. Therefore, the correct sequence is 2, 3, 1, 4 .
