Decreased secretion of lactose: Our bodies produce less of the enzyme needed to digest lactose (milk sugar). Now, let's look at List-II: This list shows the effects of these changes on our nutritional needs and health. 1. Poor Vitamin D: This is a deficiency that can cause health problems. 2. Loss of ability to detect salt and sweet: This is a consequence of having fewer taste buds. Now, let's match them up: a. Decreased taste buds directly relate to 4. Can be a risk factor for developing osteoporosis . Less calcium means weaker bones. c. Impaired glucose tolerance can lead to problems managing blood sugar, which is often linked to poor health and can relate to vitamin deficiency, making 1. Poor vitamin D a relevant consequence. d. Decreased secretion of lactose is not directly related to vitamin D or energy needs. It indicates digestive issues, but doesn't directly lead to osteoporosis. This leaves 2. Requirement of energy decreases as less appropriate, though not strictly incorrect, leaving it to be paired with this option. Therefore, the correct answer matches the descriptions accurately."

    question: Which of the following is not a method of Nutritional Status Assessment by anthropometry?
    options:
      - MUAC
      - Height
      - Blood Pressure
      - Waist Circumference
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Anthropometry is the measurement of the physical dimensions and composition of the human body, used in the assessment of nutritional status. MUAC (Mid-Upper Arm Circumference): It is a common anthropometric measurement used to assess malnutrition, particularly in children. Height: Measurement of height is another anthropometric method used to evaluate growth and nutritional status, especially in relation to age. Waist Circumference: This measurement helps assess body fat distribution and is used to evaluate the risk of obesity-related health problems. Blood Pressure , however, is not a method of nutritional status assessment by anthropometry. It is used to measure the force of blood against artery walls, which relates to cardiovascular health rather than nutrition. Thus, the correct answer is Blood Pressure ."

    question: What is the value for diastole and systole value for hypertension, according to WHO?
    options:

      - "> 100 mm hg, > 150 mm hg"
      - "> 105 mm hg, > 180 mm hg"
      - "> 90 mm hg, > 140 mm hg"
    answer: 3
    explanation: "The World Health Organization (WHO) defines hypertension as a condition in which the blood pressure in the arteries is persistently elevated. The values for diagnosing hypertension are based on two measurements: diastolic and systolic blood pressure. According to WHO, hypertension is diagnosed when the diastolic value is greater than 90 mm Hg and the systolic value is greater than 140 mm Hg . Therefore, the correct answer is > 90 mm Hg, > 140 mm Hg ."

    options:
      - Both A and R are true
      - A is false and R is true
      - A is true and R is false
      - Both A and R are false
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Question Explanation: This question is about diet-induced thermogenesis (DIT) , which is the energy your body uses to digest, absorb, and process the food you eat. The assertion (A) says that DIT is lower when you eat a diet very high in fat. The reason (R) says that digesting, absorbing, and metabolizing fats uses more energy than carbohydrates. Let's break down why one statement might be true or false: Reason (R): It's actually false . While fat does provide a lot of energy, your body uses less energy to process fat compared to carbohydrates and proteins. Assertion (A): Because your body uses less energy processing fat, it means the diet-induced thermogenesis (DIT) is lower on high-fat diets , making the assertion (A) true. Therefore, the correct answer is C: A is true and R is false ."
