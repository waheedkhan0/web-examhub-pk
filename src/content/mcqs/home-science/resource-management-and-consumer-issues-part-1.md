    question: The normal family goals is/are 1. all round development of family members. 2. cordial interpersonal relationships. 3. common aim. 4. discharge of responsibilities.
    options:
      - 1 and 2
      - 1 and 4
      - 1, 2 and 3
      - All of these
    answer: 3
    explanation: "In the context of home science, the normal family goals encompass various aspects aimed at fostering a harmonious and fulfilling family life. These goals typically include: All round development of family members: This goal emphasizes the holistic development of all family members, encompassing physical, emotional, intellectual, and social aspects. Cordial interpersonal relationships: This goal focuses on fostering positive and healthy relationships among family members, promoting mutual respect, understanding, and support. Common aim: This goal highlights the importance of having a shared vision or objective that unites family members and guides their collective efforts towards a common purpose. Discharge of responsibilities: This goal emphasizes the fulfillment of individual and collective responsibilities within the family, including roles and duties related to household chores, caregiving, financial management, and decision-making. Therefore, the correct answer is Option D: All of these, as all the mentioned goals contribute to fostering a healthy and well-functioning family unit."
