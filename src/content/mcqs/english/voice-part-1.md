    question: Our task had been completed before sunset.
    options:
      - We completed our task before sunset.
      - We have completed our task before sunset.
      - We complete our task before sunset.
      - We had completed our task before sunset.
    answer: 3
    explanation: "This question is based on the concept of Active and Passive Voice . Understanding the voice is crucial to transforming sentences correctly while maintaining their original meaning and tense. Voice: In grammar, voice refers to the form of a verb that shows whether the subject performs the action (active voice) or receives the action (passive voice). Passive Voice Structure: In passive voice, the focus is on the action or the recipient of the action rather than the doer. The structure generally follows: Object + had been + past participle (V3) + (by + subject) Original Sentence (Passive Voice): Our task had been completed before sunset. Step-by-step Conversion to Active Voice: 1. Identify the subject: Implicit in passive voice. Likely \"we\". 2. Change \"our task\" to the object. 3. \"Had been completed\" is the past perfect passive. The active form of this is \" had completed \". 4. Keep the time expression \"before sunset\" as it is. Correct Transformation: We had completed our task before sunset. Correct Answer: Option D : We had completed our task before sunset. Other Options: Option A: We completed our task before sunset. — ❌ Incorrect. Changes the tense from past perfect to simple past. Option B: We have completed our task before sunset. — ❌ Incorrect. Changes the tense to present perfect, which is not equivalent. Option C: We complete our task before sunset. — ❌ Incorrect. Changes the tense to simple present, which alters the meaning entirely. Conclusion: The correct active voice form that maintains the original tense and meaning is Option D ."

Voice: In grammar, voice refers to the form of a verb that indicates whether the subject performs the action (active voice) or receives the action (passive voice). Active Voice Structure: Subject + Verb + Object Passive Voice Structure: Object + appropriate form of \"to be\" + past participle (V3) + by + Subject Original Sentence (Active Voice): The boy laughed at the beggar. Step-by-step Breakdown: 1. The object \"the beggar\" becomes the subject in the passive sentence. 2. The verb phrase is \"laughed at\", which is a phrasal verb . Maintain the preposition \"at\" with the verb. 5. The subject \"the boy\" comes after \"by\". Correct Transformation: The beggar was laughed at by the boy. Correct Answer: Option D : The beggar was laughed at by the boy. Other Options: Option A: The beggar was laughed by the boy. — ❌ Incorrect. Omits the necessary preposition \"at\", making it grammatically incorrect. Option B: The beggar was being laughed by the boy. — ❌ Incorrect. Incorrect tense (\"was being\" indicates past continuous) and missing \"at\". Option C: The beggar was being laughed at by the boy. — ❌ Incorrect.
