    question: "Choose the correct conjunction: '_____ he was busy, he couldn't attend.'"
    options:
      - Because
      - Although
      - Since
      - Therefore
    answer: 0
    explanation: "'Because' shows cause and effect. His busyness caused him to miss the event."

  - id: 2
    question: "Choose the correct form: 'If he _____, he would have passed.'"
    options:
      - studied
      - had studied
      - has studied
      - studies
    answer: 1
    explanation: This is a third conditional sentence referring to an unreal past situation. The structure is 'if + past perfect, would have + past participle'.
    keyConcept: Grammar & Parts of Speech
  - id: 3
    question: "Choose the correct form: 'I wish I _____ to the party.'"
    options:
      - go
      - went
      - had gone
      - have gone
    answer: 1
    explanation: After 'wish' when expressing regret about the past, we use the past perfect tense for actions that didn't happen, but for general past wishes, simple past is also acceptable. Here, 'went' is the simple past form.
    keyConcept: Grammar & Parts of Speech
  - id: 4
    question: "Choose the correct preposition: 'She is interested _____ learning.'"
    options:
      - to
      - in
      - "on"
      - at
    answer: 1
    explanation: We use 'interested in' followed by a noun or gerund. This is a fixed prepositional phrase.
    keyConcept: Grammar & Parts of Speech
  - id: 5
    question: "Choose the correct article: 'She is _____ only child.'"
    options:
      - a
      - an
      - the
      - no article
    answer: 2
    explanation: We use 'the' with superlatives and with 'only' when referring to a unique position or role.
    keyConcept: Grammar & Parts of Speech
  - id: 6
    question: "Choose the correct form: 'I suggest that she _____ a doctor.'"
    options:
      - see
      - sees
      - saw
      - seen
    answer: 0
    explanation: After verbs like 'suggest', 'recommend', 'demand', etc., we use the base form of the verb (subjunctive mood) without 's' for third person singular.
    keyConcept: Grammar & Parts of Speech
