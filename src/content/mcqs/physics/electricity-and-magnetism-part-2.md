  - id: 37
    question: "A point charge Q is placed at the center of a spherical Gaussian surface. The electric flux through the surface will change if:"

      - The point charge is moved off-center
      - A second point charge is placed outside the sphere
      - The point charge is replaced by a charge 2Q
    answer: 3
    explanation: According to Gauss's law, the net flux through a closed surface is proportional to the charge enclosed (Φ = Q/ε₀). Only changing Q to 2Q changes the enclosed charge and thus the flux.
