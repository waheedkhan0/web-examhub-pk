    question: "A person standing on a frictionless horizontal surface throws a ball. The person will:"
    options:
      - Not move
      - Move in the direction of the throw
      - Move opposite to the direction of the throw
      - Move sideways
    answer: 2
    explanation: This is based on Newton's third law and conservation of momentum. When the person exerts a force on the ball to throw it forward, the ball exerts an equal and opposite force on the person, pushing them backward.

    question: "A girl is sitting on a swing. Another girl sits by her side. The time period of the swing:"
    options:
      - Increases
      - Decreases
      - Remains the same
      - Becomes zero
    answer: 2
    explanation: The time period of a simple pendulum (or swing) T = 2π√(L/g) depends only on its length (L) and the acceleration due to gravity (g). It is independent of the mass of the bob. Adding more mass does not change the time period.

  - id: 3
    question: "If a motorcyclist doubles his speed on a circular turn of the same radius, the centripetal force required becomes:"
    options:
      - Double
      - Half
      - Four times
      - Eight times
    answer: 2
    explanation: Centripetal force F_c = mv²/r. For a constant mass (m) and radius (r), F_c ∝ v². If the speed (v) is doubled, the force becomes (2)² = 4 times the original force.

    question: "During a phase change (e.g., ice melting at 0°C), the temperature of the substance:"
    options:
      - Increases
      - Decreases

      - Fluctuates
    answer: 2
    explanation: During a phase transition, the heat supplied (latent heat) is used to overcome the intermolecular forces to change the phase of the substance, not to increase the average kinetic energy of the molecules. Therefore, the temperature remains constant.

  - id: 13
    question: Which of the following is an example of a non-conservative force?
    options:
      - Gravitational force
      - Electrostatic force
      - Spring force
      - Friction
    answer: 3
    explanation: A force is non-conservative if the work done by it depends on the path taken. Friction is a classic example, as longer paths result in more negative work done. Work done by conservative forces (gravity, spring) is path-independent.

  - id: 14
    question: "A 0.5 kg ball is thrown upwards with a speed of 20 m/s. Its kinetic energy at the highest point is:"
    options:
      - 100 J
      - 50 J
      - 0 J
      - 200 J
    answer: 2
    explanation: At the highest point, the vertical velocity of the ball becomes zero. Kinetic energy (KE = 1/2 mv²) is proportional to the square of velocity. Since v=0, the KE at that instant is zero.

    options:

    explanation: The centripetal force is always perpendicular to the instantaneous displacement of the body. Work done W = F·d = F d cos 90° = 0. This is true for any displacement along the circular path.

  - id: 30
    question: "A bullet is fired from a gun. The ratio of the kinetic energy of the bullet to that of the gun (recoil) is:"
    options:
      - Equal to the ratio of their masses
      - Equal to the ratio of their velocities
      - Inversely proportional to the ratio of their masses
      - Independent of mass
    answer: 2
    explanation: "By conservation of momentum, m_b v_b = m_g v_g => v_g = (m_b/m_g)v_b. KE_b / KE_g = (1/2 m_b v_b²) / (1/2 m_g v_g²) = (m_b/m_g) × (v_b/v_g)² = (m_b/m_g) × (m_g/m_b)² = m_g/m_b. So, KE_b: KE_g = m_g: m_b."
    keyConcept: Mechanics & Motion
