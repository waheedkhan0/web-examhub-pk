    question: The half-life of a radioactive substance is 10 days. What fraction remains after 30 days?
    options:
      - 1/2
      - 1/4
      - 1/8
      - 1/16
    answer: 2
    explanation: After n half-lives, fraction remaining = (1/2)^n. After 30 days = 3 half-lives, fraction = (1/2)³ = 1/8.

    question: "The half-life of a radioactive element is 10 days. The fraction of the original sample that remains after 20 days is:"
    options:
      - 1/2
      - 1/4
      - 1/8
      - 1/16
    answer: 1
    explanation: Half-life is the time for half the nuclei to decay. After 1 half-life (10 days), 1/2 remains. After 2 half-lives (20 days), (1/2) × (1/2) = 1/4 of the original sample remains.
