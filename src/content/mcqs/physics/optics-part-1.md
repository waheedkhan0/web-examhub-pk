    question: "A convex lens forms a real image twice the size of the object. If the object distance is 30 cm, the focal length is:"
    options:
      - 10 cm
      - 15 cm
      - 20 cm
      - 30 cm
    answer: 2
    explanation: Magnification m = -v/u = -2 (real image). So v = 2u = 60 cm. Using 1/f = 1/v + 1/u = 1/60 + 1/30 = 3/60 = 1/20. Therefore f = 20 cm.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light

    question: "The focal length of a convex lens in air is 20 cm. When immersed in water (n=4/3), its focal length becomes:"
    options:
      - 20 cm
      - 40 cm
      - 60 cm
      - 80 cm
    answer: 3
    explanation: "1/f ∝ (n_lens/n_medium - 1). In air: 1/f_a ∝ (1.5/1 - 1) = 0.5. In water: 1/f_w ∝ (1.5/1.33 - 1) ≈ 0.125. Ratio f_w/f_a = 0.5/0.125 = 4. So f_w = 80 cm."
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
