      - Velocity

  - id: 4
    question: "The resultant of two forces of equal magnitude 'F' acting at an angle of 120° is:"
    options:
      - F/2
      - F
      - 2F
      - F√2
    answer: 1
    explanation: The magnitude of the resultant R = √(F² + F² + 2F² cos θ). For θ=120°, cos 120° = -1/2. So, R = √(2F² + 2F²(-1/2)) = √(2F² - F²) = √(F²) = F.
    keyConcept: Mechanics & Motion

    question: "The work done by the centripetal force on a body moving in a circle is always:"
    options:
      - Positive
      - Negative
      - Zero
      - Maximum
    answer: 2
    explanation: Centripetal force acts towards the center of the circle, which is perpendicular to the direction of the instantaneous displacement (tangential) of the body. Work done W = F·d = F d cos θ. Since θ = 90°, cos 90° = 0, so work done is zero.
