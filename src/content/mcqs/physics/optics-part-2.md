  - id: 3
    question: "The wavelength of photon emitted when electron jumps from n=3 to n=2 in hydrogen atom is:"
    options:
      - 656 nm
      - 486 nm
      - 434 nm
      - 410 nm
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Balmer-α line: 1/λ = R(1/2² - 1/3²) = 5R/36 ⇒ λ = 36/(5R) ≈ 656 nm (red light)."
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 4
    question: "A convex lens of focal length 20 cm has a power of:"
    options:
      - +2 D
      - "-2 D"
      - +5 D
      - "-5 D"
    answer: 2
    explanation: Power of a lens P = 1 / f (in meters). A convex lens has a positive focal length. Here, f = +20 cm = +0.2 m. Therefore, P = 1 / 0.2 = +5 diopters (D).
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 5
    question: "In the photoelectric effect, the number of photoelectrons emitted is proportional to:"
    options:
      - Frequency of incident light
      - Intensity of incident light
      - Wavelength of incident light
      - Work function of the metal
    answer: 1
    explanation: The intensity of light is related to the number of photons incident per second per unit area. A higher intensity means more photons, which can eject more photoelectrons, increasing the photocurrent.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 6
    question: Which of the following has a wavelength of 10⁻¹⁰ m?
    options:
      - Radio waves
      - X-rays
      - Microwaves
      - Ultraviolet rays
    answer: 1
    explanation: X-rays have wavelengths in the range of about 10⁻⁸ m to 10⁻¹¹ m. 10⁻¹⁰ m (0.1 nm) falls within the X-ray region of the electromagnetic spectrum.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 7
    question: "The phenomenon of light bending around the corners of an obstacle is called:"
    options:
      - Reflection
      - Refraction
      - Diffraction
      - Interference
    answer: 2
    explanation: Diffraction is the slight bending of light as it passes around the edge of an object or through a narrow slit. The amount of bending depends on the size of the obstacle relative to the wavelength of light.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 8
    question: "The image formed by a plane mirror is always:"
    options:
      - Real and inverted
      - Virtual and erect
      - Real and erect
      - Virtual and inverted
    answer: 1
    explanation: A plane mirror always forms an image that is virtual (cannot be projected on a screen), erect (upright), and laterally inverted (left appears as right). The image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 9
    question: "A myopic person cannot see distant objects clearly because:"
    options:
      - The eye lens has too long a focal length
      - The eyeball is too long
      - The image is formed behind the retina
      - The ciliary muscles cannot relax
    answer: 1
    explanation: Myopia (nearsightedness) occurs when the eyeball is too long or the eye lens is too curved. This causes the image of a distant object to be formed in front of the retina, making it appear blurry.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 10
    question: Which of the following statements is true for a convex mirror?
    options:
      - It always forms a real image
      - It always forms an inverted image
      - It always forms a virtual and diminished image
      - Its focal length is positive
    answer: 2
    explanation: A convex mirror always forms an image that is virtual (behind the mirror), erect, and diminished (smaller than the object). Its focal length is considered negative by the sign convention.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 11
    question: "A concave lens always forms an image which is:"
    options:
      - Real and inverted
      - Real and erect
      - Virtual and erect
      - Virtual and inverted
    answer: 2
    explanation: A concave lens (diverging lens) always forms an image that is virtual (on the same side as the object), erect, and diminished, regardless of the object's position.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 12
    question: "The critical angle for light going from medium A to medium B is θ. The speed of light in medium A is v. The speed of light in medium B is:"
    options:
      - v sin θ
      - v / sin θ
      - v cos θ
      - v / cos θ
    answer: 1
    explanation: The critical angle θ is given by sin θ = n₂/n₁ = v₁/v₂, where v₁ is the speed in medium A and v₂ is the speed in medium B. Therefore, v₂ = v₁ / sin θ = v / sin θ.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 13
    question: "The power of a lens is +2 D. Its focal length is:"
    options:
      - 0.5 m
      - 2 m
      - "-0.5 m"
      - 50 cm
    answer: 0
    explanation: Power P = 1/f (in meters). A positive power indicates a convex lens. So, f = 1/P = 1/2 = 0.5 m = 50 cm.
    keyConcept: Optics & Light
  - id: 14
    question: "A ray of light passes from air into a denser medium. The angle of refraction is:"
    options:
      - Greater than the angle of incidence
      - Less than the angle of incidence
      - Equal to the angle of incidence
      - Always 90°
    answer: 1
    explanation: When light enters a denser medium, it slows down and bends towards the normal. Therefore, the angle of refraction (r) is less than the angle of incidence (i).
