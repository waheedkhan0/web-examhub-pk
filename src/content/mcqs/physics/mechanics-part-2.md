    question: "The force acting on a charged particle moving perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field is:"
    options:
      - Parallel to velocity
      - Perpendicular to velocity
      - Parallel to field
      - Zero
    answer: 1
    explanation: Magnetic force F = q(v × B). When v ⊥ B, force magnitude is qvB and direction is perpendicular to both v and B (given by right-hand rule).
