  - id: 1
    question: Two bulbs, one of 60 W and one of 100 W, are connected in series to a power supply. Which bulb will glow brighter?
    options:
      - 60 W
      - 100 W
      - Both equally
      - Neither will glow
    answer: 0
    explanation: When connected in series, the current is the same through both bulbs. Power dissipated P = I²R. The 60 W bulb has a higher resistance (since R = V²/P for same voltage rating). With same I, the higher resistance bulb (60 W) dissipates more power and glows brighter.
    keyConcept: Electricity & Magnetism
  - id: 2
    question: Which of the following has the highest penetrating power?
    options:
      - Alpha particles
      - Beta particles
      - Gamma rays
      - Neutrons
    answer: 2
    explanation: Gamma rays are high-energy electromagnetic radiation. They have no charge and very high energy, allowing them to penetrate most materials deeply. Alpha particles have the lowest penetrating power.
