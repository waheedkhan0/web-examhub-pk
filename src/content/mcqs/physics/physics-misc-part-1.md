    question: "In Young's double slit experiment, if the separation between slits is doubled, the fringe width will:"
    options:
      - Double
      - Become half
      - Remain the same

    answer: 1
    explanation: Fringe width β = λD/d, where d is slit separation. If d is doubled, β becomes half.

    question: "The magnitude of resultant of two vectors of magnitudes 3 N and 4 N cannot be:"
    options:

      - 5 N
      - 7 N
      - 8 N
    answer: 3
    explanation: Maximum resultant = 3 + 4 = 7 N (when vectors are parallel). Minimum resultant = |4 - 3| = 1 N (when vectors are antiparallel). Resultant must be between 1 N and 7 N.
