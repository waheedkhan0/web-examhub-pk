    question: The termination of the conditioned stimulus (CS) either with the onset of the unconditioned stimulus (UCS) or during the presentation of the unconditioned stimulus (UCS) is known as ______?
    options:
      - Backward Conditioning
      - Delay Conditioning
      - Trace Conditioning
      - None of these
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Delay conditioning occurs when the conditioned stimulus (CS) begins before the unconditioned stimulus (UCS) and overlaps with it. The CS may continue until the UCS begins or ends during the UCS presentation. Additional information : In classical conditioning , the conditioned stimulus is associated with the unconditioned stimulus to produce a learned response."
