    options:
      - IYUNIFW
      - IXVMGJW
      - HWUNHGW
      - IZVPIFX
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Running Key Cipher is a polyalphabetic substitution cipher where a plaintext is combined with a keyword to generate a ciphered text. The keyword is usually a long text that matches or exceeds the length of the plaintext. Given: Plaintext: \"EXAMPLE\" Keyword: \"DATASTRUCTURE\" Step 1: Convert each letter to its corresponding position in the alphabet: E = 5, X = 24, A = 1, M = 13, P = 16, L = 12, E = 5 D = 4, A = 1, T = 20, A = 1, S = 19, T = 20, R = 18 Step 2: Add the positions of the plaintext and keyword letters: (5 + 4), (24 + 1), (1 + 20), (13 + 1), (16 + 19), (12 + 20), (5 + 18) = 9, 25, 21, 14, 35, 32, 23 Step 3: If the sum exceeds 26, subtract 26 to get the cipher position: 9, 25, 21, 14, 35 - 26 = 9, 32 - 26 = 6, 23 Step 4: Convert these positions back to letters: 9 = I, 25 = Y, 21 = U, 14 = N, 9 = I, 6 = F, 23 = W Ciphered Text: The final ciphered text is \"IYUNIFW\". Conclusion: The correct answer corresponds to the following option: Option A: IYUNIFW"
