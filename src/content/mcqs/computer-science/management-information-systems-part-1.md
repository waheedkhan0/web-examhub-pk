    question: Management is linked to the information processor by
    options:
      - Decisions
      - Data
      - Both (a) and (b)
      - Neither (a) nor (b)
      - None of the above
    answer: 2
    explanation: "In Management Information Systems (MIS), the linkage between management and the information processor (the system that processes and provides information) is crucial for effective decision-making and operations management. Option A: Decisions. Management relies on MIS to provide processed information that supports decision-making at various levels of the organization. This includes strategic decisions, operational decisions, and tactical decisions. Option B: Data. MIS collects, stores, processes, and retrieves data from various sources within and outside the organization. This data is essential for generating meaningful information that aids management in understanding trends, analyzing performance, and forecasting future outcomes. Therefore, both decisions and data are integral components of how management interacts with the information processor in MIS. Management uses the processed data to make informed decisions that drive organizational success and efficiency. Correct Answer: Option C: Both (a) and (b)"
