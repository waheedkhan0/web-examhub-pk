    question: Calorific value of dry wood may be around __________ Kcal/kg.
    options:
      - "5"
      - "50"
      - "500"
      - "5000"
    answer: 3
    explanation: The calorific value of a fuel is the amount of heat energy released during its combustion. For dry wood, the calorific value is typically around 5000 Kcal/kg, making it a good source of energy for combustion processes. This value can vary slightly depending on the type of wood and its composition, but 5000 Kcal/kg is a common average used in calculations and assessments.
