    question: Volume of blast furnace slag is about __________ times the volume of hot metal (pig iron) of the same weight
    options:
      - "1.5"
      - "3"
      - "5"
      - "7"
    answer: 1
    explanation: "The volume of blast furnace slag is about 3 times the volume of hot metal (pig iron) of the same weight. In the blast furnace process, iron ore (mainly iron oxide) is reduced to produce pig iron, and the impurities in the ore (like silica and alumina) combine with flux (like limestone) to form slag. The slag is less dense than pig iron, which leads to it occupying more volume for the same weight. Typically, for every ton of pig iron produced, approximately 3 tons of blast furnace slag are generated, meaning the volume of slag is about three times that of the hot metal. Option A: 1.5 This option is incorrect . The volume of slag is far more than 1.5 times the volume of hot metal. The ratio is much higher. Option C: 5 This option is incorrect . The volume of slag is not five times that of the hot metal. A ratio of 3:1 is more common and realistic for most blast furnace operations. Option D: 7 This option is incorrect . Although the volume of slag can vary based on the specific process and furnace conditions, the typical ratio is about 3:1, not 7:1. Therefore, the correct answer is Option B: 3 , which is the typical volume ratio of blast furnace slag to pig iron."

    question: If a heat transfer surface is pock-marked with a number of cavities, then as compared to a smooth surface the heat transfer by __________ will not increase.
    options:
      - Convection
      - Nucleate boiling
      - Radiation
      - Conduction
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Radiation: The heat transfer by radiation is independent of surface texture (like cavities or pock-marks) and relies on the emissivity and temperature of the surface. Hence, the heat transfer by radiation does not increase due to the presence of cavities. Convection: Cavities on a heat transfer surface can enhance convection by disturbing the boundary layer, promoting turbulence and fluid mixing. This increases the rate of convective heat transfer. Nucleate Boiling: Cavities act as nucleation sites, facilitating bubble formation and improving heat transfer during boiling. Conduction: Surface irregularities could affect conduction slightly but are less significant in comparison to the other modes of heat transfer. Thus, Option C: Radiation is the correct answer because it remains unaffected, while other modes of heat transfer may increase with a pock-marked surface."

    question: During sensible cooling of air, its wet bulb temperature
    options:
      - Decreases but dew point remains constant
      - Increases but dew point remains constant
      - Increases & the dew point decreases
      - Decreases & the dew point increases
    answer: 0
    explanation: "The correct answer is A: Decreases but dew point remains constant. Let's break down why: Sensible cooling means you're only changing the temperature of the air, not the amount of moisture in it. Think of it like this: you're cooling the air, but you're not taking any water out. Wet bulb temperature is the temperature a parcel of air would have if cooled to saturation (100% relative humidity) by the evaporation of water into it, with the latent heat being supplied by the parcel. When you cool the air *without* changing the amount of water vapor present, the wet-bulb temperature will also decrease . The dew point is the temperature to which air must be cooled to become saturated with water vapor. Since the amount of moisture in the air is constant during sensible cooling, the dew point remains constant . Therefore, wet bulb temperature decreases and dew point remains constant ."

    question: A highly elastic material is deformed least on loading and retains its original form on removal of the load. Which of the following is the most elastic material?
    options:
      - Steel
      - Glass
      - Rubber
      - Brass
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Rubber is the most elastic material among the options provided. A highly elastic material deforms the least on loading and retains its original form on removal of the load. Rubber exhibits high elasticity due to its molecular structure, which allows it to undergo significant deformation under stress and return to its original shape once the stress is removed. Steel is a strong material but not as elastic as rubber. It exhibits more plastic deformation under stress, meaning it retains some of the deformed shape even after the load is removed. Glass is brittle and exhibits minimal elasticity. It fractures easily under stress and does not return to its original shape once deformed. Brass is a metal alloy that is ductile but not as elastic as rubber. It deforms plastically under stress and does not regain its original shape completely upon load removal. Therefore, Option C: Rubber is the most elastic material among the given options."

A commonly used estimation is that the height of a blast furnace is approximately 10 to 30 times its useful volume.
