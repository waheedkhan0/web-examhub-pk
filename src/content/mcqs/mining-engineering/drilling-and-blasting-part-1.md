    question: The main purpose of self rescuer is to enable the wearer to escape through an atmosphere resulting after a . . . . . . . . or after an . . . . . . . . in a mine.
    options:
      - Fire, Explosion
      - Gas, Outburt
      - Rockburst, bump
      - None
    answer: 0
    explanation: "A self-rescuer is a personal protective device used in underground mines to protect miners from toxic gases , especially carbon monoxide (CO) , during emergencies. The main purpose of a self-rescuer is to enable the wearer to safely escape from an area affected by hazardous atmospheres resulting from a fire or an explosion . These incidents commonly release carbon monoxide and other harmful gases, which are life-threatening without respiratory protection. Self-rescuers function by either filtering toxic gases (chemical-based) or providing a small oxygen supply (oxygen-based) for short durations, allowing enough time to evacuate. Therefore, the correct answer is: Fire, Explosion"
