    question: The standard method of sampling for collection of samples in advancing development faces . . . . . . . .
    options:
      - Chip sampling
      - Channel
      - Bulk
      - Radio metre
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Chip sampling is the standard method of sampling for the collection of samples in advancing development faces in metal mining. In chip sampling, small chips or rock fragments are collected from the exposed face of the development area. This method allows for the collection of representative samples that can be analyzed to assess the quality and composition of the orebody as mining progresses. Channel sampling involves cutting a channel or groove into the exposed rock face to collect a continuous sample along the length of the channel. While channel sampling is also commonly used in mining exploration and development, chip sampling is typically preferred for advancing development faces. Bulk sampling involves collecting large, representative samples of ore or waste material from a specific area for detailed analysis. This method is usually employed for assessing the overall grade and characteristics of a deposit. Radio meter refers to a device used for measuring radioactivity and is not directly related to the sampling method for advancing development faces in metal mining. Therefore, Option A: Chip sampling is the correct method for collecting samples in advancing development faces in metal mining."

    question: Cut and fill stoping follows.
    options:
      - Under hand method
      - Over hand method
      - Both A and B
      - Nil
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Option A: Under hand method This option suggests that cut and fill stoping follows the underhand method. This statement is incorrect. The underhand method involves mining downward, which is not typically associated with the cut and fill stoping method. Option B: Over hand method This option suggests that cut and fill stoping follows the overhand method. This statement is correct. The overhand method involves mining upward, which is consistent with the cut and fill stoping technique where material is mined in horizontal slices starting from the bottom and moving upwards. Option C: Both A and B This option suggests that cut and fill stoping follows both the underhand and overhand methods. This statement is partially correct. While cut and fill stoping is predominantly associated with the overhand method, there are situations where it can be adapted to an underhand approach, although this is less common. Option D: Nil This option suggests that cut and fill stoping does not follow either the underhand or overhand methods. This statement is incorrect, as cut and fill stoping is a recognized mining method that primarily follows the overhand method. Conclusion: The correct answer is Option B: Over hand method because cut and fill stoping predominantly follows the overhand method, where mining progresses upwards in horizontal slices, making it the most suitable and commonly used method for this technique."
