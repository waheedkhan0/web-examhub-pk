We need to find the scale of the drawing, which tells us how many meters are represented by each millimeter on the paper. Finding the Scale: First, let's find the area of the real-life plot: Area = length × width = 200m × 400m = 80,000 m² Now, let's compare the area on the paper to the real-life area: The paper area is 800 mm², and the real area is 80,000 m². To find the scale, we need to figure out how many square meters are represented by one square millimeter on the paper. We can do this by dividing the real area by the paper area: 80,000 m² / 800 mm² = 100 m²/mm² This means that 1 mm² on the paper represents 100 m² in real life. To find the linear scale (how many meters per millimeter), we need to take the square root of this value: √100 m²/mm² = 10 m/mm Therefore, the scale of the drawing is 1 mm = 10 m Correct Option: The correct answer is Option A: 1mm = 10m"

    question: Hypotenusal allowance for a chain of 100 links measuring along a slope of 45° is . . . . . . . .
    options:
      - + 29.3 links
      - "- 29.3 links"
      - "- 4.14 links"
      - + 4.14 links
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Hypotenusal Allowance Explained: Imagine you're measuring a distance on a hill. A chain (used for measuring in surveying) measures accurately only on flat ground. If you measure along a slope, you'll measure a longer distance than the actual horizontal distance. Hypotenusal allowance corrects for this extra distance. It's the amount you need to subtract from your slope measurement to get the true horizontal distance. Think of a right-angled triangle: The slope distance is the hypotenuse. The horizontal distance is one shorter side. The angle is 45° in this question. Calculating Hypotenusal Allowance: Since the slope is 45°, the horizontal distance is less than the slope distance. The chain measures along the hypotenuse (slope), so we need to subtract (a negative allowance) to get the horizontal distance. The calculation involves trigonometry (which you'll learn later), but for now, just understand the concept. Therefore, the correct answer is the negative allowance. The calculation gives a value close to 29.3 links. Considering the other options, it becomes clear that you need to subtract this amount."
