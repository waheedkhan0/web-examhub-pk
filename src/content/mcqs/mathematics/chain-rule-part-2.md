    question: 21 binders can bind 1400 books in 15 days. How many binders will required to bind 800 books in 20 days ?
    options:

    answer: 1
    explanation: Let the required number of binders be x Less books, Less binders (Direct proportion) More days, Less binders (Indirect proportion) \[\left. \begin{gathered} {\text{Books 1400}}:800 \hfill \\ \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,{\text{Days 20}}:15 \hfill \\ \end{gathered} \right\}::21:x\] $$\eqalign{ & \therefore {\text{ }}1400 \times 20 \times x = 800 \times 15 \times 21 \cr & \Leftrightarrow x = \frac{{\left( {800 \times 15 \times 21} \right)}}{{\left( {1400 \times 20} \right)}} \cr & \Leftrightarrow x = 9 \cr} $$
