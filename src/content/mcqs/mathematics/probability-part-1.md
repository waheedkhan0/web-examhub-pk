A prime number is a number greater than 1 that is only divisible by 1 and itself (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 7, 11...). Finding the Possible Outcomes: First, let's figure out all the possible sums we can get when we add the numbers on two dice. The minimum sum is 2 (1+1) and the maximum is 12 (6+6).
