    options:
      - 44 cm²

      - 308 cm²

    answer: 1
    explanation: Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7² = 22 × 7 = 154 cm². Geometry topic from MDCAT syllabus. Matches CSS 2020 Math paper Q3.

  - id: 2
    question: "The sum of the interior angles of a hexagon is:"
    options:
      - 540°
      - 720°
      - 900°
      - 1080°
    answer: 1
    explanation: The sum of interior angles of a polygon with 'n' sides is given by (n-2)*180°. For a hexagon, n=6. (6-2)*180° = 4 * 180° = 720°.
    keyConcept: Geometry & Mensuration
  - id: 3
    question: "In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite to the right angle is called:"
    options:
      - Base
      - Perpendicular
      - Altitude
      - Hypotenuse
    answer: 3
    explanation: The hypotenuse is the longest side of a right-angled triangle and is always opposite the 90-degree angle.
    keyConcept: Geometry & Mensuration
  - id: 4
    question: "An angle greater than 180° but less than 360° is called:"
    options:
      - Acute angle
      - Obtuse angle
      - Reflex angle
      - Right angle
    answer: 2
    explanation: An acute angle is <90°, an obtuse angle is >90° and <180°, a right angle is exactly 90°, and a reflex angle is >180° and <360°.
    keyConcept: Geometry & Mensuration

    question: "The measure of the angle inscribed in a semicircle is:"

      - 45°

      - 90°
      - 180°
    answer: 2
    explanation: "This is a theorem in geometry: An angle inscribed in a semicircle (an angle whose vertex is on the circle and whose sides pass through the endpoints of a diameter) is always a right angle (90°)."
