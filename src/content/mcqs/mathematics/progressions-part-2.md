    question: A piece of equipment cost a certain factory 6,00,000. If it depreciates in value, 15% the first year, 13.5% the next year, 12% the third year, and so on, what will be its value at the end of 10 years, all percentages applying to the original cost?
    options:
      - Rs. 2,00,000
      - Rs. 1,05,000
      - Rs. 4,05,000
      - Rs. 6,50,000
    answer: 1
    explanation: Let the cost of an equipment is Rs. 100 Now the percentages of depreciation at the end of 1st, 2 nd , 3 rd years are 15, 13.5, 12, which are in A.P., with a = 15 and d = - 1.5 Hence, percentage of depreciation in the tenth year = a + (10 - 1) d = 15 + 9 (-1.5) = 1.5 Also total value depreciated in 10 years = 15 + 13.5 + 12 + ..... + 1.5 = 82.5 Hence, the value of equipment at the end of 10 years = 100 - 82.5 = 17.5 The total cost being $$\eqalign{ & = {\text{Rs}}{\text{.}}\,\frac{{6,00,000}}{{100}} \times 17.5 \cr & = {\text{Rs}}{\text{.}}\,1,05,000 \cr} $$
