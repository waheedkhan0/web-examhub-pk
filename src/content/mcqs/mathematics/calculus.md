    question: "The derivative of x² with respect to x is:"
    options:

      - "2"

    answer: 1
    explanation: Using the power rule of differentiation, d/dx (x^n) = n*x^(n-1). Therefore, d/dx (x²) = 2*x^(1) = 2x.
