P ($${{E_2}}$$) + P ($${\overline E _1}$$) . P ($${\overline E _2}$$) $$\eqalign{ & = \left( {\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{7}{{10}}} \right) + \left( {\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{3}{{10}}} \right) \cr & = \frac{{27}}{{50}} \cr & = 0.54 \cr} $$
