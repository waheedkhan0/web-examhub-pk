    options:
      - "20"
      - "21"
      - "28"
      - "32"
    answer: 2
    explanation: Let Ajit's average be x for 9 innings. So, Ajit scored 9x run in 9 innings. In the 10 th inning, he scored 100 runs then average became (x+8). And he scored (x + 8) × 10 runs in 10 innings. Now, $$\eqalign{ & \Rightarrow 9x + 100 = 10 \times \left( {x + 8} \right) \cr & {\text{or}},\,9x + 100 = 10x + 80 \cr & {\text{or}},\,x = 100 - 80 \cr & {\text{or}},\,x = 20 \cr & {\text{New}}\,{\text{average}} = \left( {x + 8} \right) \cr & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, = 28\,{\text{runs}} \cr} $$
