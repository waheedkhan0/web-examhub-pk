    question: Which of the following is a prime number?
    options:
      - "1"
      - "2"
      - "4"
      - "9"
    answer: 1
    explanation: A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 with no positive divisors other than 1 and itself. 2 is the smallest and only even prime number.
    keyConcept: Arithmetic & Number System
  - id: 2
    question: "The sum of the first 5 natural numbers is:"
    options:
      - "10"
      - "15"
      - "20"
      - "25"
    answer: 1
    explanation: The sum of the first n natural numbers is given by n(n+1)/2. For n=5, the sum is (5*6)/2 = 30/2 = 15.
    keyConcept: Arithmetic & Number System
  - id: 3
    question: Which of the following is an irrational number?
    options:
      - √4
      - 22/7
      - π
      - 0.333...
    answer: 2
    explanation: Irrational numbers cannot be expressed as a simple fraction (p/q). π (pi) is a classic example, as its decimal representation is non-repeating and non-terminating.
