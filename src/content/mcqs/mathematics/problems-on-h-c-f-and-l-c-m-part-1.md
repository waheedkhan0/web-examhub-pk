Key Concept: Finding N If a number 'N' leaves the same remainder 'R' when dividing numbers 'a', 'b', and 'c', then (a-b), (b-c), and (c-a) are perfectly divisible by N. In other words, 'N' is the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of the differences between the numbers. Steps to Solve: 1. Calculate the differences: * 4665 - 1305 = 3360 * 6905 - 4665 = 2240 * 6905 - 1305 = 5600 2. Find the HCF of these differences (3360, 2240, and 5600): The HCF of these numbers is 1120. Therefore, N = 1120 3. Calculate the sum of the digits of N: * 1 + 1 + 2 + 0 = 4 Conclusion: The sum of the digits in N is 4. Therefore, the correct option is A: 4"
