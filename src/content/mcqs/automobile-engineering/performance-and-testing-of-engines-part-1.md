    question: Engine cranks do no start during winter because of 1. Battery discharge 2. Weak spark 3. Worn out engine 4. Lean mixture
    options:
      - 1, 2 and 3 are correct
      - 2, 3 and 4 are correct
      - 3, 4 and 1 are correct
      - 4, 1 and 2 are correct
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Engine cranks may not start during winter due to several factors that affect engine performance in cold conditions. Battery Discharge: Cold weather can lead to the discharge of the battery, making it difficult for the engine to crank properly as the battery loses power more quickly in lower temperatures. Weak Spark: A weak spark occurs when the spark plugs fail to generate enough energy to ignite the fuel mixture in cold conditions, preventing the engine from starting. Lean Mixture: In cold weather, the air-fuel mixture may become lean (more air, less fuel), which makes it difficult for the engine to start since more fuel is required to ignite in cold conditions. Thus, the correct answer is 4, 1 and 2 are correct ."
