    question: The ratio of section width to section height is known as the aspect ratio-
    options:
      - "False"
      - "True"
      - Both
      - None
    answer: 1
    explanation: "The statement \"The ratio of section width to section height is known as the aspect ratio\" is True . In the context of automotive wheels and tires, the aspect ratio is indeed the ratio of the tire's section width to its section height. The section width of a tire refers to the width of the tire's cross-section (from sidewall to sidewall) in millimeters. The section height of a tire is the height of the tire's cross-section (from the rim to the outer tread) in millimeters. The aspect ratio is calculated as: $$\\text{Aspect Ratio} = \\frac{\\text{Section Width}}{\\text{Section Height}}$$ It's an important parameter that affects the tire's performance and characteristics, such as sidewall height and ride comfort. So, the statement is True ."

    question: If the centre line of the rim is located inside of the fixing face of the disc, then it is a
    options:
      - Zeroset wheel
      - Inset wheel
      - Outset wheel
      - None of these
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Imagine a wheel lying flat on the ground. The fixing face is the part of the wheel that touches the car's hub (where the wheel bolts on). The center line of the rim is an imaginary line that runs right down the middle of the wheel's width. Now, the question asks: What happens if that center line is *inside* the fixing face? Think of it like this: the wheel's center is \"pushed in\" relative to where it mounts on the car. * Inset wheel: An inset wheel means the wheel's mounting surface (fixing face) is located *outward* further than the wheel's centerline. Therefore, the wheel's centerline lies *inside* the fixing face. This is your answer! * Outset wheel: An outset wheel is the opposite. The wheel's mounting surface is closer to the centerline of the vehicle, meaning the wheel will stick out from the car further. * Zeroset wheel: A zeroset wheel is where the mounting surface is exactly in line with the centerline of the wheel. Therefore, the correct answer is Inset wheel (Option B) ."

    question: Of the four tyres on a car, the one that wears most is the
    options:
      - Right front tyre
      - Right rear tyre
      - Left front tyre
      - Left rear tyre
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Option A: Right front tyre This option suggests that the right front tire wears the most on a car. This statement is correct for most countries where cars are driven on the right side of the road. The right front tire is subjected to additional wear due to factors such as increased load during turning and braking, as well as alignment issues. Option B: Right rear tyre This option suggests that the right rear tire wears the most on a car. This statement is generally incorrect. The rear tires tend to wear more evenly compared to the front tires, as they are subjected to less steering and braking forces. Option C: Left front tyre This option suggests that the left front tire wears the most on a car. This statement is incorrect for most countries where cars are driven on the right side of the road. The left front tire does not experience the same level of stress and load as the right front tire. Option D: Left rear tyre This option suggests that the left rear tire wears the most on a car. This statement is incorrect. The rear tires, including the left rear tire, usually wear more evenly and are less prone to the forces affecting the front tires. Conclusion: The correct answer is Option A: Right front tyre because in most driving conditions, especially in countries where cars drive on the right side of the road, the right front tire is subjected to more wear due to increased load during turning, braking, and alignment issues."
