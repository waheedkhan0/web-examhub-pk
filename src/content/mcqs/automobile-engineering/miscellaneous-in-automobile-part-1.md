    question: What is concluded in section 112 of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988?
    options:
      - Shall not drive after consuming alcohol
      - Shall not use vehicle on road without paying tax
      - Speed limit shall not be exceeded
      - Shall not use vehicle if Headlight is broken
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Section 112 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 deals with the regulation of speed. It clearly states that no person shall drive a motor vehicle at a speed exceeding the maximum speed limit fixed for that class of vehicle by the Central Government or the State Government. Option A: Shall not drive after consuming alcohol This is covered under Section 185 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, which prohibits driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Hence, this is not the correct option. Option B: Shall not use vehicle on road without paying tax This provision is addressed under Motor Vehicle Taxation Acts at the state level, not Section 112. Therefore, this option is incorrect. Option C: Speed limit shall not be exceeded This is the correct answer. Section 112 specifically states that the driver must not exceed the prescribed speed limit as per rules and notifications issued. Option D: Shall not use vehicle if Headlight is broken This condition relates to fitness of the vehicle and safety requirements under other sections and rules of the Act, not Section 112. Conclusion: Section 112 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, emphasizes speed regulation , making the correct answer Option C: Speed limit shall not be exceeded."

    question: The registration of motor vehicles is indicated in which of the following chapter-
    options:
      - I
      - II
      - III
      - IV
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Registration of Motor Vehicles is governed by Chapter IV of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. This chapter lays down the provisions regarding the necessity of registration, the procedure for registration, the validity, renewal, and cancellation of registration certificates. Option A: Chapter I Chapter I deals with the short title, extent, commencement and definitions . It introduces the Act and provides meanings of important terms. It does not cover registration of vehicles. Option B: Chapter II Chapter II is related to the licensing of drivers of motor vehicles . It prescribes rules for issuing, renewing, and cancelling driving licences. Hence, not correct for vehicle registration. Option C: Chapter III Chapter III deals with the licensing of conductors for stage carriages. It provides provisions for issuing and regulating conductor licences, not motor vehicle registration. Option D: Chapter IV This is the correct option. Chapter IV clearly specifies the rules and procedures for the registration of motor vehicles . It ensures that no motor vehicle is driven in a public place without proper registration. Conclusion: The registration of motor vehicles is provided under Chapter IV of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. Hence, the correct answer is Option D."
