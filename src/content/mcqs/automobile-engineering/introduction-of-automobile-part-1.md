    question: A commercial vehicle has two steered axles and two driving axles. This is called a
    options:
      - 4 × 2
      - 2 × 4
      - 8 × 4
      - 4 × 8
    answer: 2
    explanation: "A commercial vehicle with two steered axles and two driving axles is called an 8 × 4 vehicle. In the 8 × 4 configuration, the first number (8) represents the total number of wheels, and the second number (4) tells us how many of those wheels are powered by the engine (driven wheels). So, in this case, the vehicle has 8 wheels in total, with 4 of them being driven by the engine (helping the vehicle move), and the other 4 are used for steering (helping to turn the vehicle). Why the other options are incorrect: Option A: 4 × 2 means the vehicle has 4 wheels, with 2 of them being driven, and the other 2 being non-driven. This is not what we’re talking about here. Option B: 2 × 4 means 2 wheels are driven and 4 are steered, which doesn’t match the description. Option D: 4 × 8 means the vehicle has 4 wheels being driven and 8 steered, which is also incorrect for this scenario. Therefore, the correct answer is Option C: 8 × 4."

    question: The adjustement for backlash in a differential is provided between
    options:
      - Crown wheel and the sun gear
      - Sun gear and the planet gear
      - Crown wheel and the drive pinion
      - Crown wheel and the planet gear
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Option A: Crown wheel and the sun gear The sun (side) gears sit inside the differential case and mesh with the planet (pinion) gears to allow left–right speed difference. Any clearance here is inherent to design/manufacturing and is not where final-drive backlash is set. Therefore, backlash is not adjusted between the crown wheel and sun gear. Option B: Sun gear and the planet gear These gears handle the differential action only. Their small clearances ensure smooth relative motion between the two axle shafts but are not adjusted as “backlash” for the final drive. No shimming or carrier movement is performed here for backlash adjustment, so this is not the correct location. Option C: Crown wheel and the drive pinion The crown wheel (ring gear) meshes directly with the drive pinion to form the final-drive gearset. The backlash is intentionally adjusted at this mesh to provide a precise tooth clearance for lubrication, thermal expansion, quiet operation, and tooth life. Mechanics set this by shifting the ring gear laterally via carrier side shims/adjusters and by setting pinion depth and bearing preload. This is the standard and correct adjustment point in differentials. Option D: Crown wheel and the planet gear The crown wheel is bolted to and drives the differential case; it does not directly mesh with the planet (pinion) gears. The planet gears mesh with the sun gears inside the case. Since there is no crown wheel–planet gear mesh, there is no backlash adjustment here. Conclusion: The adjustment for backlash in a differential is provided between the crown wheel and the drive pinion — Option C ."

    question: In commercial vehicle layouts engine is located forward, rear or under floor mainly to
    options:
      - Better utilization of space
      - Increase fuel economy
      - Better weight distribution
      - Reduce the weight of chassis
    answer: 2
    explanation: The correct answer is option C. Better weight distribution . In commercial vehicle layouts, the positioning of the engine forward, rear, or under the floor is mainly done to achieve better weight distribution. Weight distribution plays a crucial role in the overall performance and stability of a commercial vehicle. When the engine is located forward, it helps distribute the weight more evenly between the front and rear axles. This improves the balance of the vehicle and enhances its handling and stability, especially during cornering and braking. Alternatively, in some commercial vehicles, the engine may be located at the rear or under the floor. This configuration also helps in achieving better weight distribution by placing a significant portion of the vehicle's weight over the rear or central axles. This can provide better traction, especially in vehicles designed for off-road or heavy-duty applications. The other options mentioned in the question, such as better utilization of space, increase fuel economy, and reduce the weight of the chassis, are also important considerations in commercial vehicle design but may not be the primary reasons for determining the engine location. Hence, the correct answer is option C. Better weight distribution .

    question: The push rod is located between the
    options:
      - Valve and valve spring
      - Valve and rocker ram
      - Tappet and rocker ram
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: "The push rod is an essential component in an internal combustion engine, especially in overhead valve (OHV) engines . It plays a crucial role in the valve actuation mechanism . Function of Push Rod: - The push rod transmits motion from the tappet (or lifter) to the rocker arm . - It helps in opening and closing the engine valves by transferring the camshaft motion. - It ensures proper valve timing and smooth engine operation. Analysis of Options: - Option A: Valve and valve spring → Incorrect , as the push rod is not directly connected to the valve spring. - Option B: Valve and rocker ram → Incorrect , because the push rod is positioned between the tappet and rocker arm , not the valve. - Option C: Tappet and rocker ram → Correct , as the push rod connects the tappet (lifter) to the rocker arm . - Option D: None of these → Incorrect , as Option C provides the correct answer. Conclusion: The correct answer is Option C: Tappet and rocker ram , as the push rod transmits motion between these two components in an engine's valve mechanism."
