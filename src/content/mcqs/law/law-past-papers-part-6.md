    question: The principle “Actus reus non facit reum nisi mens sit rea” means?
    options:
      - An act does not make a person guilty unless there is a guilty mind
      - An act alone is enough for guilt
      - Only intention matters
      - None of these
    answer: 0
    explanation: It is a fundamental principle of criminal law requiring both a wrongful act (actus reus) and a guilty intention (mens rea) for liability. Without mens rea , mere acts usually do not constitute a crime .

    question: Hyde’s Case is an example of which rule of statutory interpretation?
    options:
      - Literal Rule
      - Golden Rule
      - Mischief Rule
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: In Hyde’s Case (1584), the court interpreted the statute to suppress the “ mischief ” Parliament intended to prevent. The Mischief Rule focuses on the problem or gap the law was meant to remedy, not just the literal words.
