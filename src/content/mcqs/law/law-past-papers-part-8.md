  - id: 5
    question: Is it true that Article 70 of the Qanoon-e-Shahadat Order 1984, provides that all facts may be proved by oral evidence?
    options:
      - "Yes"
      - No, it is provided by Article 71
      - No, all facts, except the contents of documents, may be proved by oral evidence
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: Oral evidence is generally admissible for facts in a case, but not for the contents of a document. The contents of a document must be proven by primary (the original document itself) or, in specific circumstances, secondary evidence. Article 70 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984 , explicitly states this exclusion. It says, "All facts, except the contents of documents, may be proved by oral evidence". The document is the best evidence of its own contents . This is a fundamental principle of evidence law. Oral testimony about what a document says is not considered reliable or admissible , as documents speak for themselves.

    question: Whoever causes a hurt on the leg of a person leading to exposing of bone, which leaves a permanent mark of the wound, but in which the injury does not extend to the body cavity of the trunk would, in addition to any imprisonment as ta'zir, shall also be liable to _____?
    options:
      - Arsh
      - Diyat
      - Daman
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: Daman is the compensation determined by the court for causing a hurt that is not liable to Arsh . The compensation is fixed based on the victim's treatment expenses , any disability caused, and the anguish suffered. The scenario described in the question— a hurt exposing bone, leaving a permanent mark , but not entering the body cavity—is considered a Ghayr-i-Jaifah (hurt not amounting to jaifah). The specific type of injury is Mudihah , which is the exposure of a bone without a fracture. Under Section 337-F of the PPC , the punishment for causing Mudihah is Daman , in addition to imprisonment as ta'zir.
