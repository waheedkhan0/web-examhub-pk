    question: Which of the following is not a true statement regarding minor's admission to partnership in a firm?
    options:
      - A minor may be admitted to the benefits of partnership with the consent of majority of partners
      - A minor may be admitted to the benefits of partnership with the consent of all the partners
      - A minor is not personally liable for any act of the firm
      - A minor has a right to such profits as agreed upon
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Option A: A minor may be admitted to the benefits of partnership with the consent of majority of partners Explanation in HTML format: According to the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 , a minor cannot become a full partner in a firm, but can be admitted to the benefits of partnership . Section 30 of the Act states that a minor may be admitted to the benefits of partnership only with the consent of all the partners , not just the majority. Hence, Option A is incorrect because it wrongly states that majority consent is sufficient. Option B is correct because unanimous consent is required. Option C is correct because a minor is not personally liable for the debts of the firm. Option D is also correct as the minor is entitled to receive only the share of profits as agreed upon. Therefore, the correct answer is Option A."
