    question: On whom does the burden of proof lie in a criminal case?
    options:
      - Prosecution
      - Witness
      - Accused
      - Judge
    answer: 0
    explanation: The prosecution bears the primary burden of proving the accused’s guilt in a criminal case. Under Article 117 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat Order (QSO), 1984 , the person who asks the court to give judgment based on asserted facts must prove those facts. The prosecution generally must prove the charge beyond a reasonable doubt. The accused is presumed innocent unless proven guilty, subject to applicable legal exceptions.
