    question: Under Section 3 of the Federal Investigation Agency Act 1974, who is empowered to constitute the Agency?
    options:
      - President of Pakistan
      - Federal Government
      - Supreme Court of Pakistan
      - Ministry of Law and Justice
    answer: 1
    explanation: Under Section 3(1) of the FIA Act 1974, the Federal Government may constitute an agency to be called the Federal Investigation Agency for investigation of offenses specified in the Schedule.
    keyConcept: FIA Act 1974 Section 3 - Constitution of the Agency
    examTip: In FPSC FIA tests, note that powers are vested in the 'Federal Government', not the President or individual minister.
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Assistant Director FIA 2022
      - FPSC Inspector FIA 2023

  - id: 2
    question: "According to Section 4 of the FIA Act 1974, the superintendence of the Agency shall vest in:"
    options:
      - The Supreme Court
      - The Federal Government
      - The Director General FIA
      - The Minister of State for Interior
    answer: 1
    explanation: Section 4(1) of the FIA Act 1974 states that the superintendence of the Agency shall vest in the Federal Government. The administration vests in the Director General.
    keyConcept: FIA Act 1974 Section 4 - Superintendence and Administration
    examTip: Differentiate between 'Superintendence' (Federal Government) and 'Administration' (Director General).
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Inspector FIA 2021
      - FPSC AD FIA 2024

      - Prime Minister
    answer: 1
    explanation: Under Section 4(2) of the FIA Act 1974, the administration of the Agency shall vest in the Director General appointed by the Federal Government.
    keyConcept: Administration under Section 4(2)

      - FPSC Inspector FIA 2022

  - id: 4
    question: "Under Section 5(1) of the FIA Act 1974, members of the Agency have throughout Pakistan all the powers which:"
    options:
      - Military police officers have
      - Provincial police officers have under the Police Order 2002 / CrPC
      - Magistrates 1st Class enjoy
      - Custom officials hold under the Customs Act 1969
    answer: 1
    explanation: Under Section 5(1), members of the Agency shall have throughout Pakistan, in relation to the investigation of offenses specified in the Schedule, all the powers including powers relating to search, arrest of persons and seizure of property which Provincial Police Officers have under the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC).
    keyConcept: Powers of Members of Agency under Section 5
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Assistant Director FIA 2023

    question: Which officer of the FIA is empowered by Section 5(2) to exercise the powers of an Officer-in-Charge of a Police Station?
    options:
      - Only the Director General
      - Any member of the Agency not below the rank of Sub-Inspector
      - Any Constable or Head Constable
      - Only Additional Directors
    answer: 1
    explanation: Under Section 5(2) of the FIA Act 1974, any member of the Agency not below the rank of Sub-Inspector may exercise any of the powers of the officer-in-charge of a police station in the area in which he is for the time being.
    keyConcept: Station House Officer (SHO) powers in FIA
    examTip: "Must remember the threshold rank: 'not below the rank of Sub-Inspector'."
    testedIn:
