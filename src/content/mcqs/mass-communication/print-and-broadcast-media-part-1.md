    options:

      - "1824"
      - "1819"
    answer: 1
    explanation: The earliest newspaper in Kolkata, formerly known as Calcutta, started in 1780. It was called the "Hicky's Bengal Gazette" and was founded by James Augustus Hicky, an Irishman. Hicky's Bengal Gazette is considered to be the first newspaper printed in India.

    question: The first newspaper published in Malayalam language was
    options:
      - Malayala Manorama
      - Vigyan Nikeshapan
      - Deen Bandhu
      - Rajyasamacharam
    answer: 3
    explanation: "1. The first newspaper published in the Malayalam language was \"Rajyasamacharam\", launched in June 1847. 2. It was started by Hermann Gundert, a German missionary known for his significant contributions to the Malayalam language, including the first Malayalam-English dictionary. 3. The newspaper was primarily aimed at spreading news and progressive ideas among the people of Kerala. 4. The other options are incorrect: - Malayala Manorama was established much later in 1888. - Vigyan Nikeshapan is not recognized as the first Malayalam newspaper. - Deen Bandhu was another notable publication but not the first in Malayalam. Thus, the correct answer is \"Rajyasamacharam.\""
