    question: Which of the following is/are advantages of sampling?
    options:
      - Convenience and easy to use
      - Highly representative of the population
      - Highly representative of the strata
      - All of the above
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Sampling has several advantages: 1. Convenience and easy to use: Sampling requires fewer resources, time, and effort compared to studying the entire population. 2. Highly representative of the population: Properly designed and executed sampling methods can accurately reflect the characteristics of the entire population. 3. Highly representative of the strata: Stratified sampling ensures that specific subgroups or strata within the population are adequately represented, providing more precise results. Thus, all of the options are correct."
