    options:
      - Socrates
      - Bacon
      - Pythagoras
      - Heraclitus
    answer: 2
    explanation: Pythagoras was the first person to declare himself a philosopher . The term philosopher comes from the Greek word "philosophos," meaning "lover of wisdom" . Pythagoras used this term to describe himself, emphasizing his pursuit of knowledge and wisdom. Although figures like Socrates , Bacon , and Heraclitus made significant contributions to philosophy, it was Pythagoras who first called himself a philosopher.

    question: "Match the following: List-I List-II a. Heraclitus 1. Flux b. Democritus 2. Atoms c. Pythagoras 3. Number d. Anaximenes 4. Air"
    options:
      - a-4, b-3, c-2, d-1
      - a-3, b-4, c-1, d-2
      - a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1
      - a-1, b-2, c-3, d-4
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Heraclitus is associated with the concept of Flux , emphasizing the constant change and flux in the universe. Democritus is known for his theory of Atoms , proposing that everything is composed of indivisible and indestructible particles called atoms. Pythagoras is renowned for his work on Number , particularly his contributions to mathematics, including the Pythagorean theorem. Anaximenes is credited with the theory that the basic substance of the universe is Air , proposing that air is the fundamental element from which everything else is derived. Hence, the correct match is: a-1, b-2, c-3, d-4, corresponding to Heraclitus, Democritus, Pythagoras, and Anaximenes respectively."
