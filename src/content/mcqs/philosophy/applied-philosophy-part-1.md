    question: Which among the following is to be considered as the part of Medical Ethics?
    options:
      - Autonomy
      - Justice
      - Beneficence
      - All of the above
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Medical ethics refers to the moral principles and values that guide healthcare professionals in making decisions and providing patient care. Autonomy is an important principle of medical ethics that emphasizes respecting a patient’s right to make informed decisions regarding their own treatment and healthcare choices. Justice refers to fairness in medical practice, including equal treatment of patients and fair distribution of healthcare resources and services. Beneficence means healthcare professionals should act in ways that promote the well-being of patients and work for their benefit and welfare. These principles are among the core foundations of medical ethics and are widely used in healthcare decision-making and professional conduct. Therefore, since Autonomy, Justice, and Beneficence are all parts of Medical Ethics , Option D: All of the above is the correct answer."
