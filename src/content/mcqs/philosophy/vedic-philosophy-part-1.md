    question: Turiya means as
    options:
      - Deep sleep stage
      - Immortal self
      - Natural stage
      - Rational stage
    answer: 1
    explanation: "In Vedic philosophy, particularly within the Advaita Vedanta school, consciousness is described as having four states: * Waking state (Jagrat): This is our normal state when we are aware of the external world. * Dreaming state (Swapna): This is when we experience dreams. * Deep sleep state (Sushupti): This is a state of unconsciousness without dreams. * Turiya: This is the transcendental state of pure consciousness . It's beyond the other three states and represents the immortal self (Atman) , which is one with Brahman (the ultimate reality). Therefore, Turiya doesn't refer to deep sleep, a natural stage, or a rational stage, but to the ultimate, immortal self ."
