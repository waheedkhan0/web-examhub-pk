      - 12th Amendment
    answer: 0
    explanation: The 8th Amendment to the Constitution of Pakistan was passed in 1985 during the rule of General Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq . It inserted Article 58(2)(b) , which empowered the President to dissolve the National Assembly under certain circumstances. This power enabled the President to dismiss elected governments and dissolve the National Assembly . Article 58(2)(b) was later removed by the 13th Amendment in 1997 .
