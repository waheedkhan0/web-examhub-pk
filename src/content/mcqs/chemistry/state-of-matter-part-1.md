    question: Matter around us can exist in three different states, namely, solid, liquid and gas. The correct order of their compressibility is
    options:
      - Liquid < Gas < Solid
      - Solid < Liquid < Gas
      - Gas < Liquid < Solid
      - Solid< Gas < Liquid
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Let's understand this question step-by-step The question asks about \"compressibility\" for solids, liquids, and gases. What is Compressibility ? Compressibility means how much you can squeeze a substance or material into a smaller space. Imagine trying to press something down or push its particles closer together. Now, let's look at the three states of matter and how easily they can be squeezed: 1. Solids (like a stone or a piece of wood): In solids, the tiny particles (atoms or molecules) are packed very, very closely together. They are held by strong forces and have very little empty space between them. Because there's hardly any empty space to push them into, it's extremely difficult to squeeze a solid. Therefore, solids are the least compressible . 2. Liquids (like water or milk): In liquids, the particles are still quite close together, but they are not as tightly packed as in solids. They can move past each other, and there's a little bit of empty space, but not much. You can squeeze liquids a tiny, tiny bit, but not very much at all. So, liquids are more compressible than solids, but much less compressible than gases . 3. Gases (like the air we breathe or the gas in a balloon): In gases, the particles are very, very far apart from each other. They move around freely and randomly, and there is a lot of empty space between them. Because there's so much empty space, it's very easy to push these particles closer together and reduce the volume. Therefore, gases are the most compressible . So, if we put them in order from least compressible to most compressible : Solid < Liquid < Gas This order means: - Solids are less compressible than liquids. - Liquids are less compressible than gases. Looking at the options: Option B: Solid < Liquid < Gas is the correct order."

    question: The temperature at which a solid melts to become a liquid at the atmospheric pressure is called its melting point. The melting point of a solid is an indication of
    options:
      - strength of the intermolecular forces of attraction
      - strength of the intermolecular forces of repulsion
      - molecular mass
      - molecular size
    answer: 0
    explanation: Melting point is the temperature at which a solid changes into liquid at atmospheric pressure. If a solid has strong intermolecular forces of attraction , more heat energy is required to overcome these forces, therefore its melting point will be higher. If the intermolecular forces are weak , less heat energy is required, and the melting point will be lower. Thus melting point basically reflects how strongly the particles of a substance attract each other. Hence it indicates the strength of intermolecular forces of attraction .
