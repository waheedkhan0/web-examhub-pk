    question: Which one of the following colloidal systems is represented by soda water?
    options:
      - Liquid in gas
      - Gas in liquid
      - Solid in gas
      - Liquid in liquid
    answer: 1
    explanation: "The correct answer is Option B: Gas in liquid . Here's why: * A colloidal system is a mixture where one substance is dispersed evenly throughout another. * Soda water is essentially carbon dioxide gas (CO₂) dissolved in water (a liquid). * Therefore, soda water represents a gas (CO₂) dispersed in a liquid (water) . Let's look at why the other options are incorrect: * Option A: Liquid in gas This would be something like fog or mist, where tiny liquid droplets are suspended in air (a gas). * Option C: Solid in gas This would be something like smoke, where tiny solid particles are suspended in air. * Option D: Liquid in liquid This would be an emulsion, like milk, where tiny droplets of one liquid are dispersed in another liquid. So, to remember: Soda water = Gas (Carbon Dioxide) in Liquid (Water) ."
