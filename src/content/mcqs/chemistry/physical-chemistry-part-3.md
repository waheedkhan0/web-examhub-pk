    question: "The volume occupied by 2 moles of an ideal gas at STP is:"
    options:
      - 11.2 L
      - 22.4 L
      - 33.6 L
      - 44.8 L
    answer: 3
    explanation: At STP, 1 mole occupies 22.4 L. Therefore, 2 moles occupy 44.8 L.
    keyConcept: Physical Chemistry & Stoichiometry
  - id: 2
    question: "According to Avogadro's law, equal volumes of gases at same temperature and pressure contain equal numbers of:"
    options:
      - Atoms
      - Molecules
      - Electrons
      - Protons
    answer: 1
    explanation: Avogadro's law states that V ∝ n (number of moles) at constant T and P, meaning equal volumes contain equal number of molecules.
    keyConcept: Physical Chemistry & Stoichiometry
  - id: 3
    question: Which of the following is NOT an assumption of kinetic molecular theory of gases?
    options:
      - Gas molecules have negligible volume
      - Gas molecules exert attractive forces on each other
      - Collisions between molecules are perfectly elastic
      - Molecules move in random straight-line motion
    answer: 1
    explanation: Ideal gas molecules are assumed to have no intermolecular forces. Real gases deviate due to attractive forces especially at high pressure/low temperature.
    keyConcept: Physical Chemistry & Stoichiometry
  - id: 4
    question: "Graham's law of diffusion states that the rate of diffusion of a gas is inversely proportional to the square root of its:"
    options:
      - Volume
      - Pressure
      - Density
      - Temperature
    answer: 2
    explanation: Rate ∝ 1/√d (at constant T and P) or rate ∝ 1/√M, where d = density and M = molar mass.
    keyConcept: Physical Chemistry & Stoichiometry

    options:

      - Boyle temperature
      - Inversion temperature

    answer: 1
    explanation: At Boyle temperature, the effects of molecular volume and intermolecular forces cancel, making Z ≈ 1 over a range of pressures.
