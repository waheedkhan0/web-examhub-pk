  - id: 2
    question: "The IUPAC name of CH₃-O-C₂H₅ is:"
    options:
      - Ethyl methyl ether
      - Methoxy ethane
      - Ethoxy methane
      - Dimethyl ether
    answer: 1
    explanation: According to IUPAC nomenclature for ethers, the larger alkyl group is considered the parent alkane, and the smaller alkoxy group is named as a substituent. CH₃-O- is methoxy, attached to ethane (C₂H₆), so the name is methoxyethane.
    keyConcept: Organic Chemistry

    question: The reaction of an alkene with HBr in the presence of peroxide follows which mechanism?
    options:
      - SN1
      - SN2
      - Markovnikov's rule
      - Anti-Markovnikov's rule (Free radical)
    answer: 3
    explanation: In the presence of peroxides, the addition of HBr to an alkene follows a free radical mechanism that goes against Markovnikov's rule. The bromine atom adds to the less substituted carbon, resulting in anti-Markovnikov addition.
