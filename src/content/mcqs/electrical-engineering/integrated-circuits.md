    question: Which of the following is most difficult to fabricate in an IC?
    options:
      - Diode
      - Transistor
      - FET
      - Capacitor
    answer: 3
    explanation: "This question is about making parts for computer chips (Integrated Circuits or ICs). Think of it like building with LEGOs, but the LEGOs are tiny electrical components. We have four choices: diodes, transistors, FETs (a special type of transistor), and capacitors. Diodes are relatively simple to make. Transistors are a bit more complex than diodes, but still manageable in IC fabrication. FETs are a type of transistor, so they have similar complexity. Capacitors , however, are the trickiest to make small and efficiently on a chip. They need a specific structure to store electrical charge, and achieving the desired capacitance value in a tiny area is challenging. This makes them the most difficult to fabricate. Therefore, the answer is D: Capacitor."

    question: Operational amplifiers use ___________.
    options:
      - Linear ICs
      - Digital ICs
      - Both linear and digital ICs
      - None of the above
    answer: 0
    explanation: "The correct answer is Option A: Linear ICs . Here's why: * Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps) are primarily designed for analog signal processing. * Linear ICs are integrated circuits that operate in the analog domain, meaning they process continuous signals. * Op-Amps amplify, filter, and perform other operations on analog signals like voltage and current. * Digital ICs, on the other hand, deal with discrete signals (0s and 1s) and are used for digital logic and computation. * While some advanced circuits might combine analog and digital components, the core functionality of an Op-Amp relies on linear (analog) operation."
