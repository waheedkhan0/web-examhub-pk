    question: Which of the following type of materials are not very important for engineering applications?
    options:
      - Ferromagnetic
      - Paramagnetic
      - Diamagnetic
      - None of the above
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Option A: Ferromagnetic - Ferromagnetic materials (like iron) are strongly attracted to magnets. They can also become permanently magnetized. Think of things like transformers, motors, and hard drives. They're very important in electrical engineering. Option B: Paramagnetic - Paramagnetic materials are weakly attracted to magnets. This attraction disappears when the external magnetic field is removed. They have some engineering applications, but they are not as widely used as ferromagnetic material. Option C: Diamagnetic - Diamagnetic materials are actually repelled by magnets (though very weakly). This repulsion also disappears when the external magnetic field is removed. They have very limited engineering applications compared to the other types. Option D: None of the above - This suggests that all the materials are important, which is incorrect. Therefore, the correct answer is Option C: Diamagnetic . Diamagnetic materials aren't as critical in many common engineering applications compared to ferromagnetic and, to a lesser extent, paramagnetic materials."

    question: The magnetic materials exhibit the property of magnetisation because of
    options:
      - orbital motion of electrons
      - spin of electrons
      - spin of nucleus
      - either of these
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Magnetic materials exhibit the property of magnetization due to the orbital motion of electrons and the spin of electrons . The orbital motion of electrons around the nucleus generates a small magnetic field, contributing to the overall magnetization of the material. The spin of electrons also creates a magnetic moment, which plays a crucial role in determining the magnetic properties of the material. Though the spin of the nucleus can contribute to magnetism, its effect is negligible compared to electron motion and spin. Since magnetization can result from either the orbital motion of electrons or the spin of electrons , the correct answer is Option D: Either of These."

    question: Air gap has_______eluctance as compared to iron or steel path
    options:
      - little
      - lower
      - higher
      - zero
    answer: 2
    explanation: The air gap in a magnetic circuit has higher reluctance compared to the iron or steel path. Reluctance is the opposition to the flow of magnetic flux, similar to resistance in an electrical circuit. Since air has a much lower magnetic permeability than iron or steel, the reluctance of an air gap is significantly higher. In contrast, iron and steel have high permeability, allowing them to conduct magnetic flux easily with very low reluctance. Hence, the correct answer is Higher (Option C).
