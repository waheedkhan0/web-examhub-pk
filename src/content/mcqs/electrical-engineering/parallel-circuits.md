    question: Four resistors of equal value are connected in parallel. If the total voltage is 15 V and the total resistance is 600 Ω, the current through each parallel resistor is
    options:
      - 25 mA
      - 100 mA
      - 6.25 mA
      - 200 mA
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Understanding the Problem: We have a parallel circuit with four identical resistors. We know the total voltage across the parallel combination and the total equivalent resistance. We need to find the current flowing through each individual resistor . Key Concepts: In a parallel circuit , the voltage across each branch is the same . Ohm's Law : Voltage (V) = Current (I) x Resistance (R), or I = V/R Step-by-Step Solution: 1. Find the total current (I total ) : Using Ohm's Law for the entire circuit, I total = V total / R total = 15 V / 600 Ω = 0.025 A = 25 mA 2. Find the resistance of each resistor (R each ) : Since the resistors are in parallel and identical, the total resistance is related to the individual resistance by R total = R each / N (where N is the number of resistors). Therefore, R each = R total * N = 600 Ω * 4 = 2400 Ω 3. Find the current through each resistor (I each ) : Since the voltage across each resistor is the same as the total voltage (15 V), we can use Ohm's Law again: I each = V each / R each = 15 V / 2400 Ω = 0.00625 A = 6.25 mA Therefore, the correct answer is: Option C: 6.25 mA"

    question: Three 47 Ω resistors are connected in parallel across a 110 volt source. The current drawn from the source is approximately
    options:
      - 2.3 A
      - 780 mA
      - 47 mA
      - 7.06 A
    answer: 3
    explanation: "First, understand what a parallel circuit means. In a parallel circuit, all the resistors are connected side-by-side to the voltage source. This means the voltage across each resistor is the same , which in this case is 110 volts. Next, we need to find the total resistance of the parallel combination. Since we have three identical resistors (47 Ω each) in parallel, we can use a simplified formula: Total Resistance (R total ) = Resistance of one resistor / Number of resistors. So, R total = 47 Ω / 3 ≈ 15.67 Ω. The total resistance is less than the resistance of any single resistor when they are in parallel. Now, we can use Ohm's Law to find the total current drawn from the source. Ohm's Law states: Current (I) = Voltage (V) / Resistance (R). Therefore, I = 110 V / 15.67 Ω ≈ 7.02 A. Looking at the options, 7.06 A (Option D) is the closest answer to our calculated value. Therefore, option D is the correct answer. Therefore, Option D: 7.06 A is the correct answer."
