    question: The power output of an audio system is 18 W. For a person to notice an increase in the output (loudness or sound intensity) of the system, what must the output power be increased to ?
    options:
      - 2 W
      - 6 W
      - 68 W
      - None of the above
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Correct Answer: Option C — 68 W Explanation: Human hearing perceives loudness on a logarithmic scale . Therefore, a noticeable increase in sound output requires a significant increase in the actual power of the audio system. The change in sound power level is calculated using: Change in dB = 10 × log₁₀(P₂/P₁) Given: Initial power, P₁ = 18 W New power, P₂ = 68 W Therefore: Change in dB = 10 × log₁₀(68/18) = 10 × log₁₀(3.78) ≈ 10 × 0.577 ≈ 5.77 dB A 3 dB increase corresponds to approximately a doubling of sound power . Since increasing the power from 18 W to 68 W produces an increase of approximately 5.77 dB , the change in output would be clearly noticeable. Option A — 2 W: This is a decrease in power, so the sound output would decrease. Option B — 6 W: This is also a decrease in power, so the sound would become quieter. Option C — 68 W: This increases the power from 18 W to 68 W, producing approximately a 5.77 dB increase . Hence, this is the correct option. Option D — None of the above: Incorrect because 68 W satisfies the requirement in the context of the question. Remember for Exams: 3 dB increase ≈ 2× power . The relationship between power and decibels is ΔdB = 10 log₁₀(P₂/P₁) . Final Answer: Option C — 68 W"
