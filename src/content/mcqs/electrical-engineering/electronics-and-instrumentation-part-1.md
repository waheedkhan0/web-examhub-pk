    question: Piezo – electric transducers work when we apply _____________ to it.
    options:
      - Mechanical force
      - Vibrations
      - Illuminations
      - Heat
    answer: 0
    explanation: Piezo-electric transducers operate based on the piezoelectric effect, which is the ability of certain materials to generate an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress . When a mechanical force is applied to a piezoelectric material, it produces a proportional voltage across its surfaces. This property is widely used in sensors, actuators, and measurement instruments . Option B (Vibrations) is incorrect because while vibrations may cause mechanical stress, it is the force behind the vibration that activates the piezoelectric effect. Option C (Illuminations) and Option D (Heat) are unrelated to the working principle of piezoelectric transducers. Therefore, the correct answer is Option A.

    question: "S1: Transducer is a device which converts physical into electrical quantity S2: Transducer is also called as sensor."
    options:
      - S1 is true & S2 is false
      - S2 is true & S1 is false
      - Both S1 & S2 are true
      - Both S1 & S2 are false
    answer: 2
    explanation: Statement 1 (S1) is true because a transducer is a device that converts a physical quantity (such as temperature, pressure, or light) into an electrical quantity (such as voltage or current). This conversion is essential for measurement and control systems in electronics and instrumentation. Statement 2 (S2) is also true because a transducer is commonly referred to as a sensor . Sensors detect changes in physical conditions and convert them into signals that can be measured and analyzed. Therefore, both statements accurately describe the nature and function of transducers.
