    question: The insulation between windings and grounded core and the insulation between the windings of the same phase is
    options:
      - minor insulation
      - major insulation
      - either of the above
      - none of the above
    answer: 1
    explanation: The insulation between windings and the grounded core, as well as the insulation between the windings of the same phase, is referred to as major insulation . Major insulation is designed to withstand higher voltage stresses as it separates the high-voltage components from the grounded core and ensures the safety and reliability of the electrical machine. In contrast, minor insulation is used for low voltage insulation, such as between adjacent turns in a winding, where the voltage stress is comparatively low. Therefore, Option B is the correct answer.

    question: Is universally used for windings of electrical machines because it is easily workable without any possibility of
    options:
      - fracture
      - Silver
      - Steel
      - Aluminium
      - Copper
    answer: 4
    explanation: "Copper is universally used for windings of electrical machines due to several reasons: 1. Easily workable: Copper is a highly malleable and ductile metal, making it easy to form into wires and windings without the risk of fracturing. 2. Conductivity: Copper has excellent electrical conductivity, which is crucial for efficient energy transfer and reducing losses in electrical machines. 3. Thermal properties: Copper has good thermal conductivity, allowing it to dissipate heat effectively, which is important for maintaining the temperature of electrical windings. Option A: Fracture is incorrect because copper does not fracture easily due to its ductility. Option B: Silver is incorrect because while silver is highly conductive, it is not commonly used in electrical machines due to its high cost. Option C: Steel is incorrect because steel is not as conductive as copper and is less suitable for electrical windings. Option D: Aluminium is incorrect because although aluminum is used in some applications, copper is preferred in electrical machines due to its superior conductivity and other properties. Therefore, the correct answer is Copper ."
