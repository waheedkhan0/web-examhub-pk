    question: The most important goal of business communication is_________.
    options:
      - favorable relationship between sender and receiver
      - organizational goodwill
      - receiver response
      - receiver understanding
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Option A: favorable relationship between sender and receiver While a good relationship is helpful, it's not the *main* goal. Having a friendly relationship doesn't guarantee the message was understood or acted upon. Option B: organizational goodwill Goodwill is positive for the company, but it's a result of good communication, not necessarily the primary *goal* of each individual communication. Goodwill is more of a long-term effect. Option C: receiver response Getting a response is good, but it doesn't guarantee the receiver *understood* the message. You want to know the receiver understood the message. Option D: receiver understanding This is the best answer. If the receiver *understands* the message, then the communication has been successful. Without understanding, nothing else matters. Therefore, the correct answer is Option D: receiver understanding . The primary objective of business communication is to ensure that the receiver correctly interprets and comprehends the message being conveyed."
    keyConcept: Business Communication
