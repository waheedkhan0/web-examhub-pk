    question: Training and development refers to the total structure of on-the-job and off-the-jobtraining systems used by the
    options:
      - organisation
      - experts
      - candidates
      - consultants
    answer: 0
    explanation: "The correct answer is A: organisation . Let's break it down: Training and Development: This is all about improving employees' skills and knowledge. On-the-job training: Learning while working. Off-the-job training: Learning outside of the regular work environment (like a classroom). The organisation is responsible for creating and implementing these training systems. It's the organisation that benefits from having well-trained and developed employees. Why the other options are incorrect: B: experts: Experts might *deliver* the training, but they don't create the overall training system for the whole company. C: candidates: Candidates are the *recipients* of training, not the ones who design the training system. D: consultants: Consultants might *help* design or deliver training, but the organisation owns and manages the overall training structure. Therefore, the total structure of on-the-job and off-the-job training systems is used by the organisation."
