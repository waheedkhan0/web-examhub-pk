    question: Which of the following countries is not a member of ASEAN?
    options:
      - Laos
      - Cambodia
      - Vietnam
      - North Korea
    answer: 3
    explanation: ASEAN, or the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, is a regional organization comprising countries in Southeast Asia that promotes economic, political, and security cooperation among its members. Laos, Cambodia, and Vietnam are all Southeast Asian nations and have been members of ASEAN since the 1990s. Laos joined ASEAN in 1997, Cambodia in 1999, and Vietnam in 1995, making them full-fledged members of the organization. On the other hand, North Korea is located in East Asia, not Southeast Asia, and is not a member of ASEAN. North Korea has a distinct political and diplomatic stance, often aligning itself differently on the global stage, and it does not participate in this Southeast Asian bloc.

    options:

      - India

      - China
    answer: 3
    explanation: The Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation (BIMSTEC) is a regional organization comprising of countries in the Bay of Bengal region. It aims to promote economic cooperation, cultural ties, and regional integration among its member countries. The organization was established in 1997 with the participation of seven countries. Out of the given options, China is not a member of BIMSTEC. The member countries of BIMSTEC are Bangladesh, India, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Nepal, and Bhutan. These countries actively engage in various areas of cooperation, including trade, investment, transport, energy, tourism, and counterterrorism, among others. Option D, "China," is the correct answer as it is not a member of BIMSTEC.
