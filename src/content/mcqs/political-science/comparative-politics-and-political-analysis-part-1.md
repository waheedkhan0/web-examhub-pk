    question: "Identify the correct sequence of the following input functions? 1. Political communication 2. Political socialisation and recruitment 3. Interest aggregation 4. Interest articulation Select the correct answer:"
    options:
      - 1, 2, 3, 4
      - 4, 1, 3, 2
      - 2, 4, 3, 1
      - 2, 3, 4, 1
    answer: 2
    explanation: "The correct answer is Option C: 2, 4, 3, 1 Here's why: Let's break down these input functions in a way that makes sense: 2. Political Socialisation and Recruitment: This is where it all starts . Political socialisation is how people learn about politics and form their political views (like from family, school, or media). Recruitment is how people are selected to participate in the political process (like becoming party members or running for office). Think of it as preparing the people who will be involved. 4. Interest Articulation: Now that people have their views, they need to express them. Interest articulation is about individuals or groups voicing their needs, demands, and concerns. This could be through protests, lobbying, writing letters, or talking to politicians. It's like saying what you want. 3. Interest Aggregation: All these different voices need to be organized and combined. Interest aggregation is the process of combining these individual interests into broader policy options. Political parties often play a big role here. It's like grouping similar ideas together. 1. Political Communication: Finally, all this information needs to be shared . Political communication is how information about politics is disseminated throughout society. This includes news, debates, propaganda, and any other way political information is spread. It's like telling everyone what's going on. Therefore, the logical order is: First, people learn and get involved ( Political Socialisation and Recruitment ). Then they express their opinions ( Interest Articulation ). Next, these opinions are grouped into policy options ( Interest Aggregation ). And finally, everyone is informed about these options ( Political Communication )."

    question: The Theory of Modernisation and decay is formulated by
    options:
      - Rostow
      - Pye
      - Huntington
      - Lipset
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Modernization theory generally deals with how societies develop and become \"modern\". Political decay refers to the decline of political institutions and order, often associated with instability and violence. Samuel Huntington , a prominent political scientist, linked these concepts in his book \"Political Order in Changing Societies\". He argued that rapid modernization, especially in developing countries, can outpace the development of strong political institutions. This imbalance can lead to political decay , as the existing institutions struggle to cope with new demands and pressures. Therefore, the connection between modernization and decay is most strongly associated with Huntington's theory . While the other options are important figures in political science, their work is not primarily focused on this specific theory: Rostow is famous for his stages of economic growth. Pye contributed to the study of political culture and development. Lipset explored the relationship between economic development and democracy."

    question: What are the three types of political culture according to Almond and Verba's study 'Civil Culture'?
    options:
      - Parochial, Subject, Participant
      - Citizen, Communal, Democratic
      - Associative, Adjudicative, Parochial
      - Feudal, Semi-feudal, Democratic
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Almond and Verba's study 'Civil Culture' categorizes political culture into three types: 1. Parochial: This type of political culture is characterized by limited political awareness and involvement among the populace. People in societies with a parochial political culture tend to be more focused on local affairs and are less concerned with national or international politics. 2. Subject: In a subject political culture, people have a higher level of political awareness compared to parochial cultures, but they still perceive themselves as subjects rather than active participants in the political process. Subjects may follow political developments but do not feel empowered to influence political decisions. 3. Participant: This type of political culture is characterized by active citizen engagement and participation in the political process. Participants are politically aware and feel empowered to influence political decisions through various means such as voting, activism, and involvement in civic organizations. Therefore, the correct answer is Option A: Parochial, Subject, Participant."

    question: This first use of concept of political culture was made by
    options:
      - Gabriel Almond
      - G. Bingham Powell, Jr.
      - Karl Deutseh
      - Lucian Pye
    answer: 0
    explanation: "The first use of the concept of political culture was made by Gabriel Almond , a prominent American political scientist. He introduced this concept in the late 1950s and developed it further in collaboration with Sidney Verba in their landmark book \"The Civic Culture\" (1963) . Political culture refers to the set of attitudes, beliefs, and sentiments that give order and meaning to a political process. It encompasses both the political ideals and the operating norms of a polity. Almond classified political cultures into parochial, subject, and participant , based on the level of political awareness and involvement of the citizens. This concept was central to understanding how political systems are maintained or changed and how they function in different societies. Therefore, the correct answer is Option A: Gabriel Almond ."
