    question: Which one of the following pairs not correct
    options:
      - Sampoorna Grameen Rozgar Yojana - 2001
      - Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yojana (PMGY) - 2000 - 01
      - Rural Employment Generation Programme (REGP) - 1995
      - Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) - 1999
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Option A: Sampoorna Grameen Rozgar Yojana - 2001 - This is correct . The Sampoorna Grameen Rozgar Yojana (SGRY) was indeed launched in 2001. Option B: Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yojana (PMGY) - 2000-01 - This is also correct . The Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yojana (PMGY) was launched in 2000-2001. Option C: Rural Employment Generation Programme (REGP) - 1995 - This is correct as well. The Rural Employment Generation Programme (REGP) was started in 1995. Option D: Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) - 1999 - This is incorrect . The Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) was launched in December 2000 , not 1999. Therefore, the pair that is not correct is Option D."
