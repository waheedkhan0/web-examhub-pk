Sertoli cells are a type of sustentacular "nurse" cell found in human testes that contribute to the process of spermatogenesis (the production of sperm) as a structural component of the seminiferous tubules. Sertoli cells also act as phagocytes, consuming the residual cytoplasm during spermatogenesis.
