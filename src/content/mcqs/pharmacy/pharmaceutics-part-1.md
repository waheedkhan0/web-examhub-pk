    question: Codeine phosphate ↔ Codeine + Phosphate. Identify the type of chemical reaction.
    options:
      - Oxidation
      - Ionic hydrolysis
      - Polymerisation
      - Isomerization
    answer: 1
    explanation: "Let's look at the reaction given: Codeine phosphate ↔ Codeine + Phosphate . This reaction shows a compound, Codeine phosphate , breaking down into two separate parts: Codeine (which would be the codeine cation) and Phosphate (which would be the phosphate anion). This breaking down into charged particles (ions) is key. Now let's understand why Option B: Ionic hydrolysis is the correct answer. Ionic Hydrolysis: Ionic means the reaction involves ions . Ions are atoms or molecules that have a positive or negative electrical charge. Codeine phosphate is an ionic compound (a salt). When it breaks apart, it forms charged particles (ions). Hydrolysis comes from \"hydro\" (meaning water ) and \"lysis\" (meaning to break or split ). So, hydrolysis generally means a chemical reaction where water is used to break down a compound. In the context of pharmaceuticals, when an ionic compound like Codeine phosphate (a salt) is dissolved in water, it dissociates (breaks apart) into its constituent ions (Codeine cation and Phosphate anion). Water molecules surround and stabilize these ions, facilitating the breaking of the ionic bond. This process is often referred to as ionic hydrolysis because water plays a crucial role in enabling the salt to split into its ions. Let's briefly look at why the other options are incorrect: Option A: Oxidation Oxidation is a chemical reaction that involves the loss of electrons or an increase in the oxidation state of a molecule or ion. The given reaction is simply about a salt breaking into its ions, not about losing or gaining electrons in that way. Option C: Polymerisation Polymerisation is a process where small repeating units (monomers) join together to form a very large molecule called a polymer. This is not happening in our reaction; instead, a single compound is breaking apart. Option D: Isomerization Isomerization is a process where a molecule is transformed into another molecule that has the same atoms but arranged differently (an isomer). The molecular formula remains the same. Here, Codeine phosphate is breaking into two distinct ions, not rearranging its atoms to form a new isomer. Therefore, the breaking down of Codeine phosphate into Codeine and Phosphate ions, typically facilitated by water, best describes Ionic hydrolysis ."

    options:
      - 250 ml
      - 500 ml
      - 750 ml
      - 1000 ml
    answer: 3
    explanation: "One quart is a unit of volume measurement in the US customary and imperial systems. Option A: 250 ml 250 ml is incorrect as it is much smaller than a quart. Option B: 500 ml 500 ml is half of a liter and also less than a quart. Option C: 750 ml 750 ml is still less than a quart and is commonly used as a standard bottle size for wine. Option D: 1000 ml 1000 ml is equivalent to 1 liter, which is very close to the volume of one quart (1 US quart ≈ 946 ml, 1 UK quart ≈ 1136 ml). Thus, 1000 ml is generally accepted as equivalent to one quart in practical terms. Conclusion: One quart is approximately equal to 1000 ml, making Option D the correct answer."
