    question: If A + B means A is the brother of B; A % B means A is the father of B and A x B means A is the sister of B. Which of the following means M is the uncle of P?
    options:
      - M % N x P
      - N x P % M
      - M + S % R % P
      - M + K % T x P
    answer: 3
    explanation: M + K → M is the brother of K K % T → K is the father of T T x P → T is the sister of P Therefore, K is the father of P and M is the uncle of P.
