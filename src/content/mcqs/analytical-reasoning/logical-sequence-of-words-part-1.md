    question: Arrange the words given below in a meaningful sequence. 1. Family 2. Community 3. Member 4. Locality 5. Country
    options:
      - 3, 1, 2, 4, 5
      - 3, 1, 2, 5, 4
      - 3, 1, 4, 2, 5
      - 3, 1, 4, 5, 2
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Let's understand this type of question! In Logical Sequence of Words , you need to arrange the given words from smallest to largest or in a natural order that makes sense, like building blocks. Here are the words we have: 1. Family 2. Community 3. Member 4. Locality 5. Country Let's think about them in increasing order of size or scope, starting from the smallest part: 1. The smallest unit is a single person. In this list, a single person is a Member . So, we start with 3. Member . 2. A group of Members who live together, like parents and children, form a Family . Next comes 1. Family . 3. Many Families living close to each other, interacting and sharing things, form a Community . So, after Family, we have 2. Community . 4. A Community usually exists within a larger defined area or neighborhood, which we call a Locality . Think of a specific neighborhood where many communities might live. After Community, it's 4. Locality . 5. Many Localities, regions, and areas together make up a big geographical area called a Country . Finally, we have 5. Country . Putting it all together, the correct meaningful sequence from smallest to largest is: Member -> Family -> Community -> Locality -> Country This translates to the numbers given: 3 -> 1 -> 2 -> 4 -> 5 This sequence matches Option A: 3, 1, 2, 4, 5 . So, Option A is the correct answer ."
