    question: Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Saturday, Wednesday, Monday, .....
    options:
      - Sunday, Sunday
      - Sunday, Monday
      - Sunday, Wednesday
      - Sunday, Saturday
    answer: 0
    explanation: Sunday to Monday = no gap Monday to Wednesday = One day gap. Wednesday to Saturday = Two days gap. Saturday to Wednesday = Three days gap. Wednesday to Monday = Four days gap. In the next term there must be five days and six days gap. So, Next term would be Sunday, Sunday. Details Explanations The sequence follows a pattern based on the increasing gap of days between consecutive terms. From Sunday to Monday , there is no day gap . From Monday to Wednesday , there is a one-day gap . From Wednesday to Saturday , there is a two-day gap . From Saturday to Wednesday , there is a three-day gap (counting forward cyclically through the week). From Wednesday to Monday , there is a four-day gap . Following this logical progression, the next gaps should be five days and then six days . A five-day gap from Monday leads to Sunday , and a six-day gap from Sunday again leads to Sunday . Hence, the next two terms are Sunday, Sunday , making Option A the correct answer.
