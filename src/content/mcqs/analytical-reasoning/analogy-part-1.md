Incorrect Options: Option A: Knife This option is incorrect because a knife is not related to a pencil in the same way that ink is related to a pen. Option D: Chalk This option is incorrect because chalk is a writing medium on its own and is not related to a pencil. The correct relationship should be between a pencil and its writing substance. Correct Usage Example: Pen : Ink :: Pencil : Graphite This construction accurately represents the analogy by identifying the correct relationship between the items, highlighting that graphite is to a pencil what ink is to a pen."
