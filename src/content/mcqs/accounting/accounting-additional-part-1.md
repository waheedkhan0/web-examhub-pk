questions:
  - id: 1
    question: The normal balance of asset account is____________?
    options:
      - Credit balance
      - Debit balance
      - Cash balance
      - Neither debit nor credit balance
    answer: 1
    explanation: Assets carry a debit balance because every acquisition is entered on the debit side, so the rule is debit what comes in. Liabilities and capital run the other way and carry credit balances.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 2
    question: The normal balance of liability account is____________?
    options:
      - Debit balance
      - Credit balance
      - Cash balance
      - Neither debit nor credit balance
    answer: 1
    explanation: Liabilities are credited when they arise, which gives them a normal credit balance. The same rule covers capital, which is what the business owes back to its owner.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 3
    question: Which of the following statements is incorrect regarding capital account?
    options:
      - Debit increases the capital account balance
      - Credit increases the capital account balance
      - Fresh capital increases the capital account balance
      - Net income increases the capital account balance
    answer: 0
    explanation: The incorrect statement is the debit one, because a debit reduces capital. Drawings and losses are debited to capital while profit and fresh capital are credited to it.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 4
    question: An account records the ___________ in the balance of an item?
    options:
      - Increase
      - Decrease
      - Increase or decrease
      - Appreciation
    answer: 2
    explanation: An account is the summary record of increases and decreases in one item, not of a single direction of change, so both movements must be captured.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 5
    question: If credit side of a bank account is greater than the debit side, it indicates which of the following?
    options:
      - Bank overdraft
      - Cash at bank
      - Bank balance
      - Current Asset
    answer: 0
    explanation: A bank account is an asset to the business. When its credit side exceeds the debit side the account turns negative, which means the bank has lent the business money, that is, an overdraft.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 6
    question: If debit side of a bank account is greater than credit side it indicates which of the following?
    options:
      - Cash at bank
      - Bank understatement
      - Bank overdraft
      - Balance overstatement
    answer: 0
    explanation: When the debit side of the bank account is heavier, the business holds more at the bank than it has withdrawn, giving the asset balance known as cash at bank.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 7
    question: _________ will be credited if goods are given as charity?
    options:
      - Cash
      - Charity
      - Purchases
      - Sales
    answer: 2
    explanation: Goods distributed as charity leave the business without a sale, so purchases are credited to remove them at cost and charity is debited as the expense incurred.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 8
    question: Which of the following is a liability?
    options:
      - Cash
      - Equipment
      - Debtors
      - Creditors
    answer: 3
    explanation: Creditors are amounts owed to suppliers and are therefore liabilities. Cash, equipment and debtors are all resources owned or recoverable by the business, that is, assets.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 9
    question: "Identify the asset from the following:"
    options:
      - Cash and cash equivalent
      - Creditors
      - Notes payable
      - Bank loan
    answer: 0
    explanation: Cash and cash equivalents are the clearest asset on the list. Creditors, notes payable and a bank loan are all obligations owed to outsiders.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 10
    question: _______ the withdrawal of cash and goods by the owner of the busienss for his/her personal use?
    options:
      - Depreciation
      - Drawings
      - Outflow of cash
      - Appreciation
    answer: 1
    explanation: Drawings is the name for any withdrawal of cash or goods by the owner for personal use. It reduces capital and is never treated as a business expense.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 11
    question: Net loss occurs when______________?
    options:
      - Expenses are greater than Income
      - Expenses are less than Income
      - Expenses=Income
      - Liabilities are greater than income
    answer: 0
    explanation: A net loss arises when total expenses exceed total income for the period. The excess is transferred to the capital account as a debit.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 12
    question: Identify the nominal account
    options:
      - Machinery account
      - Building account
      - Creditors account
      - Rent expenses account
    answer: 3
    explanation: Nominal accounts record incomes, expenses and losses and are closed at the year end. Rent expense qualifies, while machinery and building are real accounts and creditors is a personal account.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 13
    question: Which of the following accounts can be classified as a real account?
    options:
      - Rent expenses account
      - Rent income account
      - insurance expenses account
      - Cash account
    answer: 3
    explanation: Real accounts survive the accounting period and appear on the balance sheet. Cash is the real account here, since rent expense, rent income and insurance expense are all nominal.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 14
    question: Liabilities are which of the following?
    options:
      - Resources
      - Obligations
      - Future benefits
      - Expenses
    answer: 1
    explanation: A liability is a present obligation arising from a past event, whose settlement causes an outflow of resources. Resources and future benefits describe assets instead.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 15
    question: ________ is the gross inflow of economic benefits?
    options:
      - Assets
      - Liabilities
      - Income
      - Expenses
    answer: 2
    explanation: Income is defined as the gross inflow of economic benefits during the period, excluding amounts collected on behalf of owners. Expenses are the gross outflow.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 16
    question: The gross decrease in economic benefits for the business are what?
    options:
      - Expenses
      - Obligations
      - Creditors
      - Income or gain
    answer: 0
    explanation: Expenses are the gross decrease in economic benefits during the period, other than drawings, and they reduce the capital of the business.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 17
    question: Liability are arisen from which of the following events?
    options:
      - Present event
      - Future event
      - Past event
      - None of them
    answer: 2
    explanation: Liabilities must arise from a past event that has already created an obligation. A future event has not yet given the business any duty to pay.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 18
    question: Which is the most important characteristic that all assets of a business have?
    options:
      - Long life of assets
      - Value of assets
      - Intangible nature of assets
      - Future economic benefits
    answer: 3
    explanation: The one characteristic shared by all assets is the ability to produce future economic benefits. Long life, measurable value and physical form do not apply to every asset.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 19
    question: ________ is a separate legal entity that Total capital can be divided in many shares.
    options:
      - Partnership
      - Sole proprietorship
      - Company
      - Non-profit organization
    answer: 2
    explanation: A company is a separate legal person whose total capital is divided into transferable shares. A sole proprietorship and a partnership are not wholly separate from their owners.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 20
    question: An asset posses which of the following?
    options:
      - Future economic benefits for the business
      - All kind of benefits for the business
      - Expenses for the business
      - Merits and Demerits for the business
    answer: 0
    explanation: An asset must promise future economic benefits for the business. Benefits already consumed are expenses, and an asset need not deliver every kind of advantage.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 21
    question: Auditing is what?
    options:
      - Reporting the financial information
      - Examination of financial statements
      - Preparation financial statements
      - maintaining the ledger records
    answer: 1
    explanation: Auditing is the independent examination of financial statements, whose purpose is to express an opinion on them. Preparing statements and maintaining ledgers are accounting duties, not auditing.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 22
    question: Keeping the log of financial information in books of original entries is called__________?
    options:
      - Recording
      - summarizing
      - Grouping
      - Processing
    answer: 0
    explanation: Recording is the first step of the accounting process and means keeping the log of transactions in the books of original entry. Summarising and classifying come afterwards.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 23
    question: Which of the following is the external user of financial statements?
    options:
      - Manager of the business
      - CEO of the business
      - Creditor of the business
      - Controller of the business
    answer: 2
    explanation: Creditors sit outside the business and rely on published statements to judge whether they will be paid. Managers, the chief executive and the controller are all internal users.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 24
    question: Which of the following is the internal user of financial statements?
    options:
      - Creditor of the business
      - Government agency
      - Shareholder of the business
      - Manager of the business
    answer: 3
    explanation: Managers use accounting information to run the business and are internal users. Creditors, government agencies and shareholders all depend on statements from outside the firm.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 25
    question: Financial statements differ from management account because
    options:
      - They are mainly prepared for external users of financial information
      - They are more complex and hard to prepare
      - The are the summary of accounting data
      - The are prepared on basis of actual concept
    answer: 0
    explanation: Financial accounting exists to serve external users and must follow prescribed standards, while management accounting is internal, forward-looking and free of such rules.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 26
    question: Which of the following is known as an evidence that a transaction took place?
    options:
      - Cash receipts journal
      - General journal
      - Source document
      - Cash book
    answer: 2
    explanation: A source document, such as an invoice or a receipt, is the original evidence that a transaction occurred. Journals and ledgers are the recording that follows it.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 27
    question: A sale of Rs. 50.000 to A was entered as a sale to B. This is an example of____________?
    options:
      - Error of omission
      - Error of commission
      - Compensating error
      - Error of principle
    answer: 1
    explanation: Posting a correct amount to the wrong account name is an error of commission. Here the figure is right and the class of account is right, so the trial balance still agrees.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 28
    question: Goods sent on approval basis have been recorded as Credit sales. This is an example of____________?
    options:
      - Error of principle
      - Error of commission
      - Error of omission
      - Error of duplication
    answer: 0
    explanation: Treating goods sent on approval as a sale records a revenue transaction that has not yet taken place, which is an error of principle because the correct accounting treatment itself has been misunderstood.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 29
    question: The unsold goods are ______________?
    options:
      - Equity
      - Sale return
      - Inventory
      - Purchases
    answer: 2
    explanation: Goods still unsold at the reporting date are inventory, an asset carried forward at the lower of cost and net realisable value.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 30
    question: Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan was established in____________?
    options:
      - "1949"
      - "1956"
      - "1961"
      - "1972"
    answer: 2
    explanation: The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan was established in 1961 under its own Act, and it regulates the accountancy profession in the country.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 31
    question: When owner withdraw cash for its private use, it is called ___________?
    options:
      - Profit
      - Income
      - Expense
      - Drawing
    answer: 3
    explanation: Withdrawals of cash by the owner for private use are drawings. They reduce capital directly and are not an expense of the business.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 32
    question: Assets having physical existence are called ___________?
    options:
      - Current asset
      - intangible asset
      - Tangible asset
      - Liquid asset
    answer: 2
    explanation: Assets with physical existence are tangible assets, such as machinery, furniture and stock. Intangible assets have no physical substance, and current and liquid assets classify assets by convertibility.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 33
    question: The assets which have some market value are called ____________?
    options:
      - Fixed asset
      - Quick asset
      - Fictitious assets
      - Real assets
    answer: 3
    explanation: Real assets are those with a market value that can be realised, in contrast with fictitious assets such as goodwill written off or preliminary expenses, which carry no resale value.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 34
    question: The assets which come into existence upon the happening of a certain event are called__________?
    options:
      - Contingent assets
      - Fixed assets
      - Fictitious assets
      - Quick assets
    answer: 0
    explanation: A contingent asset arises only if some uncertain future event happens. It is disclosed but not recognised until virtually certain, unlike fixed, quick and fictitious assets.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 35
    question: Income earned but not received is called ____________?
    options:
      - Fictitious assets
      - Quick asset
      - Real asset
      - Outstanding asset
    answer: 3
    explanation: Income earned but not yet received is an outstanding asset, also called accrued income, and is shown on the asset side as a receivable.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 36
    question: Debts which are payable in the course of a month are called _____________?
    options:
      - External liabilities
      - Fixed liabilities
      - Current liabilities
      - Liquid Liabilities
    answer: 2
    explanation: Debts payable within the operating cycle or twelve months are current liabilities. Fixed liabilities are repayable over a longer horizon.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 37
    question: For the business, capital is ____________?
    options:
      - Expense
      - Liability
      - Assets
      - All of them
    answer: 1
    explanation: Capital is a liability of the business to its owner under the entity concept, because the firm owes back to the owner what the owner introduced.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 38
    question: Cash discount is provided on _______________?
    options:
      - Purchases
      - Prompt payment
      - Sales
      - Sales return
    answer: 1
    explanation: Cash discount is allowed to encourage prompt payment and is recorded as an expense when allowed or income when received. It differs from a trade discount, which is deducted before entry.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 39
    question: Goods return to supplier are known as:__________?
    options:
      - Merchandise return
      - Purchase return
      - Return inwards
      - Sales return
    answer: 1
    explanation: Goods returned to a supplier are purchase returns, also called returns outwards. Sales returns are goods coming back from customers and are called returns inwards.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 40
    question: "The excess of current assets over current liabilities is called:"
    options:
      - Working Capital
      - Circulating capital
      - Fixed capital
      - Trading capital
    answer: 0
    explanation: Working capital is current assets minus current liabilities. Circulating capital refers to the current assets themselves before deducting their liabilities.
