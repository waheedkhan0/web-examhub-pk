questions:
  - id: 1
    question: When goods, in which business deals are sold, it is called___________?
    options:
      - Purchases
      - Return inward
      - Sales
      - Return outwards
    answer: 2
    explanation: Sale of the goods the business deals in is recorded in the sales account. Returns inwards are goods coming back from customers, and returns outwards are goods going back to suppliers.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 2
    question: Sales return is also known as____________?
    options:
      - Return received
      - Return Payed
      - Return inward
      - Return outward
    answer: 2
    explanation: Sales returns are goods received back from customers, so they are called returns inwards because the stock flows inward. They are debited, since they reduce the value of sales.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 3
    question: "reduction in price is called:"
    options:
      - Paid price
      - Invoice price
      - Book price
      - Discount
    answer: 3
    explanation: A discount is a reduction in the invoice price, granted either as a trade discount before entry or as a cash discount for prompt payment.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 4
    question: Cash brought by the owner to start business is called__________?
    options:
      - Capital
      - Loan
      - Drawing
      - None of these
    answer: 0
    explanation: Cash introduced by the owner to start the business is capital, a liability of the business to its owner under the entity concept. A loan would be an outside liability.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 5
    question: "The concession received on the price of defective goods is called:"
    options:
      - Discount
      - Cash discount
      - Allowance
      - Trading discount
    answer: 2
    explanation: A concession on defective goods retained by the buyer is an allowance, not a discount, because the goods themselves are not returned. The seller debits allowance.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 6
    question: In support of business transaction, any written evidence is called ___________?
    options:
      - Discount
      - Voucher
      - Allowance
      - Price
    answer: 1
    explanation: A voucher is the written evidence supporting a transaction, which is why every journal entry requires one before it is posted.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 7
    question: Building and furniture are called ____________?
    options:
      - Current asset
      - Fictitious asset
      - Tangible asset
      - Fixed assets
    answer: 3
    explanation: Buildings and furniture are fixed assets because they are acquired for use in the business rather than for resale. They are also tangible, but the classification asked for here is by purpose.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 8
    question: The person to whom goods are sold on credit is called___________?
    options:
      - Buyer
      - Seller
      - Debtor
      - Creditor
    answer: 2
    explanation: A customer who buys on credit owes the business money and is therefore a debtor. Creditors are the opposite, that is, suppliers the business pays.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 9
    question: "Discount received is a/an:"
    options:
      - Asset
      - Expense
      - Liability
      - Revenue
    answer: 3
    explanation: Discount received reduces the amount payable to a supplier and is treated as income, credited to the profit and loss account.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 10
    question: Obligation of the Business are known as___________?
    options:
      - Liabilities
      - Revenues
      - Expenses
      - Assets
    answer: 0
    explanation: Liabilities are the obligations of the business arising from past events, whose settlement causes an outflow of resources.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 11
    question: "The amount invested by the proprietor to start the business is called:"
    options:
      - Capital
      - Business
      - Drawings
      - All of them
    answer: 0
    explanation: The amount invested by the proprietor is capital, which the accounting equation treats as the owner's claim on the assets of the business.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 12
    question: A systematic way to maintain the books of accounts is called_________?
    options:
      - Accountancy
      - Economics
      - Book Keeping
      - Auditing
    answer: 2
    explanation: Book keeping is the systematic maintenance of the books of account. Accountancy is the broader discipline that includes classification, summarising and interpretation.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 13
    question: An Art of reading, classifying & Summarizing of accounts in a systematic way is called__________?
    options:
      - Accounting
      - Accountancy
      - Auditing
      - Book Keeping
    answer: 0
    explanation: Accounting is the art of recording, classifying and summarising financial events in a significant way and interpreting the results, which is why it goes beyond mere book keeping.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 14
    question: The maintenance of accounts in a systematic way is called__________?
    options:
      - Accounting
      - Reading
      - Book Keeping
      - Auditing
    answer: 2
    explanation: The day-to-day maintenance of accounts in books is book keeping, the recording stage of the wider accountancy process.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 15
    question: "Goods Return by the customer are termed as :"
    options:
      - Purchase return
      - Customer return
      - Sales return
      - Inventory return
    answer: 2
    explanation: Goods sent back by a customer are sales returns, also called returns inwards, and are debited to reduce sales.
    keyConcept: Accounting
  - id: 16
    question: Modern system of book keeping is called______________?
    options:
      - Double entry system
      - American system
      - Single entry system
      - Italic system
    answer: 0
    explanation: The double-entry system is the modern method because every transaction records both aspects, debit and credit, which makes the books self-verifying through the trial balance.
    keyConcept: Accounting
