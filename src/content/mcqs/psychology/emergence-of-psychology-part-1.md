    question: Who wrote the famous book 'Glimpses of Abhidhamma'?
    options:
      - Eric Fromm
      - Dalai Lama
      - Chogyam Trungpa
      - Daniel Goleman
    answer: 2
    explanation: The correct answer is C. Chogyam Trungpa. Chogyam Trungpa was a Tibetan Buddhist meditation master, scholar, and teacher. He is known for his influential teachings on Buddhism, meditation, and psychology. "Glimpses of Abhidhamma" is one of the books written by Chogyam Trungpa, where he explores the principles and concepts of Abhidhamma, a Buddhist philosophical and psychological system. Eric Fromm, Dalai Lama, and Daniel Goleman are notable figures in their respective fields, but they are not the authors of the book "Glimpses of Abhidhamma."
