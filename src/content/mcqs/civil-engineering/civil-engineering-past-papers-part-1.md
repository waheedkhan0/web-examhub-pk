    question: For a simply supported beam of span L carrying a uniformly distributed load of w per unit length, what is the maximum bending moment and its location?
    options:
      - wL^2 / 4 at quarter span
      - wL^2 / 8 at mid-span (L/2)
      - wL^2 / 12 at supports
      - wL / 2 at mid-span
    answer: 1
    explanation: "For a simply supported beam with total uniform load w across span L, the reaction at each end is wL/2. The bending moment equation is M(x) = (wL/2)*x - (w*x^2)/2. Maximum moment occurs at mid-span (x = L/2), yielding M_max = wL^2 / 8."
    keyConcept: Maximum Bending Moment for Uniformly Distributed Load
