    question: "The ratio of the total solar radiant energy returned by a planetary body to the total radiant energy incident on the body, the called:"
    options:
      - Reflectance
      - Reflectance factor
      - Albedo
      - None of these
    answer: 2
    explanation: Albedo is the ratio of the total solar radiant energy returned by a planetary body to the total radiant energy incident on the body. It is a measure of how much light that hits a surface is reflected without being absorbed. This term is commonly used in the fields of astronomy and remote sensing to describe the reflectivity of planets, moons, and other celestial bodies.
