    answer: 0

Option B: Temperature of moisture in air suddenly increases This option is not correct . An increase in temperature typically leads to the evaporation of moisture rather than precipitation. Option C: Temperature of moisture in air sharply decreases This option is partly correct . The circular motion of winds is associated with weather phenomena like cyclones or hurricanes but is not the primary cause of precipitation. Therefore, the most accurate options related to precipitation are a combination of Option A (The sky is overcast with clouds) and Option C (Temperature of moisture in air sharply decreases) . Precipitation often occurs when the sky is cloudy and the temperature of moist air decreases, leading to condensation and the formation of precipitation."
