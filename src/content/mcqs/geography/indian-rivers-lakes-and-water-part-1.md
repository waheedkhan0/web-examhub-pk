    options:

    explanation: "The correct answer is Option B: Damodar Let's break down why: Understanding the Question: The question asks us to identify a river that starts in a specific location (Kamarpet hill in Chhotanagpur Plateau, Bihar) and is known by a nickname: \"The River of Sorrow.\" Why Damodar is Correct: The Damodar River indeed originates in the Kamarpet hills of the Chhotanagpur Plateau . Why the Other Options are Incorrect: Mahanadi: The Mahanadi originates in Chhattisgarh , not the Chhotanagpur Plateau.
