    question: A UJT has ___________.
    options:
      - Two pn junctions
      - One pn junction
      - Three pn junctions
      - None of the above
    answer: 1
    explanation: A Unijunction Transistor (UJT) has one pn junction. The UJT consists of a single pn junction between its emitter and the base regions. This single pn junction is used for the UJT's unique characteristics, including its ability to act as a trigger device in various electronic circuits. Options A and C are incorrect as UJTs do not have two or three pn junctions; the correct number is one.
