    question: Which of the following is non-linear circuit parameter?
    options:
      - Inductance
      - Condenser
      - Wire wound resistor
      - Transistor
    answer: 3
    explanation: The non-linear circuit parameter among the given options is Transistor . A transistor is a semiconductor device that can amplify and switch electronic signals. It exhibits non-linear behavior, which means the output current or voltage is not directly proportional to the input current or voltage. The relationship between the input and output of a transistor is governed by complex exponential functions and is highly dependent on the operating conditions and biasing. This non-linearity makes transistors crucial components in various electronic circuits, enabling functions like amplification and signal processing.
