    question: In which year was the Indian Home Rule Society founded?
    options:

      - "1908"
      - "1911"
      - "1914"
    answer: 0
    explanation: "The Indian Home Rule Society was founded in 1905 in London by Shyamji Krishna Varma, along with other Indian nationalists like Bhikaji Cama and S.R. Rana. Purpose: The main aim of the society was to promote Indian self-rule (Swaraj) outside India and to provide a base for revolutionary activities against British colonial rule. Activities: It published nationalist literature, including the journal \"The Indian Sociologist,\" which spread anti-British propaganda and encouraged young Indians to join the struggle for independence. Significance: The society became a key platform for Indian revolutionaries abroad, linking freedom fighters in India with those working in Europe, and inspiring future movements for independence. Thus, the Indian Home Rule Society was established in 1905, making Option A the correct answer."
