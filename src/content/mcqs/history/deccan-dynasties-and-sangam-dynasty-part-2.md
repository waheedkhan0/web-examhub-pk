  - id: 19
    question: The bronze coins of Natraja or Dancing Shiva during the Chola period invariably depicted the deity with __________ arms
    options:
      - Eight
      - Six
      - Four
      - Two
    answer: 2
    explanation: "This question is about the Chola period , which was a very important time in the history of South India. The Cholas were powerful kings who were also great lovers of art, especially making beautiful statues out of bronze. One of their most famous artistic creations was the image of Nataraja . Nataraja is a form of Lord Shiva who is shown dancing. This dance is very symbolic; it represents the creation, preservation, and destruction of the universe. The question asks about the number of arms Nataraja has on the bronze coins and statues made during the Chola period. When the Cholas depicted Nataraja, they almost always showed him in a very specific way. The iconic Nataraja from the Chola period is always shown with four arms . Here's a simple look at what these four arms represent: - One upper hand holds a small drum, symbolizing creation . - The other upper hand holds a flame, symbolizing destruction . - One lower hand is raised in a gesture of blessing, showing protection . - The other lower hand points to his raised foot, symbolizing salvation or release. So, if you ever see a Chola bronze coin or statue of Nataraja, you will find him beautifully crafted with four arms . Therefore, the correct answer is Option C: Four ."
