    question: Apart from the Quit India Movement which started on 9th August 1942, what other sensational activity of the freedom fighters was done on 9th August?
    options:
      - Salt Satyagraha
      - Boycott of Simon Commission
      - Champaran Satyagraha
      - Kakori Mail train robbery
    answer: 3
    explanation: "Apart from the Quit India Movement which started on 9th August 1942, the other sensational activity of the freedom fighters that was done on 9th August was the Kakori Mail train robbery . The Kakori train robbery took place on 9th August 1925. The Kakori train robbery was an important incident during the Indian independence movement. A group of revolutionaries, led by Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqullah Khan, and others, targeted a train carrying British government funds. The aim was to use the funds for revolutionary activities against British colonial rule. The Kakori train robbery was an attempt by the revolutionaries to secure funds for their cause and create awareness about their struggle for independence. The incident highlighted the extent to which some freedom fighters were willing to go to achieve their goals. Option A: The Salt Satyagraha, also known as the Salt March, was a civil disobedience campaign initiated by Mahatma Gandhi in 1930 to protest against the British salt tax. It began on March 12, 1930, and concluded with the symbolic picking up of salt by Gandhi on April 6, 1930, at Dandi, Gujarat. Option B: The boycott of the Simon Commission took place in 1928 when the Simon Commission, a group of British officials, was appointed to review and recommend constitutional reforms in India. Indians protested the Commission's composition, which had no Indian members, and called for its boycott. Option C: The Champaran Satyagraha, initiated by Mahatma Gandhi in 1917, aimed to address the grievances of indigo farmers in Champaran, Bihar, against the oppressive indigo plantation system imposed by British landlords. Therefore, the correct option is Option D: Kakori Mail train robbery , which was another sensational activity of freedom fighters that took place on 9th August, apart from the Quit India Movement."
