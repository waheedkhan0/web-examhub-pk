    question: Under its Charter, the United Nations was established with how many principal organs?
    options:
      - Four
      - Five
      - Six
      - Seven
    answer: 2
    explanation: "Article 7 of the Charter names six principal organs: the General Assembly, the Security Council, the Economic and Social Council, the Secretariat, the International Court of Justice and the Trusteeship Council. The Trusteeship Council is the only one to have ceased working, suspending its operational activities in 2005."
    keyConcept: "UN Charter Article 7: the six principal organs and their functions"
    examTip: Count them as GA, UNSC, ECOSOC, Secretariat, ICJ and Trusteeship; a question on 'principal organs' expects six, not the number of specialised agencies.

    question: How many judges sit on the International Court of Justice, and for what length of term?
    options:
      - 15 judges, nine-year terms
      - 9 judges, six-year terms
      - 11 judges, five-year terms
      - 20 judges, seven-year terms
    answer: 0
    explanation: The ICJ Statute provides fifteen judges elected for nine-year terms by the General Assembly and the Security Council, with five seats renewed every three years. Nine judges constitute a quorum, and no two judges may be nationals of the same state.
    keyConcept: "ICJ composition: 15 judges, 9-year staggered terms, quorum of 9, seated at The Hague"
    examTip: "Do not confuse the ICJ's fifteen judges with the Security Council's fifteen members: the numbers match, the terms and the bodies do not."

  - id: 3
    question: The Universal Postal Union (UPU), which regulates international mail and postal money orders, has its headquarters in which city?
    options:
      - Geneva
      - Paris
      - Vienna
      - Bern
    answer: 3
    explanation: The UPU was created by the Treaty of Bern in 1874 and still sits in Bern, becoming a United Nations specialised agency in 1947. Geneva hosts the WHO, ILO, ITU and WMO, Paris hosts UNESCO, and Vienna hosts the IAEA and UNIDO.
    keyConcept: "Specialised agency seats: Bern (UPU), Geneva (WHO, ILO, ITU, WMO), Paris (UNESCO), Rome (FAO), Vienna (IAEA), Montreal (ICAO), London (IMO)"
    examTip: "UPU is the standard exception question: almost every other UN agency seat is Geneva, Paris, Rome, Vienna or New York."

    question: Which United Nations specialised agency sets global standards for civil aviation safety and air navigation under the 1944 Chicago Convention?
    options:
      - International Air Transport Association (IATA)
      - International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO)
      - International Maritime Organization (IMO)
      - World Tourism Organization (UNWTO)
    answer: 1
    explanation: ICAO was created by the 1944 Chicago Convention and is headquartered in Montreal; it issues Standards and Recommended Practices for aviation and developed the phonetic alphabet and airport code system. IATA, the most tempting distractor, is not a UN body but a trade association of airlines.
    keyConcept: State-level aviation regulation (ICAO, Chicago 1944) versus airline industry body (IATA)
    examTip: Treaty, standards and UN agency equals ICAO; airlines, fares and commercial cooperation equal IATA.
    testedIn:
      - FPSC
      - PPSC
  - id: 5
    question: The International Maritime Organization (IMO), author of the SOLAS and MARPOL conventions, is headquartered in which city?
    options:
      - Geneva
      - Brussels
      - London
      - Singapore
    answer: 2
    explanation: The IMO, established by a convention adopted in 1948 and known until 1982 as the Inter-Governmental Maritime Consultative Organization, is headquartered in London. SOLAS deals with the safety of life at sea, while MARPOL addresses pollution from ships.
    keyConcept: "Shipping and maritime safety law: IMO conventions SOLAS (1974) and MARPOL (1973)"
    examTip: IMO is the London-seat agency in a group of Geneva-seat agencies; the Law of the Sea itself is governed by UNCLOS, not the IMO.

      - PPSC
  - id: 6
    question: The World Trade Organization began operations on 1 January 1995 as the outcome of which international agreement?
    options:
      - The Havana Charter
      - The Tokyo Round agreements
      - The Bretton Woods agreements
      - The Marrakesh Agreement closing the Uruguay Round
    answer: 3
    explanation: The WTO was established by the Marrakesh Agreement, signed in April 1994 to conclude the Uruguay Round of multilateral trade negotiations, and replaced the GATT system. The Havana Charter of 1948 was the failed attempt to create an International Trade Organization, while Bretton Woods in 1944 produced the IMF and the World Bank.
    keyConcept: "Trade governance timeline: GATT 1947, Havana/ITO failure, Uruguay Round 1986-1994, WTO from 1995"
    examTip: Uruguay Round and Marrakesh give the WTO; Bretton Woods gives IMF and World Bank; Havana gives the ITO that never began.
    testedIn:
      - CSS
      - FPSC
      - PMS
  - id: 7
    question: The European Union, as a formal political and economic union, was created by which treaty?
    options:
      - The 1951 Treaty of Paris
      - The 1957 Treaty of Rome
      - The 1992 Treaty on European Union signed at Maastricht
      - The 2007 Treaty of Lisbon
    answer: 2
    explanation: The Treaty on European Union, signed at Maastricht in 1992 and in force from November 1993, created the European Union and introduced EU citizenship. Paris in 1951 established the European Coal and Steel Community, Rome in 1957 the European Economic Community, and Lisbon in 2007 amended rather than founded the union.
    keyConcept: "EU treaty chain: ECSC 1951, EEC 1957, Single European Act 1986, Maastricht 1992, Lisbon 2007"
    examTip: "'Which treaty created the EU' expects Maastricht; 'which treaty created the common market/EEC' expects Rome."
    testedIn:
      - CSS
      - PPSC
      - NTS

    question: Where is the General Secretariat of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) located?
    options:
      - Bangkok
      - Jakarta
      - Manila
      - Kuala Lumpur
    answer: 1
    explanation: ASEAN was founded by the Bangkok Declaration of 8 August 1967, but its permanent Secretariat is in Jakarta, Indonesia, hosted by the founding member. The five original members were Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand.
    keyConcept: ASEAN founding (Bangkok Declaration 1967) and institutional seat (Jakarta)
    examTip: "Founding city and secretariat city differ: founded at Bangkok, headquartered at Jakarta."

    question: The 'Shanghai Five' became the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation in 2001 when which state joined China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan as a founding member?

      - Pakistan
      - Iran
    answer: 0

    keyConcept: "SCO origins: Shanghai Five 1996, SCO establishment 2001, secretariat in Beijing, RATS in Tashkent"

  - id: 11
    question: The 1949 London Declaration allowed which country to remain a member of the Commonwealth after it became a republic?
    options:
      - Australia
      - Canada
      - India
      - South Africa
    answer: 2
    explanation: The London Declaration of 1949 dropped the requirement that members owe allegiance to the Crown, so India stayed in the Commonwealth when it became a republic in 1950, and the British sovereign took the title 'Head of the Commonwealth'. Pakistan, also a republic from 1956, similarly remained a member.
    keyConcept: "Empire to Commonwealth: Statute of Westminster 1931, London Declaration 1949, republics admitted"
    examTip: The declaration is the reason Asian and African republics could join; remember the year 1949, not 1931.

    question: The Group of Twenty (G20) was established in 1999 chiefly in response to which economic crisis?
    options:
      - The Asian financial crisis of 1997
      - The 1973 oil crisis
      - The Latin American debt crisis of the 1980s
      - The 2008 global financial crisis
    answer: 0
    explanation: The G20 was created in September 1999, bringing finance ministers and central bank governors together after the Asian financial crisis exposed the limits of the existing forums. Heads of state first met at summit level in Washington in November 2008 in response to the global financial crisis, which is why 2008 is the distractor.
    keyConcept: "Economic governance tiers: G7/G8, G20 founded 1999 at ministerial level, upgraded to leaders' summits in 2008"
    examTip: "Two dates for the G20: founded 1999 after the Asian crisis, elevated to leader level at the 2008 Washington summit."
