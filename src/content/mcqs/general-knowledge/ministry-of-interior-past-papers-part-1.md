    question: Under the Constitution of Pakistan 1973, internal security and national civil registration (NADRA) are administrative subjects governed by which Federal Ministry?
    options:
      - Ministry of Defense
      - Ministry of Interior
      - Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs
      - Ministry of Inter-Provincial Coordination
    answer: 1
    explanation: The Federal Ministry of Interior is the executive agency responsible for internal security, border control, citizenship, national registration (NADRA), immigration, and federal civil armed forces (Rangers, Frontier Corps, Coast Guards).
    keyConcept: Portfolios and Mandates under Federal Rules of Business
    testedIn:

  - id: 2
    question: The National Counter Terrorism Authority (NACTA) was formally established as an autonomous body under an Act of Parliament in which year?
    options:
      - 2008
      - 2013
      - 2015
      - 2018
    answer: 1
    explanation: NACTA was initially created in 2008 by executive order and formally enacted into law as an autonomous federal authority under the NACTA Act 2013 (Act XIX of 2013), tasked with national threat assessment and counter-terrorism coordination.
    keyConcept: NACTA Act 2013 - National Counter Terrorism Authority
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Assistant Director IB 2021
      - FPSC Ministry of Interior 2023

  - id: 3
    question: The National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) was constituted under which statutory legal instrument?
    options:
      - NADRA Ordinance 2000 (Ordinance No. VIII of 2000)
      - Civil Servants Act 1973
      - Pakistan Citizenship Act 1951
      - Electronic Transactions Ordinance 2002
    answer: 0
    explanation: NADRA was established on 10 March 2000 under the National Database and Registration Authority Ordinance 2000 (Ordinance VIII of 2000), merging the National Data Base Organization (NDO) and Directorate General of Registration (DGR).
    keyConcept: NADRA Ordinance 2000 - Legislative Basis
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Ministry of Interior 2022
      - NTS IB 2023

  - id: 4
    question: Which international border crossing connects Pakistan with Afghanistan through the Khyber Pass in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?
    options:
      - Chaman Border Crossing
      - Torkham Border Crossing
      - Taftan Border Crossing
      - Khunjerab Pass
    answer: 1
    explanation: Torkham is the major international border crossing between Pakistan (Khyber District, KP) and Afghanistan (Nangarhar Province) passing through the historical Khyber Pass. Chaman connects Balochistan with Kandahar.
    keyConcept: Major Strategic Border Terminals of Pakistan
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Ministry of Interior 2023
      - FPSC AD FIA 2022

  - id: 5
    question: Which constitutional article provides for the creation and functioning of the Council of Common Interests (CCI) to resolve inter-provincial disputes?
    options:
      - Article 153
      - Article 160
      - Article 184
      - Article 245
    answer: 0
    explanation: Article 153 of the Constitution of Pakistan establishes the Council of Common Interests (CCI), chaired by the Prime Minister and comprising the four Provincial Chief Ministers and three federal ministers appointed by the PM.
    keyConcept: Article 153 - Council of Common Interests (CCI)
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Inspector FIA 2022
      - Ministry of Interior 2023

  - id: 6
    question: "Under Article 245 of the Constitution of Pakistan 1973, the Armed Forces can be called by the Federal Government to:"
    options:
      - Enact federal financial budgets
      - Act in aid of civil power when called upon to do so
      - Dissolve provincial assemblies
      - Regulate commodity export prices
    answer: 1
    explanation: Article 245 stipulates that the Armed Forces shall, under the directions of the Federal Government, defend Pakistan against external aggression or threat of war, and, subject to law, act in aid of civil power when called upon to do so.
    keyConcept: Article 245 - Calling Armed Forces in Aid of Civil Power
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Assistant Director 2023
      - FPSC Ministry of Interior 2021

  - id: 7
    question: "The Durand Line, serving as the international boundary between Pakistan and Afghanistan, was demarcated following an agreement signed in:"
    options:
      - 1876
      - 1893
      - 1905
      - 1947
    answer: 1
    explanation: The Durand Line Agreement was signed on 12 November 1893 in Kabul between Sir Mortimer Durand (Foreign Secretary of British India) and Afghan Amir Abdur Rahman Khan, establishing the border stretching approximately 2,640 km.
    keyConcept: Historical Treaties - Durand Line Agreement 1893
    testedIn:
      - FPSC IB Screening 2022
      - PPSC Tehsildar 2023

  - id: 8
    question: Which federal security agency in Pakistan is primarily responsible for civil aviation security, counter-hijacking, and safeguarding airports?
    options:
      - Airports Security Force (ASF)
      - Maritime Security Agency (PMSA)
      - Pakistan Coast Guards
      - Federal Investigation Agency (FIA)
    answer: 0
    explanation: The Airports Security Force (ASF) was raised under the Airports Security Force Act 1975 to ensure the security of all civil airports, airstrips, aircraft, and passengers throughout Pakistan.
    keyConcept: ASF Mandate under ASF Act 1975
    testedIn:
      - FPSC ASF Assistant Director 2022
      - Ministry of Interior 2023

  - id: 9
    question: Under the Pakistan Citizenship Act 1951, citizenship by descent is primarily provided under which section?
    options:
      - Section 3
      - Section 5
      - Section 9
      - Section 14
    answer: 1
    explanation: Section 5 of the Pakistan Citizenship Act 1951 governs citizenship by descent for persons born outside Pakistan whose father (or mother, post-2000 amendment) is a citizen of Pakistan at the time of birth.
    keyConcept: Pakistan Citizenship Act 1951 - Citizenship Provisions
    testedIn:
      - FPSC Ministry of Interior 2023

    question: The Taftan border terminal links the Islamic Republic of Pakistan with which neighboring nation?

      - Islamic Republic of Iran

    explanation: Taftan (in Chagai District, Balochistan) is the primary official overland border checkpoint and trade terminal connecting Pakistan with the Islamic Republic of Iran (Mirjaveh border post).
