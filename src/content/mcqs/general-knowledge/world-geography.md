    question: Which strait connects the Black Sea to the Sea of Marmara?
    options:
      - Bosphorus
      - Gibraltar
      - Hormuz
      - Malacca
    answer: 0
    explanation: The Bosphorus carries Black Sea water south into the Sea of Marmara, and the Dardanelles then links the Sea of Marmara to the Aegean. Gibraltar joins the Atlantic to the Mediterranean, Hormuz the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, and Malacca the Andaman Sea to the South China Sea.
    keyConcept: "Black Sea route: Bosphorus -> Sea of Marmara -> Dardanelles -> Aegean"
    examTip: Examiners pair Bosphorus with Gibraltar; Gibraltar is the Atlantic-Mediterranean gateway.
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - FPSC
  - id: 2
    question: Which canal, opened in 1914, allows shipping to move between the Atlantic and Pacific without rounding Cape Horn?
    options:
      - Suez Canal
      - Kiel Canal
      - Panama Canal
      - Corinth Canal
    answer: 2
    explanation: The Panama Canal, linking the Caribbean Sea with the Pacific, opened to traffic on 15 August 1914 and replaced the long Cape Horn and Strait of Magellan route. The Suez Canal (opened 1869) joins the Mediterranean to the Red Sea and has no locks because both seas are at similar levels.
    keyConcept: "Sea-level canal versus lock canal: Suez (opened 1869, sea-level) and Panama (opened 1914, lifted through the Gatun and Miraflores lock flights)"
    examTip: Cape Horn and the Strait of Magellan are the pre-1914 route; the Kiel Canal instead links the Baltic with the North Sea.
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - CSS
      - NTS
  - id: 3
    question: Which mountain is the highest peak in the world outside Asia?
    options:
      - Denali
      - Aconcagua
      - Kilimanjaro
      - Elbrus
    answer: 1
    explanation: Aconcagua (about 6,961 m) in the Argentine Andes is the highest summit outside Asia, and the highest point in both the Western and Southern Hemispheres. Among the other options, Denali is the highest peak in North America (about 6,190 m), Kilimanjaro the highest in Africa (5,895 m), and Elbrus is usually counted as Europe's highest.
    keyConcept: "Highest peaks by range: Andes (Aconcagua), Rockies/NA (Denali), Caucasus (Elbrus), Alps (Mont Blanc)"
    examTip: All fourteen 8,000 m peaks lie in Asia, so 'highest outside Asia' always points to Aconcagua.
    testedIn:
      - FPSC
      - PMS
  - id: 4
    question: Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest summit in Africa, is located in which country?
    options:
      - Kenya
      - Ethiopia
      - Tanzania
      - Uganda
    answer: 2
    explanation: Kilimanjaro is a free-standing dormant volcano in northeastern Tanzania, close to the Kenyan border; its highest point, Uhuru Peak, is 5,895 m. The commonly tested trap is Mount Kenya (about 5,199 m), Africa's second-highest peak, which lies inside Kenya.
    keyConcept: "African physiography: Kilimanjaro (Tanzania), Mount Kenya, Ethiopian Highlands (Ras Dashen)"
    examTip: "Answer Tanzania, not Kenya: Kilimanjaro stands close to the Kenyan border, which is why Mount Kenya is the tempting trap."
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - FPSC
      - NTS
  - id: 5
    question: Which is the world's largest lake by surface area?
    options:
      - Lake Victoria
      - Lake Superior
      - Aral Sea
      - Caspian Sea
    answer: 3
    explanation: The Caspian Sea, at roughly 371,000 square kilometres, is the largest enclosed inland body of water on Earth, and is counted as the world's largest lake. Lake Superior is the largest lake by surface area among freshwater lakes, while Lake Baikal holds the largest volume of freshwater.
    keyConcept: "Superlative lakes: Caspian (largest area, saline), Superior (largest freshwater by area), Baikal (largest by volume)"
    examTip: "Read the wording closely: 'largest lake' is Caspian, 'largest freshwater lake by area' is Superior, 'deepest' is Baikal."
    testedIn:
      - CSS
      - PPSC
      - PMS

    question: The Danube rises in Germany's Black Forest and empties into which sea through a large delta?
    options:
      - Black Sea
      - Baltic Sea
      - Adriatic Sea
      - North Sea
    answer: 0
    explanation: The Danube flows east from the Black Forest and drains into the Black Sea through the Danube Delta, shared mainly by Romania and Ukraine. Its course is usually counted as passing through or bounding ten countries, the most of any river, which is why it features so often in mapping questions.
    keyConcept: "European river systems: Danube to Black Sea, Rhine to North Sea, Volga to Caspian, Dnieper to Black Sea"
    examTip: The Rhine reaches the North Sea and the Volga the Caspian Sea; the Danube, Dnieper and Dniester all end in the Black Sea.
    testedIn:
      - FPSC
      - NTS

    question: The Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, lies off the north-eastern coast of which country?
    options:
      - Indonesia
      - Philippines
      - Australia
      - Papua New Guinea
    answer: 2
    explanation: The Great Barrier Reef stretches for some 2,300 km off Queensland, Australia, in the Coral Sea. Indonesia and the Philippines sit in the nearby Indo-Pacific 'coral triangle', which is why they are tempting, but the reef system itself belongs to the Australian coast.
    keyConcept: Marine geography of the Coral Sea and Australian continental shelf
    examTip: Pair 'largest coral reef system' with Australia; 'most coral-species-rich waters' points to the Indonesia-Philippines triangle.
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - FPSC
  - id: 8
    question: The Atacama Desert, widely described as the driest non-polar desert on Earth, lies mainly in which country?
    options:
      - Peru
      - Chile
      - Bolivia
      - Argentina
    answer: 1
    explanation: The Atacama runs about 1,000 km down the Pacific side of the Andes, largely within northern Chile, shadowed by the Andes and the cold Humboldt Current. The similar-looking distractor Namib is in Namibia, and the Kalahari spans Botswana, Namibia and South Africa.
    keyConcept: "Rain-shadow and cold-current deserts: Atacama, Namib, coastal Peru"
    examTip: Atacama equals Chile; the Great Basin and Mojave are North American, the Namib is in southern Africa.
    testedIn:
      - CSS
      - PPSC
      - NTS

    question: Which of the following is one of the world's only two 'doubly landlocked' countries?
    options:
      - Switzerland
      - Austria
      - Hungary
      - Liechtenstein
    answer: 3
    explanation: A doubly landlocked state is surrounded entirely by other landlocked states, and only Liechtenstein and Uzbekistan qualify. Switzerland, Austria and Hungary are landlocked, but each touches at least one coastal country, so none of them is doubly landlocked.
    keyConcept: "Landlocked categories: landlocked vs doubly landlocked (Liechtenstein, Uzbekistan)"
    examTip: If asked to name the two doubly landlocked countries, answer Liechtenstein and Uzbekistan; Kazakhstan is the largest ordinary landlocked state.
    testedIn:
      - FPSC
      - PMS
      - CSS
  - id: 10
    question: Which is the world's largest island by total area?
    options:
      - New Guinea
      - Borneo
      - Madagascar
      - Greenland
    answer: 3
    explanation: Greenland, at about 2.16 million square kilometres, is the largest island; Australia is treated as a continental landmass rather than an island. New Guinea is the second-largest island and Borneo the third.
    keyConcept: "Island ranking: Greenland > New Guinea > Borneo > Madagascar"
    examTip: Textbooks exclude Australia from island lists; if Australia appears as an option, choose Greenland.
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - NTS
  - id: 11
    question: The western section of the Canada-United States boundary runs mainly along which line of latitude?
    options:
      - 42nd parallel north
      - 45th parallel north
      - 49th parallel north
      - 54th parallel north
    answer: 2
    explanation: From the Strait of Georgia eastward to the Lake of the Woods, the border follows the 49th parallel, fixed by the Anglo-American Convention of 1818 and extended by the Oregon Treaty of 1846. The 54th parallel is remembered only from the 1844 'Fifty-four forty or fight' slogan, which was never the settled boundary.
    keyConcept: "Boundary-making by latitude: 49th parallel (Canada-US west), 45th parallel (US-Canada east segment)"
    examTip: The 'Fifty-four forty' line was a war cry, not a treaty; the actual answer is the 49th parallel.
    testedIn:
      - CSS
      - FPSC
      - PMS
  - id: 12
    question: The Yangtze (Chang Jiang), the longest river in Asia, flows entirely within which country?
    options:
      - India
      - Russia
      - Vietnam
      - China
    answer: 3
    explanation: The Yangtze rises on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau and runs about 6,300 km east to the East China Sea at Shanghai, entirely inside China, making it the longest river contained within a single country. The Mekong, by contrast, is the great international river of South-East Asia, flowing through six countries to the South China Sea.
    keyConcept: "River basin geography: Yangtze (single-state) vs Mekong and Danube (multi-state)"
    examTip: Longest river entirely in one country equals Yangtze in China; the Lena and Volga are Russia's long single-state rivers.
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - NTS

    question: Which country has the largest total area in the world?
    options:
      - Canada
      - China
      - Russia
      - United States
    answer: 2
    explanation: Russia covers about 17.1 million square kilometres, roughly eleven percent of the world's inhabited land, and spans eleven time zones. Canada is the second-largest country at about 9.98 million square kilometres, which makes it the strongest distractor.
    keyConcept: "Country area ranking: Russia, Canada, China/USA, Brazil, Australia"
    examTip: "Ranking order to memorise: Russia, Canada, then China and the United States, then Brazil and Australia."
    testedIn:
      - PPSC
      - CSS

    question: The shores of which body of water, at roughly 430 metres below sea level, are the lowest exposed land surface on Earth?
    options:
      - Caspian Sea
      - Dead Sea
      - Lake Assal
      - Great Salt Lake
    answer: 1
    explanation: The Dead Sea shore, fed only by the Jordan River and lost to evaporation, lies about 430 m below sea level and is the lowest exposed land point on Earth. The Caspian Sea depression is also below sea level, but only by about 28 m, and Lake Assal in Djibouti is far shallower at about 155 m below sea level.
    keyConcept: "Endorheic basins below sea level: Dead Sea (Jordan Rift Valley), Caspian depression, Lake Assal"
    examTip: The Dead Sea is also among the saltiest lakes, roughly ten times normal seawater salinity.

      - FPSC
