    question: "Match List-I with List-II and select the correct answer: List I List II a. Quantitative increase and service 1. Sustainable Development b. Development which does not affect 2. Under development c. To increase national and earning per capita income 3. Growth d. Development that has not been achieved by international beliefs 4. Economic development"
    options:
      - a-3, b-1, c-4, d-2
      - a-1, b-4, c-3, d-2
      - a-3, b-2, c-4, d-1
      - a-1, b-2, c-3, d-4
    answer: 0
    explanation: "Okay, let's break down this question step-by-step so it's easy to understand! Understanding the Lists: List I gives you descriptions of different types of development. List II gives you the terms for those descriptions. Your job is to match the description to the correct term. Breaking Down Each Matching: a. \"Quantitative increase and service\" : This is about increasing the *amount* of something and also improving available services. This most closely relates to 3. Growth because it's generally about increasing production or activity. b. \"Development which does not affect\" : This points to a type of development that does not compromise resources for future needs. This best matches with 1. Sustainable Development , because Sustainable Development focuses on meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. c. \"To increase national and earning per capita income\" : This directly relates to improving the financial well-being of a country and its people. This is the definition of 4. Economic development , where the goal is to improve the standard of living through wealth creation. d. \"Development that has not been achieved by international beliefs\" : This describes the state of not achieving development because of the lack of resources. So this matches with 2. Under development . Putting it All Together: a - 3 (Growth) b - 1 (Sustainable Development) c - 4 (Economic Development) d - 2 (Under development) The Answer: Therefore, the correct answer is Option A: a-3, b-1, c-4, d-2"
