questions:
  - id: 1
    question: "A portion of profits, which a company retains itself for further expansion, is known as:"
    options:
      - Dividends
      - Retained Earnings
      - Capital Gain
      - None of the given options
    answer: 1
    explanation: Retained earnings are the part of profit kept in the business instead of distributed, and they fund expansion without raising new capital.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 2
    question: Finance is vital for which of the following business activity (activities)?
    options:
      - Marketing Research
      - Product Pricing
      - Design of marketing and distribution channels
      - All of the given options
    answer: 3
    explanation: Finance touches every activity listed, because marketing research, pricing decisions and channel design all require funds and are judged on their financial return.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 3
    question: "Agency theory suggests that managers(the agents), particularly those of large , publically-owned firms, may have different objectives from those of the:"
    options:
      - Workers
      - Subordinates
      - Shareholders
      - Employees
    answer: 2
    explanation: Agency theory contrasts the objectives of managers, the agents, with those of the shareholders, the principals, whose wealth the managers are engaged to maximise.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 4
    question: Period costs include which of the following?
    options:
      - Selling expense
      - Raw material
      - Direct labor
      - Manufacturing overhead
    answer: 0
    explanation: Period costs are charged to the period in which they are incurred rather than to the product, so selling expense qualifies while raw material, direct labour and manufacturing overhead are product costs.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 5
    question: Product costs include which of the following?
    options:
      - Selling expenses
      - General expenses
      - Manufacturing overhead
      - Administrative expenses
    answer: 2
    explanation: Product costs are those incurred to make the goods, namely raw material, direct labour and manufacturing overhead. Selling, general and administrative expenses are period costs.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 6
    question: Financial policy is evaluated by which of the following?
    options:
      - Profit Margin
      - Total Assets Turnover
      - Debt-equity ratio
      - None of the given options
    answer: 2
    explanation: The debt-to-equity ratio describes how the firm finances itself and therefore measures financial policy, whereas profit margin measures operating efficiency and assets turnover measures asset use.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 7
    question: Cash flow from assets involves which of the following component(s)?
    options:
      - Operating cash flow
      - Capital spending
      - Change in net working capital
      - All of the given options
    answer: 3
    explanation: Cash flow from assets equals operating cash flow less capital spending and less the change in net working capital, so all three components are involved.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 8
    question: "Which of the following refers to the cash flows that result from the firm\x9Fs day-to-day activities of producing and selling?"
    options:
      - Operating Cash Flows
      - Investing Cash Flows
      - Financing Cash Flows
      - All of the given options
    answer: 0
    explanation: Operating cash flow is the cash generated by the routine business of producing and selling, before spending on fixed assets and before transactions with investors.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 9
    question: "All the constituencies with a stake in the fortunes of the company are termed as:"
    options:
      - Stakeholders
      - Directors
      - Chief executives
      - Subordinates
    answer: 0
    explanation: Stakeholders is the term for every group with a stake in the company's fortunes, including shareholders, employees, creditors, customers and the community.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 10
    question: "The system by which companies are managed and controlled is known as:"
    options:
      - Management System
      - Strategic System
      - Corporate Governance
      - Internal System
    answer: 2
    explanation: Corporate governance is the system by which companies are directed and controlled, defining the allocation of rights and responsibilities among the board and managers.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 11
    question: "Corporate governance encompasses the relationship among a companys:"
    options:
      - Shareholders and board of director
      - Board of directors and senior management
      - Shareholders and senior management
      - Shareholders, board of directors and senior management
    answer: 3
    explanation: Corporate governance spans all three relationships at once, that is, between shareholders and the board, between the board and management, and consequently between shareholders and senior management.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 12
    question: "The Board of Directors sets company-wide policy and advices the CEO and other senior executies, who manage the companys:"
    options:
      - Managerial activities
      - Year-to-Year activities
      - Day-to-Day activities
      - Financial activities
    answer: 2
    explanation: The board sets policy and advises the chief executive, while management runs the day-to-day activities of the company within that framework.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 13
    question: "A major facet of financial management involves providing the financing necessary to support:"
    options:
      - Liabilities
      - Debts
      - Loans
      - Assets
    answer: 3
    explanation: Financial management must provide financing for the assets the firm holds, since every asset on the balance sheet is funded by either debt or equity.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 14
    question: ___________ is concerned with the acquisition, financing, and management of assets with some overall goal in mind.
    options:
      - Financial management
      - Profit maximization
      - Agency theory
      - Social responsibility
    answer: 0
    explanation: Financial management is defined by exactly these three decisions, the acquisition, financing and management of assets, pursued toward an overall objective.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 15
    question: "Having some overall goal in mind, financial management is concerned with:"
    options:
      - Acquisition of assets
      - Financing of assets
      - Management of assets
      - All of them
    answer: 3
    explanation: The three interlocking concerns of financial management are acquiring assets, financing them and managing them, all directed at the firm's overall goal.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 16
    question: "Profit maximization is the maximizing a firms Earning:"
    options:
      - Before Tax
      - After Tax
      - Both A and B
      - None of Them
    answer: 1
    explanation: Profit maximisation is normally stated in after-tax terms, because it is the residual available to owners that determines their return, not the pre-tax figure.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 17
    question: "An individual authorized by another person, called the principle, to act on the latters on behalf is known as an/a:"
    options:
      - Agent
      - Servant
      - Subordinate
      - Assistant
    answer: 0
    explanation: An agent is a person authorised by the principal to act on the principal's behalf, which is the relationship that agency theory analyses.
    keyConcept: Finance
  - id: 18
    question: "Stakeholders include:"
    options:
      - Stakeholders
      - Creditors and customs
      - Employees and suppliers
      - All of Them
    answer: 3
    explanation: Stakeholders include creditors, employees, suppliers and customers as well as shareholders, so all of the listed groups are covered.
