## What is the National Aptitude Test (NAT)?

The **National Aptitude Test (NAT)**, administered by the **National Testing Service (NTS)**, is Pakistan’s primary standardized entrance test for admissions into undergraduate and graduate programs across dozens of affiliated public and private universities.

For students completing their Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC / F.Sc / ICS / I.Com / A-Levels), the **NAT-I series** serves as the gateway to premier higher education institutions, including COMSATS University Islamabad, Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU), the National Textile University (NTU), and the Institute of Space Technology (IST).

Securing a high percentile in the NAT not only secures your admission offer but also qualifies you for merit-based scholarships. Below is an exhaustive breakdown of the examination structure, category requirements, scoring mechanics, and preparation strategies.

### The NAT-I Category Breakdown (For Intermediate Graduates)

Because undergraduate academic disciplines demand distinct academic foundations, NTS designs the NAT-I test in six specialized streams corresponding to your intermediate study background:

| Category | Target Academic Background | University Degree Pathways |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NAT-IE** | Pre-Engineering (Math, Physics, Chemistry) | Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, and Software Engineering |
| **NAT-IM** | Pre-Medical (Biology, Physics, Chemistry) | Biotechnology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacy, and Allied Health Sciences |
| **NAT-ICS** | Computer Science (Math, Physics, CS) | BS Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Cyber Security, Data Science |
| **NAT-IGS** | General Science (Math, Statistics, Economics) | BS Economics, Statistics, Data Analytics, Actuarial Sciences |
| **NAT-IA** | Arts & Humanities | BS English Literature, International Relations, Mass Communication, Sociology |
| **NAT-ICOM** | Commerce | BBA, BS Accounting & Finance, Banking, Commerce |

### Paper Pattern & Marks Distribution (90 MCQs | 120 Minutes)

All NAT-I examination categories follow a uniform structural pattern: **90 multiple-choice questions** to be attempted in **120 minutes (2 hours)**. There is **no negative marking** in NTS examinations.

```
Total Test Scheme: 90 MCQs (100 Marks Scaled)
├── Section 1: Verbal Reasoning (20 MCQs)
├── Section 2: Analytical Reasoning (20 MCQs)
├── Section 3: Quantitative Reasoning (20 MCQs)
└── Section 4: Subject Knowledge (30 MCQs)
```

#### Detailed Breakdown by Section

#### 1. Verbal Reasoning (20 MCQs)
Assesses English grammatical proficiency, vocabulary precision, and logical verbal relationships:
* **Sentence Completion (5 MCQs):** Selecting appropriate context words or connectors to fill blank spaces.
* **Analogies (5 MCQs):** Evaluating proportional conceptual relationships (*Author : Novel :: Sculptor : Statue*).
* **Antonyms & Synonyms (5 MCQs):** Standard academic vocabulary comprehension.
* **Reading Comprehension (5 MCQs):** Answering critical inference, theme, and factual questions based on a 250-word passage.

#### 2. Analytical Reasoning (20 MCQs)
Consistently considered the most challenging section of the NAT. It presents scenario-based puzzles requiring deductive logic:
* **Linear & Circular Seating Arrangements:** Placing 5 to 7 individuals around a table or in a row based on given constraints (*"A cannot sit adjacent to B; C must be immediately left of D"*).
* **Grouping & Selection Problems:** Selecting a committee or team from a pool of candidates under specific conditional rules (*"If X is chosen, Y cannot be selected"*).
* **Sequencing & Scheduling Puzzles:** Ordering days, tasks, or event milestones without violating precedence rules.

#### 3. Quantitative Reasoning (20 MCQs)
Evaluates basic problem-solving arithmetic and algebraic competence up to matriculation level:
* **Arithmetic:** Fractions, decimals, percentages, profit and loss, ratios, and proportional sharing.
* **Algebra:** Linear equations, simultaneous equations, exponent rules, and factoring.
* **Geometry:** Perimeter, area, volume of spheres and cylinders, Pythagorean theorem, and angle measurements.

#### 4. Subject-Specific Section (30 MCQs)
Tests core textbook concepts from your intermediate syllabi:
* **NAT-IE:** Physics (10 MCQs), Chemistry (10 MCQs), Mathematics (10 MCQs).
* **NAT-IM:** Biology (10 MCQs), Physics (10 MCQs), Chemistry (10 MCQs).
* **NAT-ICS:** Computer Science (10 MCQs), Mathematics (10 MCQs), Physics (10 MCQs).
* **NAT-ICOM:** Accounting (10 MCQs), Commerce (10 MCQs), Economics (10 MCQs).

### NAT Scoring, Percentiles, and Score Validity

* **Raw Score vs. Percentile:** NTS issues result cards reporting both your raw marks (out of 90, scaled to 100) and your **percentile rank**. Highly competitive programs (such as BS Computer Science at COMSATS Islamabad or Lahore) typically demand a percentile score of **88th percentile or higher**.
* **Validity Period:** A NAT result is officially valid for **one full calendar year** from the date of result declaration. 
* **Multi-Attempt Advantage:** NTS conducts NAT examinations every single month throughout the year. You can take the NAT multiple times, and universities will evaluate your application using your **highest valid score**.

### Step-by-Step Registration Guide for NAT 2026

1. **Create an NTS Portal Account:** Visit the official NTS candidate registration portal (`portal.nts.org.pk`) and register using your CNIC or B-Form number.
2. **Upload Scanned Documents:** Upload a recent passport-sized photograph with a white background and a clear scanned copy of your CNIC/B-Form.
3. **Select Test Category & Center:** Choose your relevant category (e.g., NAT-IE or NAT-ICS) and select your preferred test city (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Faisalabad, etc.).
4. **Fee Payment via 1Link:** Generate the fee challan (approx. PKR 1,000–1,200) and pay instantly through mobile banking apps (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or any 1Link ATM/banking portal) using your unique 1Bill consumer number.
5. **Download Roll Number Slip:** Roll number slips are made available for download 7 days prior to the test date. Print your slip and ensure you bring your original CNIC/B-Form to the test center.

### Tactical Advice for Scoring 85+ in the NAT

* **Master Analytical Logic First:** Do not leave Analytical Reasoning to luck. Practice 10 scenario puzzles daily to train your mind to sketch logic diagrams within 60 seconds.
* **Pace Yourself:** 90 questions in 120 minutes allows roughly **80 seconds per question**. Never spend more than 2 minutes on a single arithmetic problem—mark it, move on, and return if time permits.
* **Practice on ExamHub:** Sharpen your skills with timed modules across [Verbal, Quantitative, and Analytical Reasoning on ExamHub](/practice).
