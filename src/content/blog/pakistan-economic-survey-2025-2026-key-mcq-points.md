## Why the Pakistan Economic Survey is Crucial for Competitive Exams

The **Pakistan Economic Survey**, released annually ahead of the Federal Budget by the Ministry of Finance, is the government's official scorecard on the national economy. For competitive examination aspirants (CSS, PMS, FPSC General Recruitment, PPSC Tehsildar, and Assistant Director positions), this document is the primary source for factual verification.

Examiners in CSS Pakistan Affairs, Current Affairs, and General Knowledge consistently draw both objective screening MCQs and descriptive analytical questions directly from its chapters.

Below are **50 high-yield, verified statistical facts** categorized by sector to streamline your revision.

---

### Section 1: Macroeconomic Growth & GDP Composition (Facts 1–8)

1. **GDP Growth Rate:** Pakistan's provisional Gross Domestic Product (GDP) real growth rate for the financial year stood at **2.38%**, rebounding from a previous contraction.
2. **Nominal GDP Size:** Pakistan's nominal GDP reached approximately **PKR 106,045 billion** (equivalent to roughly **$375 billion** at prevailing exchange rates).
3. **Services Sector Share:** The Services sector remains the largest contributor to the national economy, accounting for **57.7% of total GDP**.
4. **Agriculture Sector Share:** Agriculture contributes approximately **24.0% of GDP**, employing over **37.4% of the country's total labor force**.
5. **Industrial Sector Share:** The Industrial and Manufacturing sector accounts for approximately **18.3% of GDP**.
6. **Per Capita Income:** Per capita income was recorded at **$1,680**, registering an upward trajectory compared to previous flood-impacted fiscal cycles.
7. **Investment-to-GDP Ratio:** Total investment dropped to **13.1% of GDP**, highlighting persistent structural bottlenecks in domestic capital formation.
8. **National Savings Rate:** Gross national savings stood at **13.0% of GDP**, reflecting sustained inflationary pressure on household disposable incomes.

### Section 2: Agriculture & Major Crops (Facts 9–16)

9. **Agricultural Growth Performance:** The agriculture sector posted a robust growth rate of **6.25%**, propelled by bumper harvests in major cash crops.
10. **Wheat Production Record:** National wheat production achieved a historic benchmark of **31.44 million tonnes**, supported by favorable weather conditions and higher minimum support prices.
11. **Cotton Harvest Recovery:** Cotton output surged by over **60% year-on-year**, reaching **10.22 million bales** after disastrous flood damage in preceding cycles.
12. **Rice Production:** Rice output expanded to **9.87 million tonnes**, providing significant momentum to Pakistan's non-textile export receipts.
13. **Sugarcane Yield:** Sugarcane production reached **67.7 million tonnes**, maintaining comfortable domestic reserves for refined sugar.
14. **Livestock Sector Dominance:** Within the agriculture sector, **Livestock accounts for 60.84%** of the agricultural value-added and over **14.6% of overall national GDP**.
15. **Water Availability:** Canal water availability during the Kharif season was recorded at **61.9 million acre-feet (MAF)**, while Rabi availability stood at **30.6 MAF**.
16. **Fertilizer Consumption:** Urea offtake during the year exceeded **6.6 million tonnes**, reflecting enhanced domestic demand across major agricultural belts.

---

### Section 3: Industry & Large-Scale Manufacturing (Facts 17–23)

17. **Large-Scale Manufacturing (LSM) Growth:** LSM registered a modest recovery of **0.99%**, dampened by elevated interest rates, energy tariffs, and import controls on raw industrial inputs.
18. **Leading Industrial Sectors:** Wearing apparel, food and beverages, and chemicals contributed positively to industrial value addition.
19. **Automobile Contraction:** The automotive sector suffered a contraction exceeding **35%**, driven by elevated auto financing rates and currency depreciation.
20. **Cement Production & Consumption:** Total cement dispatches (domestic and exports) hovered around **45.3 million tonnes**, reflecting subdued public sector development spending.
21. **Mining and Quarrying:** The mining sub-sector contracted by **1.5%**, with significant unexploited potential remaining in Balochistan's Reko Diq and Saindak deposits.
22. **Textile Sector Share:** The textile manufacturing chain constitutes over **58% of total export receipts** and roughly **46% of manufacturing employment**.
23. **Small-Scale Manufacturing:** Small-scale industries posted an estimated growth of **8.6%**, proving resilient against broader economic headwinds.

### Section 4: Fiscal Deficit & Tax Mobilization (Facts 24–30)

24. **Fiscal Deficit Ratio:** The overall fiscal deficit was contained at **6.8% of GDP**, compared to 7.7% in the corresponding previous period.
25. **Primary Surplus Milestone:** Pakistan achieved a **primary budget surplus of 1.5% of GDP**, fulfilling a critical structural benchmark of the IMF Extended Fund Facility (EFF).
26. **FBR Tax Collection:** The Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) mobilized over **PKR 9,252 billion**, reflecting a nominal growth of nearly 30%.
27. **Direct Taxes Contribution:** Direct taxes (income tax and corporate super taxes) accounted for approximately **47% of total FBR revenues**, closing the gap with indirect taxation.
28. **Sales Tax Dominance:** Sales tax remains the single largest component of indirect taxes, contributing over **PKR 3,000 billion**.
29. **Debt Servicing Burden:** Interest payments and debt servicing consumed over **55% of total federal net revenues**, crowding out discretionary development expenditures.
30. **Public Sector Development Programme (PSDP):** The revised federal PSDP size was rationalized to **PKR 950 billion**, prioritizing core water infrastructure and CPEC road corridors.

### Section 5: Money, Banking & Monetary Policy (Facts 31–36)

31. **Policy Rate Benchmark:** The State Bank of Pakistan's (SBP) Monetary Policy Committee maintained the benchmark policy rate at **22%** for consecutive quarters before beginning phased rate cuts as headline inflation eased.
32. **Headline CPI Inflation:** Average National Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation was recorded at **23.4%**, driven by energy tariff adjustments and elevated food prices.
33. **Broad Money (M2) Growth:** Broad money supply expanded by **12.8%**, driven primarily by net budgetary borrowing from the banking sector.
34. **Private Sector Credit:** Credit to the private commercial sector remained deeply suppressed at under **PKR 250 billion**, reflecting high borrowing hurdles.
35. **Islamic Banking Assets:** The market share of Islamic banking assets in the total banking industry rose to **22.5%**, reflecting ongoing transition mandates towards Riba-free commercial finance.
36. **Digital Payment Transactions:** Mobile and internet banking transactions crossed **PKR 100 trillion** in value, propelled by the SBP Raast instant payment system.

### Section 6: External Sector, Trade & Remittances (Facts 37–43)

37. **Current Account Deficit (CAD):** The Current Account Deficit narrowed dramatically to **under $1.5 billion** (less than 0.5% of GDP), compared to over $17 billion two years prior.
38. **Workers' Remittances:** Foreign remittances sent by overseas Pakistani workers totaled approximately **$30.2 billion**, with Saudi Arabia and the UAE serving as the two leading geographic sources.
39. **Export Earnings:** Total merchandise exports stood at approximately **$30.6 billion**, led by ready-made garments, bedwear, knitwear, and non-basmati rice.
40. **Import Bill:** Total merchandise imports were compressed to **$54.7 billion**, reflecting stringent administrative screening of non-essential luxury imports.
41. **Trade Deficit:** The merchandise trade deficit narrowed by over **12%**, settling at approximately **$24.1 billion**.
42. **Foreign Exchange Reserves:** Official liquid reserves held by the State Bank of Pakistan recovered from crisis lows of $3 billion to approximately **$9.1 billion**, supported by multilateral inflows and bilateral rollovers.
43. **External Debt Stock:** Pakistan's total external public debt stood at **$86.4 billion**, with multilateral development banks (World Bank, ADB) holding the largest single creditor share.

### Section 7: Energy, Power & Circular Debt (Facts 44–47)

44. **Total Installed Power Capacity:** Pakistan's total installed electricity generation capacity reached **45,885 MW**.
45. **Energy Generation Mix:** Hydel power accounts for **28.6%**, Thermal (coal, RLNG, gas, furnace oil) accounts for **55.2%**, Nuclear contributes **8.2%**, and Renewables (wind and solar) account for **8.0%**.
46. **Circular Debt Stock:** The power sector circular debt was reported at approximately **PKR 2,310 billion**, creating persistent liquidity crises across the power distribution companies (DISCOs).
47. **Transmission Bottlenecks:** While generation capacity exceeds 45,000 MW, the national grid's maximum transmission evacuation capacity remains capped at roughly **26,000 MW**.

---

### Section 8: Demographics & Human Development (Facts 48–50)

48. **National Population Benchmark:** According to the 7th Population and Housing Census (Digital Census), Pakistan's total population stands at **241.49 million**, with an annual population growth rate of **2.55%**.
49. **Urban-Rural Distribution:** **61.18%** of Pakistan's population resides in rural areas, while **38.82%** is settled in urban municipal centers.
50. **Literacy Rate & Out-of-School Children:** The national literacy rate stands at **62.8%** (male 73.4%, female 51.9%), with an estimated **26.2 million out-of-school children**, representing the second largest out-of-school cohort globally.
