## Physics: Reasoning Under a Clock

Physics is the section where preparation shows up fastest in marks, because the question types are narrow: a definition, a unit, an order-of-magnitude estimate, or a single-step calculation. In MDCAT it carries 50 marks, and in CSS/PMS General Science & Ability it forms the quantitative half of the Everyday Science paper.

### What the examiner actually tests

- **Units, measurement & vectors:** SI base and derived units, dimensional analysis, significant figures, error and uncertainty, and vector resolution.
- **Mechanics:** Newton's laws, friction, circular motion and centripetal force, work-energy-power, conservation of momentum and collision types, gravitation and orbital velocity, torque and equilibrium, and simple harmonic motion.
- **Waves & sound:** wave parameters, superposition, resonance, beats, the Doppler effect, stationary waves in pipes and strings, and ultrasound applications.
- **Optics:** reflection and refraction, Snell's law and critical angle, total internal reflection, thin-lens and mirror formulae, magnification, dispersion, interference, diffraction, and polarisation.
- **Electricity & magnetism:** Coulomb's law, Ohm's law and resistor combinations, Kirchhoff's laws, power and energy in circuits, capacitors, magnetic field around conductors, Fleming's rules, electromagnetic induction, transformers, and AC circuits.
- **Thermodynamics:** heat transfer modes, specific and latent heat, the ideal gas equation, the first law and its sign convention, processes (isothermal, adiabatic, isobaric, isochoric), and Carnot efficiency.
- **Modern physics:** photoelectric effect and work function, de Broglie wavelength, atomic spectra and the hydrogen energy levels, radioactivity and half-life decay calculations, nuclear reactions, and basic electronics (diodes, transistors as switches and amplifiers, logic gates).

### How to use these sets

Every calculation carries the formula and the substitution, so practise the arithmetic without a calculator — MDCAT does not allow one. When an explanation checks dimensions or units first, copy that habit: option-elimination by unit is the fastest legitimate shortcut in a physics MCQ.

### Formulas that appear every year

- v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as.
- F = ma, p = mv, KE = ½mv², PE = mgh.
- Ohm's and power: V = IR, P = VI = I²R = V²/R.
- Waves: v = fλ; lenses: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u; mirrors: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u.
- Gas and heat: PV = nRT, Q = mcΔT, Q = mL, η = 1 − T₂/T₁.
- Modern: E = hf, λ = h/mv, N = N₀(½)^(t/t½).
